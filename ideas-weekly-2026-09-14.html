The strongest counter-argument. Valuation is graded C. That is a cross-sectional ranking against the universe, not a level against the company's own history, and C means mid universe. There is no margin of safety in the entry price. If the estimate run stalls, a mid-priced defensive telecom with an adequate rather than strong fundamental profile has nothing underneath it. The counter-case is that rank one should go to a stock that is both confirmed and cheap, and this one is only confirmed.

What would move it up or drop it out. It is at the top, so the only question is what drops it.
