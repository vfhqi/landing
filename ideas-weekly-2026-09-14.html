How the twenty were chosen

Plain-English glossary for the terms used below. Stage 2 is a confirmed advancing price uptrend; Proven Stage 2 means four or more qualifying months in the trailing twelve; Early Stage 2 means a young or partial signal. Stage 1 Transition is a confirmed basing-to-advancing turn bought at a cheap valuation grade. Timeliness is the grade for how well-timed the setup is now. Sell-side estimate momentum is the direction and breadth of analyst revisions to earnings, profits and sales, graded against the whole universe. Valuation grade is a ranking against the universe now, not against the stock's own history, so grade A means among the cheapest tenth of all stocks, not near its own floor. Market paradigm fit, fundamental change and fundamental foundations are three separate fundamental readings, blank for two thirds of the pool. A falling knife is a cheap stock whose estimates are still being cut, which is a value trap rather than a value turn.

The three gates, and what they actually did. Every stock joining a list must pass all three. The timeliness gate did nearly all the work, removing 46 of 348 names that graded C, F or blank. The stage-signal gate removed one name, Atrium Ljungberg, which carries no technical bucket at all and also graded F on timeliness. The sector-weight gate removed nobody: no stock in the pool carries a sector graded F, strong underweight. The strong-underweight sectors had already been screened out before the pool was assembled, so that gate is inert in this pack. 302 of 348 names passed all three.

No carried-over name failed a gate, because there are no carried-over names. This is the first run, so gate_failures_kept is empty. Every one of the eighty listed names passes all three gates on its own merits.

What the ranking weighed. Sector target weight grade, timeliness, the three fundamental readings where present, sell-side estimate momentum, valuation grade, Richard's recorded interest level, and whether a published memo already exists. A blank was never scored as a bad grade. A blank cost confidence, not quality: where two cases looked equally good, the stock whose readings can actually be seen was preferred, and every note below says when that happened.

One correction could not be applied in full, and this matters. The build procedure requires that for a proven name, the earnings, profit and sales revision lines be read individually, because a positive headline can hide a recent cut to the profit lines. Those line items are not in this pack. Only the single headline estimate-momentum grade is available. The best proxy available was used instead: a proven name graded D or F on headline estimate momentum was treated as deteriorating and kept out of the top of the Bench. No Bench name carries a D or F on that reading. Bravida, at Longlist 12, is the one listed proven name flagged deteriorating under that proxy.

A second correction could not be applied at all. The build procedure prefers a shorter proven record, five to seven months rather than twelve, because less of the move has been banked. Record length is not in this pack. The Proven bucket could not be ordered freshest-first. This is the largest single gap between the procedure as written and the ranking as produced, and it affects all nine Proven names on the Bench.
