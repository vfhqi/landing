# Consulting Business Economics

## Definition
Consulting businesses typically have 10-15% EBIT margins and work optimally with 70% utilisation ratios for their FTEs (68-74% range, approximately). Below 68% = margin pressure. Above 74% = burnout risk and quality degradation.

## Why It Matters
Industry-specific benchmark that immediately orients analysis. Knowing the optimal utilisation range prevents mistaking normal cyclical variation for structural change.

## Application
When assessing any consulting/professional services business: check utilisation ratio. If below 68%, ask whether it's cyclical or structural. If above 74%, ask about employee retention and delivery quality.
