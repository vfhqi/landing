# Cable-Industry Capacity-Overhang Timing

## Definition

In specialty cable manufacturing (HV power cables, fibre optic cables, defence-grade cables), capacity additions take **18-36 months** from announcement to commercial operation. This creates a predictable backlog-quality window: capacity announcements made today reach the market in 18-36 months. Cases that depend on tight-supply pricing power (e.g., 20%+ HV margins) should be valued against THIS specific lead time, not generic capacity-cycle assumptions.

## Why It Matters

Different from generic capacity-cycle thinking because the lead time is industry-specific and asymmetric:
- Consumer goods: 3-6 months capacity build
- Specialty cables: **18-36 months**
- Mining / oil / gas: 5-10 years
- Semi fabs: 2-4 years

Specialty cable falls in the "long enough to be predictable, short enough to bite within typical investment horizons" zone. This makes the monitoring discipline ACTIONABLE: scan competitor capacity announcements 18-36 months back to forecast when oversupply will hit.

## Application

When evaluating a cable case with a backlog-quality thesis (e.g., PRY's €17bn HV transmission backlog at 20%+ margins):

1. List all competitor capacity announcements in the last 36 months (for HV: NKT Karlskrona, Hellenic Cables, Furukawa, etc.)
2. Map each announcement to its expected commercial-operation date (announcement + 18-36 months)
3. Identify the YEAR when cumulative capacity additions materially exceed cumulative demand growth
4. Backlog-margin protection: contracts signed BEFORE that year are typically locked-in (fixed-price); contracts signed AFTER face price-pressure
5. APM should explicitly differentiate "first half of backlog at locked margins" vs "last half of backlog at compression risk"

For PRY: NKT + Hellenic capacity reaches commercial operation 2027-29. PRY's €17bn backlog runs to 2028+. Roughly: 2025-26 deliveries at 20%+ margins (locked tight-supply); 2027-28 deliveries at 15-17% (capacity-overhang risk on new orders, partial impact on backlog with price-pass-through clauses).

## Boundary Conditions

- Lead time varies by cable type: HV power 24-36 months; fibre optic 12-18 months; defence-grade 18-24 months
- Capacity announcements can be cancelled or delayed (see post-2008 capacity-build cancellations)
- BACKLOG with price-pass-through clauses partially insulates margin from capacity overhang on new orders only
