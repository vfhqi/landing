# Semi-Cap WFE Early-Mid Recovery Signal — Mid-Cap First

## Definition

In the semiconductor capital equipment cycle, the transition from late-trough to early-mid-recovery is most reliably signalled by a **single quarter** of book-to-bill > 1.3 + order growth > 20% YoY at a **mid-cap supplier** (not at the leaders). Mid-caps like AEIS, COMET, MKSI track the cycle more cleanly because they lack the AI-driven structural lift that distorts large-cap leaders' (ASML, AMAT, LRCX) signals. The mid-cap inflection precedes the headline-leader inflection by 1-2 quarters because mid-caps sit deeper in the supply chain.

## Why It Matters

The signal disambiguates two distinct dynamics that are often conflated:

1. **AI capex structural lift** — sustainable 18-36 months; benefits ASML/AMAT/LRCX disproportionately; not a "cycle" phenomenon
2. **WFE cyclical inflection** — mean-reverting 6-18 months; benefits the WHOLE supply chain including mid-caps; THIS is the trade

Misreading dynamic #1 as #2 leads to extrapolating temporary AI-driven growth as cyclical recovery (over-rates leaders). Misreading #2 as #1 leads to dismissing genuine mid-cap recovery as "second-order AI noise" (under-rates mid-caps). The mid-cap order signal cuts through the noise because mid-caps don't get the AI structural lift directly.

## Application

When evaluating a semi-cap case at IG or Triaging:

1. Pull mid-cap supplier book-to-bill ratios (AEIS, COMET, MKSI, BESI, ASMI, KLAC) for the most recent 2-4 quarters
2. Test for a single-quarter B2B > 1.3 + order growth > 20% YoY
3. If present at mid-cap: cycle inflection confirmed, allocate to mid-cap suppliers (better leverage to the cycle than leaders)
4. If absent at mid-cap but present at leaders: AI capex lift only, NOT a cycle play — sizing implications very different

The signal expects **mid-cap-first** sequencing. If you see leaders inflect WITHOUT mid-caps, the cycle inflection thesis is unconfirmed — reverse causality (AI lift at top, not yet flowing to mid-caps).

## Examples from Track Record

- **COMET (May-26):** Q1-26 orders +22.3% YoY at book-to-bill 1.4 — textbook signal. Triaging memo correctly framed as "cycle inflection confirmed in orders."
- **AEIS (mid-cap reference):** AEIS divergence (+21% revenue FY25 vs COMET +7.3% cc) initially looked like a cohort win, but ESA depth on COTN-CH revealed it was AEIS's data-centre segment lift (AI-adjacent) NOT cycle-share-shift. Distinguishing those was material to the case.
- **Historical precedents (training):** 2017 cycle bottom (mid-caps inflected Q3-16, leaders Q1-17). 2020 cycle bottom (mid-caps inflected Q3-20, leaders Q4-20 / Q1-21). Pattern repeats.

## Boundary Conditions

- Signal sensitivity decays in mid-cycle (B2B 1.3 less informative when cycle is mid-cycle than at trough)
- Single-quarter signals can false-positive — confirmation requires 2 consecutive quarters of B2B > 1.0 minimum
- Geographic concentration matters: a Chinese-customer-concentrated mid-cap (some BESI lines) may signal China-specific dynamics rather than global cycle
