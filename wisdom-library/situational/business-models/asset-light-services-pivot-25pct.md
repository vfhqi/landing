# Asset-Light Services Pivot — 25% Revenue Threshold

## Definition

When a commodity-product manufacturer (low margin, cyclical, capacity-overhang prone) pivots to bundle **services** around the products (installation, integration, monitoring, software), the services revenue carries 2-3× the gross margin of the product business and is structurally less cyclical. The pivot earns a multiple re-rating IF and only IF three tests pass:

1. **Services revenue >25% of total revenue** (below this, services are a rounding error in the multiple framework)
2. **Margin gap demonstrably wider** (services GM ≥1.5× product GM)
3. **Services growth structural, not cyclical-bounce** (3+ quarters of growth >2× product growth)

Without all three: cosmetic re-classification, not a real pivot. With all three: multiple re-rating ~30-50% over 2-3 years from "commodity manufacturer multiple" toward "industrial services multiple."

## Why It Matters

Many commodity-product cases claim a services pivot. Most fail one or more tests. The 25% threshold is the inflection point in the multiple framework — below it, the bull-case rests on the product cycle; above it, the case can lean on services structural growth. The transition is non-linear.

Cosmetic re-classification (just calling old product-related service revenue "subscription") doesn't earn re-rating because (a) the margins aren't actually different, (b) growth correlates with product cycle, (c) sell-side analysts see through it within 1-2 print cycles.

## Application

When evaluating a stated services-pivot case:

1. Pull services revenue % of total over the last 8 quarters
2. Compute services growth vs product growth differential
3. Check segment GM disclosure — is services GM ≥1.5× product GM?
4. Test the "structural, not cyclical-bounce" criterion: do services grow when products contract? (Recession years; mid-cycle product slowdowns)
5. If all three tests pass + revenue % >25% + trajectory rising: bull-case multiple re-rating thesis is ALIVE
6. If any single test fails: thesis is COSMETIC — discount accordingly

## Examples from Track Record

- **HTRO (May-26):** ESA confirmed Data Centre segment +62% organic Q4-25 at 15.3% EBITA margin (vs group 8.4%). DC + Harsh Environment combined ~37% of revenue (passes 25% threshold). Margin gap demonstrably wider. Growth structural, driven by hyperscaler capex (independent of FTTH cycle). All three tests pass — multiple re-rating thesis is alive.
- **Halma (training reference):** Long-running serial-acquirer pivoting toward IoT-services on top of point products. Services revenue ~30%+ of total. Has earned a structural premium multiple over the past decade.
- **Spirax (training reference):** Similar pattern — steam systems products with overlay of monitoring + services. Premium multiple sustained.
