# Pricing-Model Erosion as Cross-Sector AI Signal

## Definition

A measurable transition from per-seat / per-licence pricing toward value-based / outcome-based pricing across multiple software incumbents simultaneously is a leading indicator of AI-driven business-model disruption. Volume can hold or grow; what shifts is the unit economics — from $/seat to $/value-delivered.

## Why It Matters

The shift is happening across European software incumbents during the AI thematic (Dassault, RELX, Sage, Nemetschek all confirmed transitioning between H2 2025 and Q1 2026 per AS Deep Research). It is structural, not cyclical. Standard sell-side models that extrapolate per-seat ARPU trends miss the change because seat counts hold while revenue mechanics evolve underneath.

**Detection significance:** When you see one incumbent disclosing a pricing-model shift, ask whether peers are doing the same. If 3+ incumbents in adjacent verticals are transitioning simultaneously, the shift is sector-wide and reflects the underlying AI capability eroding the per-seat economics, not company-specific strategy.

## Mechanism

1. AI agents reduce the per-user productivity premium that justified per-seat pricing.
2. Customers push back on seat-based renewal economics ("we have fewer humans using the software but the same business value being created").
3. Incumbent must move to value-based pricing to preserve revenue per customer.
4. Unit economics shift; ARPU trajectory de-anchors from headcount; revenue forecasting becomes harder.

## Evidence Base (3-May-26)

- **Dassault:** CEO Pascal Daloz quote *"the model cannot be anymore the same"* + explicit shift from seat-based to value-based pricing (Q4 2025 earnings).
- **RELX:** Engstrom confirmed "shifting away from per-seat pricing in Legal" (12-Feb-26).
- **Sage:** Continuing perpetual → subscription transition with value-tier expansion.
- **Nemetschek:** Subscription/SaaS revenues +54.7% constant currency Q1 26; Bluebeam Max premium tier launched.

## Application Rules

1. **Watch for cross-sector simultaneity.** One incumbent shifting = company strategy. Three+ shifting = thematic signal.
2. **Discount per-seat ARPU forecasts.** Sell-side models that haven't repriced for value-based transition are stale.
3. **Re-rate winners and losers within the cohort.** Incumbents that successfully shift to value-based capture upside (premium tiers, expansion revenue). Incumbents that fail compress.
4. **Cross-check with M&A activity.** Defensive consolidation accelerates when pricing-model erosion is real (Hexagon-Waygate, Nemetschek-Firmus, RELX-LeapSpace pattern).

## Limits / Counter-Evidence

- Pricing-model shifts can be incumbent-driven (proactive monetisation strategy) rather than AI-disruption-driven. Test: does the shift correlate with AI-product launches AND customer pressure? If only the former, it's strategy not signal.
- Long-cycle CAD/PLM customers have 4-5 year switching costs (per AS Q2). Pricing-model erosion in such customers is a slower-burn process; the seat-volume holds longer than ARPU mechanics suggest.
