# Drawdown Expectations & Psychology

## Definition
Studies of 20%+ CAGR stocks: 15% drawdown multiple times per year, 20%+ most years, 50% every decade. 'Hope for/welcome the counter-punch, not annoyance.' Reframe: 'I want a short-term 30% decline to add 50%.'

## Why It Matters
Calibrates emotional expectations. If you expect drawdowns, you can prepare for them. If they surprise you, Chimp mode takes over.

## Application
Before entry: 'If this drops 30-50%, am I doing a Protector (exit) or buying more?' Answer BEFORE the drop happens. If the answer is 'exit', the position may be wrong.

## Examples from Track Record
- Track record: -54.75% max drawdown. 43% monthly loss rate. This is NORMAL for concentrated, conviction-driven strategies.
- 2022 catastrophe: drawdown psychology destroyed confidence for 2+ years. Recovery requires structured rebuilding.
