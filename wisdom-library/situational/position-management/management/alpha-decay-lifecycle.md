# Alpha Decay Lifecycle (18M)

## Definition
3Y+ case required. Normal expected ownership: 18 months. Enter within 0-6 months of trough/turn. Alpha decay apex: ~18 months. First 6 months: grace period — must prove winner by month 4-6, otherwise case is incorrect.

## Why It Matters
Creates the accountability mechanism for holding periods. Positions older than 18 months are past the alpha apex and should be reviewed for realisation or thesis refresh.

## Application
At month 4-6: has the position proven itself? If not, the case was wrong — don't extend the grace period. At month 18: is the alpha decaying? Is there a fresh catalyst for the next 18 months?

## Examples from Track Record
- Baltic Classifieds: alpha decay in second third of ownership.
- Greggs: alpha captured within expected 18M lifecycle.
