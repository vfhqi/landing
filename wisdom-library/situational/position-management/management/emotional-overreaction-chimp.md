# Emotional Overreaction (Chimp Trading)

## Definition
Panic selling disguised as prudent risk management. 'Feels mature to take risk off, but that's chimp brain tricking you.' The decision looks rational but is emotionally driven. Observable signal: the exit/trim feels like relief, not like a calculated reallocation.

## Why It Matters
The most expensive recurring error in the track record. Chimp-driven trims consistently lock in losses on positions that subsequently recover.

## Application
Before any trim or exit: 'Is this decision coming from Observer or Chimp?' If it feels like relief, it's Chimp. Pause. Breathe. Run the analytical framework. If the framework says hold, hold.

## Examples from Track Record
- Instalco: trimmed 4% on inflation concerns, stock up 30% after. 'We overreacted. We were not calm.'
- BFF: Chimp held (fear of loss) when Observer should have exited (ACH breached). Chimp can work in both directions.
