# 30-Day Shot Clock (Disprove-or-Exit)

## Definition
Any emergence of deterioration triggers a 30-day shot clock. Default is reduce to 0%. Position can be re-argued from zero immediately if risk is genuinely disproved. Frame exits as 'reallocating capital', not 'accepting a loss'.

## Why It Matters
The operational expression of exit discipline. Without a clock, deteriorating positions get held indefinitely through rationalisation.

## Application
When any deterioration signal appears: start the clock. Default action at 30 days: exit. To override: provide clear, specific evidence that the deterioration is disproved (not just 'it might be fine').

## Examples from Track Record
- BFF: shot clock should have fired but exit was delayed through analysis-as-avoidance.
- Technical escalation: when technical + fundamental deterioration simultaneous, exit immediately (don't wait 30 days).
- Minervini: 'Sell discipline is what makes buy discipline possible.'
