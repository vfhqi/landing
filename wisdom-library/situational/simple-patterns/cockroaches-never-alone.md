# Cockroaches Never Alone

## Definition
Once you see one cockroach (problem), assume there are more in dark spaces. Problems compound beyond initial expectations. First problem implies second and third. Don't wait for all to emerge — exit on first clear signal.

## Why It Matters
The single most important exit discipline model. Most losses come from holding through cascading problems, not from the first problem itself.

## Application
When any red flag appears (IR weirdness, management quality concern, financial miss): immediately ask 'what else might be wrong that I can't see yet?' Start the 30-day shot clock. Default: exit unless clearly disproved.

## Examples from Track Record
- BFF: IR weirdness → management risk → capital allocation risk → execution risk. Each revealed the next.
- S4: egotism in communication → deeper cultural problems.
- Attendo: whistleblower scandal → deeper political/regulatory fragility.
- Richard's Principle #5: 'Worser, odder, longer, further.'
