# CFO-Refresh as Leading Indicator of Capital-Discipline Reset

## Definition

A new CFO from a cost-discipline reputation house (e.g., LPKF Laser, German industrial Mittelstand, certain UK industrials like Diploma / Bunzl) joining ~6 months **after** a credibility-damaging guidance miss is a strong leading signal that the company is preparing for an explicit guidance reset + scenario-analysis discipline at the next CMD or strategic update. The signal fires 6-9 months before the formal reset event.

## Why It Matters

Most operator-quality assessment focuses on the CEO. But the CFO refresh after a credibility event is often the load-bearing personnel change. CEOs set strategic direction; CFOs determine the discipline with which it's communicated to investors. A cost-discipline-reputation CFO joining post-miss is usually executing a planned reset — the new CMD will be more conservative, more quantitative, and more scenario-analysed than the prior one.

The signal helps APM time the credibility-recovery window. Different from generic "good CFO" assessment because it's a discrete event (named CFO from named house) with a discrete follow-on (CMD 6-9 months later). Trackable, not mood-based.

## Application

When evaluating a turnaround / Corporate Change EPSU/EPT case:

1. Identify the CFO appointment date and prior employer
2. Test prior employer's reputation: "cost-discipline house" = visible margin-recovery track record under prior CFO + Mittelstand-style scenario discipline
3. Confirm the CMD timing relative to CFO start date (typically 6-9 months post-arrival)
4. Pre-CMD: expect quiet defensive guidance (qualitative, no quantitative ranges) — this is the CFO setting the table
5. At CMD: expect explicit scenario analysis, conservative-but-quantitative ranges, and (this is the tell) MULTIPLE pessimistic scenarios alongside the base case
6. Post-CMD: case progression conditional on the next 2 quarters DELIVERING on the new conservative guidance

If the CFO refresh + 6-9 month CMD pattern does NOT show the pessimistic-scenario discipline, the CFO arrival was likely cosmetic (CV-add for new CEO, not a planned discipline reset). Downgrade interpretation accordingly.

## Examples from Track Record

- **COMET (May-26):** CFO Witt (ex-LPKF Laser, 28y cost-discipline reputation) joined Jan-25, 6 months after FY25 guidance miss. CMD Nov-25 explicit scenario-analysis presentation + cut of peak revenue target -16%. Textbook pattern.
- **Getinge under Just-Bomholt (training reference):** Similar CFO refresh pattern preceded the Getinge margin recovery 2022-24 that informs Just-Bomholt's reputation today.

## Boundary Conditions

- The pattern is more reliable in Mittelstand-culture / Nordic / UK industrials than in US / Asia where capital-allocation framing differs
- A CFO refresh WITHOUT a prior credibility event = different signal (often a normal succession, not a discipline reset)
- The signal degrades if the CEO is also new (too much personnel change = chaotic, not disciplined)
