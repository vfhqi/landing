# Operator Quality (Three Dimensions)

## Definition
Three dimensions of a great operator: (1) Right direction — big ambition, growth-first strategy, dynamic for range of environments. (2) Right resources — great CEO measured via action, hiring as top priority, conservative guidance, clear communication. (3) Right record — high RONIC, acts not talks, outlier standards, shareholders first.

## Why It Matters
The lower the quality of the business/sector/value chain, the more essential the great operator. A 'C' rated operator needs 'B' or better on everything else.

## Application
Assess all three dimensions independently. A CEO with great direction but poor resources = execution risk. Great resources but wrong direction = wasted potential. All three aligned = highest conviction.
