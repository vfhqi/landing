# Massive Corporate Change Plan

## Definition
One of the four patterns: big restructuring, strategic pivot, or transformation. Most attractive when combined with Animal CEO and/or demand explosion. The change plan creates a predictable earnings trajectory independent of macro.

## Why It Matters
Corporate change is one of the most forecastable catalysts because it's largely within management control. Unlike demand (external), the company controls the timeline.

## Application
Assess: is the change plan clearly defined? Is the CEO credible on execution? Is the timeline realistic? Are there verifiable milestones? If yes to all four, this is a high-conviction setup.

## Examples from Track Record
- Pattern 2 of four. Most attractive scenario: company coming out of large investment phase.
- Hilton, Greencore: transformation plays with clear cost-out and efficiency drivers.
