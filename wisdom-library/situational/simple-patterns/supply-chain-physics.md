# Supply Chain Physics (Embedded Positioning)

## Definition
Companies supplying into physically tight supply chains are attractive. Demand EPSU (embedded supply chain utility) rather than demand transformation. Tight supply chains create sustainable positioning and pricing power.

## Why It Matters
Identifies a specific type of competitive advantage that is structural and hard to replicate. Physical embedding in a supply chain is a moat that grows over time.

## Application
When assessing competitive positioning: is the company physically embedded in its customers' supply chain? How costly would switching be? Does the company's positioning get stronger over time?

## Examples from Track Record
- Diploma: supply chain positioning without glitz. Physical embedding creates value chain lock-in.
- Cable companies (NKT, NEX, PRY): supply chain tightness creates structural demand.
