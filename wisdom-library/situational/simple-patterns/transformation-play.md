# Transformation Play (Q-Turn)

## Definition
Company emerging from large investment phase — multi-year margin/EPS depression from doing the right thing, then revenue growth accelerates, margins expand as one-off costs fall away, operating leverage materialises. The 'Q-turn' setup: quality company turning from investment phase to harvest phase.

## Why It Matters
The most attractive single scenario in Richard's framework. The confluence of quality + catalyst + margin expansion creates powerful multi-year returns.

## Application
Look for: company has been investing heavily (depressed margins), revenue base is growing, the investment cycle is ending, consensus is still anchored to depressed margins. The Q-turn is about to happen.

## Examples from Track Record
- 'Any change on any of these fronts — the more the better — is a high, high priority for workflow prioritisation.'
- Internal changes checklist maps every dimension of potential positive change.
