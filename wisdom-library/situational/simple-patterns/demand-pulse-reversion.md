# Demand Pulse Reversion

## Definition
Most demand pulses totally reverse to trend. Assume 100% cyclical/temporary unless proven structural, even if the pulse persists for multiple years. The lesson: don't lazily extrapolate cyclical highs.

## Why It Matters
Prevents the most common analyst error: confusing cyclical demand with structural growth. The emotional temptation to call a cycle 'structural' is enormous when you own the stock.

## Application
For every growth thesis: is this demand structural or cyclical? What is the base rate for demand pulses in this industry reverting? Demand must be proven structural with hard evidence, not assumed.

## Examples from Track Record
- Avanza: lazy extrapolation of cyclical high trading activity.
- Hemnet: lazy extrapolation of earnings growth rate.
- COVID stocks: most demand pulses totally reversed to trend.
