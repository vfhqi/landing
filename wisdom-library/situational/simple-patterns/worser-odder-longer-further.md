# Worser, Odder, Longer, Further

## Definition
Things can get worse than expected, in stranger ways, for longer, and go further than imagined. The human tendency is to underestimate the depth and duration of deterioration.

## Why It Matters
Calibrates expectations for negative scenarios. When something is going wrong, your first estimate of 'how bad' is almost certainly too optimistic.

## Application
When deterioration appears: multiply your worst-case estimate by 1.5-2x on duration and severity. If the position is still acceptable at that level, hold. If not, exit now.

- Attendo: took 2-3 years for CfC and SP to turn. 'Trends normally trend.'
- BFF: cockroach cascade went worser, odder, longer than initial assessment.
