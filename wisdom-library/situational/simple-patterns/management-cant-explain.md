# Management That Can't Explain

## Definition
Management unable to explain themselves clearly won't give conviction to invest, no matter how large the wave they're riding. Clear communication is a proxy for clear thinking.

## Why It Matters
Communication quality is an observable, low-cost signal of management quality. It's testable in a single IR call.

## Application
After any management interaction: could they clearly explain their strategy, their drivers, their financial trajectory? If not, downgrade operator quality regardless of other signals.

## Examples from Track Record
- Alfen: management couldn't explain, no conviction despite large market wave.
- Avanza lesson: CEO meeting was vague because Richard was vague on numbers. IR calls must be financial-centric.
- Positive example: Fevertree — financial-centric and clear.
