# Quality Compounder Pattern

## Definition
High quality + recurring revenue + compounding value. The most reliable pattern for long-term returns. Key signals: stable/expanding margins, high customer retention, pricing power demonstrated in market, management with long tenure.

## Why It Matters
The default target profile. When in doubt about what to own, bias toward quality compounders.

## Application
At IG: does this fit the quality compounder profile? If yes, fast-track through Triaging. If the business is genuinely high quality AND compounding, the bar for entry is lower because the holding period risk is lower.

## Examples from Track Record
- Avanza, Rightmove: canonical quality compounders.
- Risk: overvaluation after extended compounding. Top-slice when multiple stretches.
- Archetype #1 from stock-archetypes library.
