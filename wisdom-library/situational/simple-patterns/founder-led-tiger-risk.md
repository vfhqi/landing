# Founder-Led / Owner-Operator Tiger Risk

## Definition
Great entrepreneurs/founder-led businesses give call option on positive unknown unknowns. BUT they're not your friends — misaligned interests possible. Bidders often pay them differently. 'You're riding a tiger.' Large insider ownership ≠ alignment of interests.

## Why It Matters
The AND-not-OR of founder-led investing: great optionality AND governance risk simultaneously. The positive optionality is real but so is the minority shareholder risk.

## Application
When assessing founder-led businesses: separate the operational quality (usually excellent) from the governance risk (often underappreciated). Check: are controlling shareholders' interests aligned with minorities? Would a bid structure benefit them differently?

## Examples from Track Record
- MasMovil: taken out at 2-3x but controlling shareholders captured disproportionate value.
- SMCP: levered controlling shareholders don't have alignment with minorities.
- Nilfisk: be careful listening to one person — event-driven thesis carries concentration risk in operator quality.
