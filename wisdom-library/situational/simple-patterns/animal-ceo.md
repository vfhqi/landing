# Animal CEO (Slootman Archetype)

## Definition
Relentless, commercial, ambitious CEO who attacks and widens the moat. Holistic/congruent culture. Not defensive — creating forward momentum. 'I love Slootman operators with big ambition with holistic/congruent cultures. I detest Meh-ness.'

## Why It Matters
One of the four patterns that produce outlier returns. Animal CEO is the strongest single signal because great operators create optionality that models can't capture — call option on positive unknown unknowns.

## Application
At IG/Triaging: does the CEO fit the Animal archetype? Check: commercial intensity, ambition level, hiring quality, communication clarity, culture congruence. If CEO is 'meh', downgrade the thesis regardless of other qualities.

## Examples from Track Record
- Ryanair: Animal CEO + demand dynamics + SP decline = confluence.
- Alfen: management that can't explain themselves won't give conviction, no matter how large the wave.
- S4: egotism in communication = red flag. Cut.
- Cellnex: great management gives call option on positive unknown unknowns.
