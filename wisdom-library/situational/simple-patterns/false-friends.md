# False Friends

## Definition
Setups that look good but have structural flaws. Defining characteristic: zero clarity of the transmission mechanism from company actions to EPS. If you cannot clearly explain how strategy translates into financial outputs, it's a false friend.

## Why It Matters
False friends are more dangerous than obvious 'no' stocks because they consume time and capital before revealing themselves. The ACH framework exists specifically to catch them.

## Application
At ESA stage: can you clearly trace the transmission mechanism from strategy → operational execution → qualitative change → KFM → FSO → EPS → SP? If any link is unclear, flag as potential false friend.

## Examples from Track Record
- HBX: no clarity of tracking inputs to EPS.
- Adevinta: no 'soul'.
- S4: egotism in communication.
- Nexi: deep value but defensive strategy only, no moat-widening.
- 'Pret sandwich risk': nothing individually inoffensive but the collection is fatal.
