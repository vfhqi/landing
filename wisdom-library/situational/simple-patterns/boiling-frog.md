# Boiling Frog (Paper Cuts Compound)

## Definition
Losses came from accumulated small breaks, not one big event. When many small things go wrong, they usually compound. The frog doesn't notice the water heating until it's too late.

## Why It Matters
Counteracts normalcy bias — the tendency to treat each small negative as isolated noise rather than part of a pattern.

## Application
Track the number of 'small' negatives over time. If you're on the third minor concern in three months, it's not noise — it's a pattern. Escalate to disprove-or-exit.

## Examples from Track Record
- Burford: paper cuts compounding across foundations.
- BFF: each new problem seemed manageable in isolation but the cascade was fatal.
- Complex systems: systems appear stable even while losing resilience. Collapse is sudden.
