# Pret Sandwich Risk

## Definition
Nothing is individually inoffensive but the collection is fatal. Like a mediocre Pret sandwich — each ingredient is acceptable, the whole is disappointing. Applied to stocks: multiple minor concerns that individually seem manageable but collectively destroy the thesis.

## Why It Matters
Catches the gradual accumulation of minor negatives that don't individually trigger exit criteria but collectively make the position uninvestable.

## Application
Periodically list ALL concerns about a position, even minor ones. If the list has 5+ items, even if each is minor, treat the collection as a serious signal. Run ACH.

## Examples from Track Record
- Sdiptech: Pret sandwich risk. Unclear growth transmission mechanism to EPS.
- Burford: '10s of tiny cuts from lots of degradation across foundations.'
