# Tennis Balls vs Eggs

## Definition
Tennis balls = solid operating momentum, will rebound to trend. Safe to hold through noise. Eggs = negative momentum, structural deterioration. High risk — exit first, re-attack only after troughing evidence.

## Why It Matters
Quick classification for position management under pressure. When a stock drops 15%, the first question is: tennis ball or egg?

## Application
Classify every live position. For tennis balls: hold through pullbacks, add on strength. For eggs: exit immediately, monitor for troughing, re-attack only with clear evidence of recovery.

## Examples from Track Record
- Minervini: 'The strongest performers will rebound the most — tennis balls versus eggs.'
- Instalco: treated as egg (trimmed) but was a tennis ball (up 30% after trim). Chimp brain misclassified.
