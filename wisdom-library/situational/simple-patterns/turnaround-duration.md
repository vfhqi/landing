# Turnarounds Take Longer Than You Think

## Definition
Turnarounds take much longer and are harder than initially thought. The base rate for successful turnarounds is ~30%. Even successful ones take 2-3 years minimum.

## Why It Matters
Calibrates expectations for turnaround positions. If you're expecting a 12-month turnaround, double or triple the timeline.

## Application
For turnaround positions: add 50-100% to your expected timeline. Size accordingly (smaller, because duration risk = capital trapped). Set clear milestones at 6M intervals. If milestones missed, treat as invalidation, not 'it needs more time.'

## Examples from Track Record
- Hostelworld: 800lb gorilla situation + turnaround took much longer/harder.
- Attendo: took 2-3 years for CfC and SP to turn. But then 'can be great rebound setup because of multi-year upgrade path.'
