# Corporate Change EPSU/EPT — Operator Validation Horizon

## Definition

Per the "Animal CEO" archetype + "Massive Corporate Change Plan" framework, a new CEO's classification (Animal vs capable custodian) cannot be reliably validated in less than **12-18 months** of capital-allocation track record. Specifically, three execution dimensions have different validation horizons:

| Execution dimension | Validation horizon |
|---|---|
| **Cost execution** | 6 months — visible via OpEx + headcount metrics |
| **Revenue execution** | 9 months — orders + pipeline |
| **Capital allocation discipline** (M&A, buybacks, R&D) | 12-18 months minimum — allocation decisions take that long to manifest in returns |

A premature operator-quality upgrade based on early cost-execution evidence is a common bull-case error.

## Why It Matters

Corporate Change EPSU/EPT cases often hinge on whether the new CEO is "Animal" (high-conviction-required for fullsize position) or "capable custodian" (B-grade execution but C-grade outcome). The verdict has 2× conviction-grade implications.

Most cost-execution evidence visible in the first 6 months of a new CEO is genuine but incomplete. Cost cuts are the easiest first move (the levers are obvious). Revenue execution requires sales-cycle time. Capital allocation requires deal flow + integration time + return manifestation.

Until the 12-18 month capital-allocation track record exists, the operator-quality verdict is INFERENTIAL (based on prior employer + early signals). Inferential = honest C-grade rating; promoting to B requires actual evidence.

## Application

When evaluating a Corporate Change EPSU/EPT case with a new CEO:

1. Calculate months-since-CEO-arrival
2. Assess what evidence is mature enough to use:
   - 0-6 months: rely on prior employer reputation + announced strategic direction (inferential — cap rating at C)
   - 6-9 months: cost execution evidence comes in (B grade possible if cost cuts deliver)
   - 9-12 months: revenue execution evidence (orders + pipeline)
   - 12-18 months: capital allocation track record begins to manifest
   - 18+ months: full operator-quality verdict possible
3. Map the case-progression decision to the validation horizon:
   - At <12 months: case can be at C-conviction maximum, not B
   - At 12-18 months: B-conviction possible if all three execution dimensions track
   - At 18+ months: A-conviction possible if Animal-CEO criteria met (per archetype framework)

For EKTA (Just-Bomholt 8 months in role at session date): operator-quality verdict deferred. Cost execution credible (visible) but capital allocation pending — earliest reliable verdict ~Mar-27.

## Boundary Conditions

- The horizon assumes a "transformational" change scope (org delayering, M&A engine activation, capital allocation restart). For incremental change scope (run-rate optimisation), shorter validation horizons may suffice
- Internally-promoted CEOs have a faster validation path because their prior in-role evidence is already integrated (Just-Bomholt was internal X-ray business head before promotion — partial credit)
- A CEO from a track-record-with-explicit-capital-allocation-evidence (e.g., second-time turnaround CEOs) may have a shorter required horizon — but APM should still avoid premature upgrades on the new role
