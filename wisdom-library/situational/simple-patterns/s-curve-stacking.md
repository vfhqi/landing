# S-Curve Stacking & Co-Evolution

## Definition
Sustained innovation is a series of overlapping S-curves with increasing adoption rates. 'Co-evolution of disruption technologies': adjacent technologies simultaneously enable each other (GPUs → AI → drug discovery). 'Deliverability doesn't matter for first 12 months' — changing expectations, not proof.

## Why It Matters
Identifies structural themes that command scarcity premium. The opportunity is finding listed companies on the inflection point of a stacking S-curve.

## Application
For thematic positions: is the S-curve stacking (multiple waves) or single (one wave, likely to revert)? Is there co-evolution with other technologies? If single wave, treat as demand pulse (likely to revert).

## Examples from Track Record
- AI + datacentres, gold, oil: simple, obvious mega trends to ride. Thematic and style agnosticism creates knowledge optionality.
- Critical caveat: 'Most demand pulses totally reverse to trend.' Only hold for structural themes.
