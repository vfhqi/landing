# Scale Economies Shared

## Definition
A chain pattern: company achieves economies of scale, then shares the cost savings with customers through lower prices, which attracts more customers, which creates more scale. The cycle is self-reinforcing. Different from hoarding scale benefits (which invites competition).

## Why It Matters
Identifies the most durable form of scale advantage. Companies that share scale benefits build loyalty and create a barrier that's nearly impossible to replicate because competitors would need to match both scale AND generosity.

## Application
Look for: margins stable or slowly expanding despite aggressive pricing. Customer base growing because the value proposition improves with scale. Competitors struggling because they can't match the economics.

## Examples from Track Record
- Costco: canonical example of scale economies shared.
- Metacognition checklist: 'Scale economies shared' as a specific moat type distinct from pure scale.
