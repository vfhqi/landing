# Cone of Outcomes (Time Horizon)

## Definition
The range of plausible outcomes expands the further forward you forecast. At 3Y, the range is very wide. Value is highest in the 18-24 month window: enough conviction to size meaningfully, low enough uncertainty for edges.

## Why It Matters
Prevents two errors: (1) trying to be right on 5-10Y outcomes (impossible), (2) sizing based on distant forecasts (unreliable). Anchor sizing to 18M+4Y horizons.

## Application
Size positions to be right on 18-24 month outcomes. Let long-term optionality ride, but don't let it drive sizing. If the 18M case doesn't work independently of the 4Y case, the position is too speculative.

## Examples from Track Record
- Investment lifecycle: 3Y+ case required, normal ownership 18 months, enter within 0-6 months of trough/turn, alpha decay apex ~18 months.
