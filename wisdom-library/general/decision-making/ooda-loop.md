# OODA Loop (Boyd)

## Definition
Observe → Orient → Decide → Act. Speed of the OODA loop is the competitive advantage. The investor who can observe, orient, decide, and act faster than consensus wins.

## Why It Matters
Speed of decision-cycle is a primary alpha source. Not speed for speed's sake — speed of GOOD decisions. The OODA loop ensures decisions are grounded (Orient) not reactive.

## Application
Apply to: earnings reactions (observe print → orient vs expectations → decide thesis intact? → act), macro shifts, deterioration signals.

## Examples from Track Record
- Burford lesson: 'SP OODA loop faster than yours = red line.' When the market's OODA loop is faster than your analytical cycle, the position is structurally disadvantaged.
- Eight meta winning behaviours: 'Speed of OODA loop — observe, orient, decide, act faster than consensus.'
