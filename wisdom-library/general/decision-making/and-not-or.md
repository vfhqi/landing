# AND Not OR (2x2 Reframing)

## Definition
Most people think decisions are on a one-dimensional spectrum (OR). They are not. They are a 2x2 matrix (AND). Example: 'positive vs negative energy' AND 'looking for optionality vs risk' are independent axes. You can positively look for risks — pre-morteming, then being positive about finding problems ex ante. Any difficult problem has a 2x2 reframing.

## Why It Matters
Breaks false dichotomies that constrain thinking. When someone presents an either/or choice, there's almost always a both/and solution on a higher dimension.

## Application
When facing any difficult trade-off: draw the 2x2. What are the two independent dimensions? Where does the optimal solution sit? The 'AND' quadrant is usually the one nobody is exploring.

## Examples from Track Record
- Pre-mortem example: analysts think being negative = risk management. Wrong. You can be positive energy AND looking for risks. The AND quadrant is pre-morteming with enthusiasm.
- Portfolio construction: not 'growth OR value' but 'quality AND momentum' — independent axes.
