# Strong Views, Weakly Held

## Definition
Always have a conclusion, even if revisable. The worst state is ambiguity — it prevents action and wastes energy. Form the best judgement with current information, act on it, update when new evidence arrives.

## Why It Matters
Prevents both paralysis (no view) and rigidity (won't change view). The combination of decisiveness and flexibility is the target.

## Application
Every Watson output ends with a judgement. Flag confidence level and key assumptions, but never end with 'it depends' without a directional view. Be willing to revise when evidence warrants.

## Examples from Track Record
- Applied across all IAJA outputs. The directional view is mandatory.
- Pattern at Triaging: 'Strong views, weakly held' — sensible hypotheses that need testing with more evidence.
