# Inversion (Jacobi / Popper)

## Definition
'Invert. Always invert.' Don't just look for what you want — test for the disproving evidence, the invalidating hypotheses. Combined with Popper's scientific method: a theory is only valid if it can be falsified. Seek falsification actively.

## Why It Matters
The most reliable path to truth is eliminating what's false. Confirmation bias is the default; inversion is the antidote. Applied to investing: don't prove the case, disprove the bear case.

## Application
Before any judgement: invert. What would make this wrong? What evidence would invalidate this thesis? If you can't articulate the invalidation criteria, the thesis isn't testable.
