# Complex Systems Thinking

## Definition
Complex systems appear stable even while losing resilience. Underlying stability is hard to assess. Collapse often appears sudden and surprising. Three degradation indicators: slower recovery rates, rising autocorrelation, rising variance.

## Why It Matters
Directly applicable to both portfolio positions and market regimes. By the time negatives manifest visibly, there are many problems under the surface. By the time you emotionally recognise deterioration, you are likely very late.

## Application
Track the three degradation indicators for every live position. If any two are present, escalate to disprove-or-exit framework.

## Examples from Track Record
- Applied to positions: 'By the time you mentally/emotionally recognise this in this specific situation, I am likely very late.'
- Applied to self-management: performance is a complex system — many interacting variables, cannot optimise in isolation.
