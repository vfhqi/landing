# Bayesian Updating

## Definition
Update beliefs incrementally as new evidence arrives. Prior probability × likelihood of evidence given hypothesis = posterior probability. The key is updating proportionally to the strength of evidence, not overreacting to noise or underreacting to signals.

## Why It Matters
Prevents two failure modes: (1) anchoring on original thesis despite contradicting evidence, (2) overreacting to single data points. Proper updating is the foundation of disciplined position management.

## Application
Stay 3 steps ahead, not 1. Update mental model intra-quarter as data arrives. Rebalance sizing as conviction/framework confidence change.
