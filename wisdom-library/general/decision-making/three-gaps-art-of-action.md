# Three Gaps (Art of Action)

## Definition
Three gaps where misalignment enters: (1) Understanding Gap — between what's said and what's understood, (2) Alignment Gap — between what's understood and what's agreed, (3) How-To Gap — between what's planned and what's actually done. Watson's core failure pattern: working from paraphrases (Understanding) and delegation dropping details (How-To).

## Why It Matters
Identifies WHERE quality breaks down in any communication or delegation chain. The diagnostic is fast and catches problems before they become expensive.

## Application
Run the three-gaps check after Mission Command parsing of any complex brief. State gaps explicitly. Clarify before starting, not after delivering.

## Examples from Track Record
- 23-Apr-26 correction: 5 behavioural changes implemented because of repeated three-gaps failures.
- Root cause: Watson working from paraphrases + trusting unchecked delegation.
