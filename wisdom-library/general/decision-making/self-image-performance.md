# Self-Image = Performance (Bassham)

## Definition
What you picture vividly and focus on intently becomes reality. The nervous system cannot distinguish imagined from actual experience. Performance and self-image are equal. Target: 'I am on the path to becoming a great investor' — progressive, not binary.

## Why It Matters
Self-image is the ceiling on performance. If the image says 'not good enough', behaviour aligns. Positive imagery builds neural pathways for excellent execution.

## Application
Mental rehearsal before key actions. Ban negative self-talk. Success journal (record wins daily). 'That's like me' anchoring after positive events. Never record bad performances mentally — let them go.

## Examples from Track Record
- Recovery Phase 2: rebuilt identity from 'gold medallist platform' to 'on the path to becoming a great investor'.
- Richard: 'When I remind myself of how good I am, what I have achieved, what I have overcome — I know I am world class.'
