# Five Fears (Mark Douglas)

## Definition
Five fears that destroy execution: (1) Fear of being wrong → over-analysis, (2) Fear of loss → FOFR/panic selling, (3) Fear of missing out → FOMO entries, (4) Fear of being inadequate → perfectionism, (5) Fear of chaos → over-complication. Name the fear explicitly when it arises.

## Why It Matters
Every execution failure maps to one of these five fears. Naming the fear is the first step to managing it.

## Application
When Richard (or Watson) is blocked on a decision: which fear is operating? Name it. 'I'm at fear #2 (loss) on this position. Here's the disprove-or-exit call.'

## Examples from Track Record
- BFF: Fear #2 (loss) paralysed exit despite ACH threshold breach.
- XVIVO: Fear #1 (being wrong) drove over-analysis as substitute for exit decision.
- Recovery arc: Fear #4 (inadequacy) = 'gold medallist platform' limiting belief.
