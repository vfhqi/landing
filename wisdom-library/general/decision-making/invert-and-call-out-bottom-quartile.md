# Invert and Call Out the Bottom Quartile (D/F Markers)

## Definition
Bottom-quartile findings (D and F grades on the A-F bell curve) get a **mandatory `❌` prefix and explicit grade**. F findings get `❌❌`. Do not soften, balance, or bury them. The fastest path to good investing is eliminating bad businesses — invert the question, identify the disqualifiers first, and make them visually loud.

This is Munger's "All I want to know is where I'm going to die so I'll never go there" applied to the communication layer, not just the analytical layer.

## Why It Matters
Watson's tendency (and analyst tendency generally) is to be diplomatic — present a "balanced view" of a deteriorating fact. That tendency is unhelpful for a concentrated long-only investor whose bottleneck is judgement bandwidth, not data. Bad must look bad. Bottom quartile must read as bottom quartile.

It is also empirically easier to identify mediocrity than to identify excellence — the data on D/F findings is usually clearer than the data on A findings. So make the easy identification visible and let it carry the load.

## Application
- Every D or F finding gets a `❌` prefix at the bullet level
- F findings get `❌❌ F:` — these are flagging for likely disqualification
- Do not pad a D/F finding with mitigants in the same bullet — put mitigants in a sub-bullet so the headline reads cleanly
- In Key Findings or BLUF: D and F findings come BEFORE the As and Bs (invert the ordering)
- Cultural rule: be less polite. "Margin pressure persists" → "**[D] Margin pressure persists** — third consecutive quarter; sector peers expanding"
- When the case as a whole sits in the bottom quartile, say so loudly: "**[F] The case as a whole sits in the bottom quartile** (5 of 7 IC pillars rated D or F)"

This is operationalised as **Communication Principle #3** in `memory/skills/communication-principles/SKILL.md`.

## Examples from Track Record
- **BFF (Feb-26):** "Cockroaches never alone" — the single first D-grade finding (sales decline) was treated as a one-off. Had it been marked `❌ [D]` clearly, the implied "expect more cockroaches" call-to-action would have been louder. Instead, additional D and F findings accumulated quietly. Exit was 3-6 months too late.
- **XVIVO (track record):** Multiple D-grade findings on transmission-mechanism clarity were softened in writeups as "areas for further work." Had they been `❌ [D]` they would have triggered the IC#2 false-friend test earlier.
- **CARLB (Apr-26 inversion test):** Forced inversion exercise revealed that 2 of 13 pillars were genuinely D-grade despite the overall positive earnings reaction. The `❌` markers in the resulting memo made the case-fragility analysis sharp rather than smoothed.

## Boundary Conditions
- This is about **clarity of communication**, not pessimism. A D/F finding is not "bad" until put in the context of the case — but the marker IS mandatory so the context can be applied.
- Excessive ❌ markers in a memo (e.g. >5) usually indicate the case is not investable. The visual density is itself a signal.
- Combine with Principle #4 (Outlier flagging): a `🚩 RARE: ❌ F` is a particularly valuable finding — a clear, unusual disqualifier.
