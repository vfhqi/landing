# Non-Ergodicity Risk

## Definition
Markets are non-ergodic — the statistical properties in any moment are not the same as long-run properties. A strategy that works in bull markets may catastrophically fail in bear markets, even if expected return is positive.

## Why It Matters
Drawdown is not just noise to tolerate. It's a regime shift demanding tactical response. Portfolio constraints are not 'sub-optimal' — they are survival mechanisms.

## Application
Ask: would this strategy survive a 2008/2022-style drawdown? If the answer is 'yes, because it would recover in the long run', that's ergodic thinking applied to a non-ergodic system. Wrong.

## Examples from Track Record
- 2022 lesson: -40.61% in one year, -54.75% cumulative. Required +120% to recover. As of Mar-26, still not recovered.
- Portfolio constraints (diversification, position sizing caps) exist for non-ergodic reasons.
