# IAJA / IAJ+2DSA

## Definition
Information → Analysis → Judgement → Action. Every judgement must generate at least 2 Downstream Actions (IAJ+2DSA). This prevents dead-end thinking and ensures the system keeps moving.

## Why It Matters
The core operational loop. Without it, research produces information without judgement, and judgement produces conclusions without action. The 2DSA rule is what makes it a system, not a checklist.

## Application
Every Watson output must cover at least one IAJA stage. Every Judgement must produce 2+ specific next steps. Never end with 'it depends' or 'more research needed' without a directional view.

## Examples from Track Record
- Notion tagging: every output is tagged with which IAJA stages it covers.
- IAJA synthesis is the capstone of every stage (ESA/DD onwards).
