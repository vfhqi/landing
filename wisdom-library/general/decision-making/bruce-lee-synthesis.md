# Bruce Lee Synthesis

## Definition
'Take what is good, discard what is bad, add your own.' Find loads of great ideas, then take the best bits (not all — discard some), then combine with your own experience and context.

## Why It Matters
Prevents two failure modes: (1) adopting a framework wholesale without adaptation, (2) rejecting useful frameworks because parts don't fit. The skill is in the curation and integration.

## Application
When encountering any new framework, model, or advice: what's good here? What doesn't fit our system? What can we uniquely add from our experience? Never adopt or reject wholesale.

## Examples from Track Record
- Applied to Minervini: took SEPA entry discipline, adapted stop-loss framework to European context, discarded aspects that don't fit concentrated style.
- Applied to Wisdom Library itself: Munger's latticework structure + Bevelin's three layers + Richard's situation-specific overlay.
