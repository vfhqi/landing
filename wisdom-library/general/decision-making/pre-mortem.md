# Pre-Mortem

## Definition
Imagine the investment has failed. Work backwards: what went wrong? The pre-mortem surfaces risks that optimism bias would otherwise hide. Always use REFV02_RB template.

## Why It Matters
The single most effective debiasing technique for investment decisions. Forces confrontation with the bear case before capital is committed.

## Application
Run pre-mortem at ESA stage (mandatory). Ask: 'It's 18 months from now and this position has lost 30%. What happened?' Identify the 3-5 most plausible failure modes.

## Examples from Track Record
- Pre-mortem is mandatory at ESA and DD stages.
- AND-not-OR application: pre-mortem with positive energy (finding problems is good, not negative).
