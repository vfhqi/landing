# Base Rates vs Case Rates

## Definition
Base rate = how often does this type of event typically occur? Case rate = what does the specific evidence for this case suggest? Most people anchor on the case rate and ignore the base rate. Good forecasters start with the base rate and adjust.

## Why It Matters
Prevents overconfidence in specific narratives. The base rate of corporate turnarounds succeeding is ~30%. Even a great narrative needs to overcome that prior.

## Application
For every thesis: what is the base rate for this type of situation? Turnarounds? M&A synergy delivery? Demand acceleration persistence? Start there, then adjust based on case-specific evidence.

## Examples from Track Record
- Hostelworld: turnaround took much longer than thought. Base rate for turnaround duration was ignored.
- Superforecasting principles: observable base rates, not analytical constructs.
