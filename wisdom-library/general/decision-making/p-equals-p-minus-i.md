# P = p - i (Performance Equation)

## Definition
Performance = Potential minus Interference. Richard's potential (p) is very high (proven by 2015-2021 +24% CAGR, COVID +34%). The constraint is interference (i): ostriching, complexity creep, delayed exits, creativity vs execution tension.

## Why It Matters
Shifts the coaching focus from 'get better' to 'remove what's blocking you'. The interference is identifiable, trainable, removable.

## Application
Three mental switches: ON (engagement), CONTROL (recognise and reset unhelpful thoughts — the most important), OFF (deliberate recovery). Watson's primary role is helping Richard manage the CONTROL switch.

## Examples from Track Record
- Concordance assessment: analytical depth is a strength but misaligned with monitoring demands. 'Built for the hunt, not the maintenance.'
- Solution: make monitoring visual and pattern-based (dashboards), brief and decisional (10 min), an on-ramp to the hunt.
