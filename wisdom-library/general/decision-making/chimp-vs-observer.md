# Chimp vs Observer (Peters)

## Definition
Two operating modes: Chimp (reactive, emotional, threat-focused) and Observer (thoughtful, process-oriented, solution-focused). Chimp decisions are rarely optimal. The skill: recognise the trigger, pause, breathe, move to Observer, then act.

## Why It Matters
Chimp mode is not failure — it's biology. The skill is not preventing Chimp mode (impossible) but recognising it quickly and transitioning to Observer before acting.

## Application
Pre-trigger identification: What situations flip you into Chimp? Loss of control, time pressure, disagreement, market dislocations. Pre-commitment: 'When triggered, I pause before acting. Breathe. Reorient.'

## Examples from Track Record
- Ostriching: Chimp avoidance of threatening information. 'Avoided looking at prices. Still ostriching.'
- Instalco: 'Feels mature to take risk off, but that's chimp brain tricking you.'
- Morning routine as 'Chimp armour' — daily activation of Observer before first trigger.
