# Stage-Scope Flex Doctrine

## Definition

When working through the 6-stage research funnel (IG → Triaging → ESA → DD → Capital), there are legitimate situations where APM consults research from a deeper stage while authoring at a shallower stage. The doctrine: scope-flex is permissible when explicitly authorised, must be flagged in QC footer and at memo header, and must NOT become default. Scope-flex used silently is a doctrine breach; scope-flex used deliberately is a controlled deviation.

Two named patterns from this session:
- **"Triaging+":** Triaging decision logic + ESA-stage research consulted where it materially sharpens judgement
- **"Option B":** ESA-depth analysis on existing Triaging+ corpus, BEFORE ESA RESEARCHER workstreams complete

## Why It Matters

The 6-stage funnel exists to enforce escalating depth, evidence, and conviction-cost discipline. Each stage gate is a deliberate choke point: research depth grows; APM analytical effort grows; Richard's review depth grows; capital exposure grows. If shallow stages are allowed to absorb deep-stage research at will, three risks emerge:

1. **Triaging memos systematically look weaker than strict-Triaging would.** More research surfaces more bear case. A name evaluated under strict-Triaging would progress; the same name under Triaging+ might park because the deeper research surfaces invalidations the IG/Triaging-stage queries weren't designed to find. This creates inconsistency across the funnel.

2. **Deep-stage memos lose their analytical earn-rate when shallow stages consume the research first.** If the ESA Short-Sellers report is fully consumed at Triaging, the ESA memo has nothing new to say. ESA memos become re-statements rather than new analysis.

3. **The stage transitions stop being decision points.** STAGE PROGRESSION SOP §51 ("the transition decision is made at Step 4, NEVER at Step 2 unilaterally") only works if shallow stages are limited to shallow research. Otherwise APM is effectively progressing the case unilaterally by reading deeper than the stage allows.

The doctrine: scope-flex is a tool, not a default. Used deliberately and flagged, it captures upside (richer judgement, time-efficient when research already exists) without sacrificing the stage-discipline that makes the funnel work.

## Application

**When scope-flex is permissible:**

1. **Authorised by Richard explicitly.** "Run Triaging+" or "do Option B" or equivalent.
2. **Research already exists for the deeper stage.** This is the typical trigger: a prior session has run ESA queries, and the next Triaging needs to leverage them rather than ignore them.
3. **The flex is doctrine-deviating but value-additive.** A capability test, time-pressured client deliverable, or batch-efficiency consideration justifies the deviation.

**When scope-flex must be flagged:**

- Memo header (top of file) — "**Scope: Triaging+** (full ESA-stage research consulted; Triaging decision logic) — flagged in QC footer"
- QC footer — explicit "SCOPE NOTE" line documenting which stage's research was consulted under what stage's decision logic
- STAGE PROGRESSION handoff package — flag prominently so Richard's Step 4 decision is informed
- Calibration log — record the deviation as a calibration event

**When scope-flex must NOT happen silently:**

- Routine Triaging without authorisation — strict-Triaging is the default
- ESA memo authored on existing Triaging+ corpus without explicit Option B authorisation — this is a doctrine breach, not a "richer analysis" win
- Any flex that affects a case-level rating without flagging — undermines the calibration log and the stage-progression discipline

**Recurring review:**

- Calibration log captures every scope-flex event
- Quarterly review assesses whether scope-flex events were value-additive or doctrine-corrosive
- SOP refinement: if pattern shows scope-flex is consistently used inappropriately, tighten the SOP language

## Examples from Track Record

- **GYM-TRIO Triaging (May 2026 — Triaging+ scope, the namesake):** Richard authorised "Triaging+" — Triaging decision logic + full ESA-stage research consulted (BM-Primer, Earnings-L3Y, Short-Sellers, VCMap, Value-Chain, Guidance). Worked well: bear case quality at Triaging was richer because ESA Short-Sellers report was consulted (EY auditor flag, equipment-life depreciation extension). Risk surfaced: BFIT Triaging memo would have looked cleaner under strict-Triaging — bear-case findings come from ESA research. Scope-flex flagged in every memo header + QC footer.

- **GYM-TRIO ESA (May 2026 — Option B, same session):** Richard authorised "Option B" as a capability test — ESA-depth memos authored on existing Triaging+ corpus BEFORE ESA RESEARCHER workstreams ran. Outcome: ESA memos added analytical depth (sandbagging culture confirmation, Gen Z over-index, deleveraging precision for SATS) but cannot trigger DD progression alone. 15 ESA workstreams remain mandatory pre-DD. Doctrine-deviation flag prominent in handoff package. Verdict pending at Step 4: permanent doctrine option or one-off?

- **General lesson:** scope-flex captured ~3-4 hours of value-additive analysis. Scope-flex without flagging would have masked the doctrine deviation and made future calibration impossible.

## Counter-Examples (When Scope-Flex Is NOT Permitted)

- **Routine triaging cycles.** When working through a batch of 5-10 names at IG/Triaging, the default is strict-stage research. Scope-flex creates inconsistency across the batch.

- **Time-pressure as the only justification.** "I want a deeper read now" without authorisation = pressure to violate stage discipline. The fix is to wait for the proper-stage research, not to flex the scope.

- **Anchoring the decision after the fact.** If Triaging+ research surfaces a finding that would change the case, the decision must still be made at the Triaging-stage gate (Step 4). The temptation is to write the memo as if the deeper research were already in. Resist.
