# Bill Walsh Playbook Analogy

## Definition
Great coaches don't improvise on game day. They have a tested playbook. Every situation is mapped. When condition X appears, run play Y. This removes the burden of deciding in real time and accelerates execution.

## Why It Matters
The investment process (pipeline, KQ framework, monitoring, exit triggers) IS the playbook. The game is the portfolio. Pre-decide, then execute.

## Application
When a stock shows signal X, the play is Y (30-day shot clock). When conviction is Y, position is Z%. No improvisation; just execution.

## Examples from Track Record
- Steve Ward coaching framework: process → psychology → performance → P&L.
- Creativity loop: improvise one day per month. Rest of the time: run the playbook.
