# Value Chain Analysis

## Definition
Where are the profit pools in the value chain? Is the company in the best position? Value chain dynamics determine sustainable margin and competitive positioning more than company-level analysis alone.

## Why It Matters
A great company in a bad value chain position will underperform a good company in a great value chain position. The chain determines the ceiling.

## Application
Map the full value chain. Identify where margin concentrates. Ask: is the company positioned at the point of maximum value capture? Can its position be replicated or disrupted?
