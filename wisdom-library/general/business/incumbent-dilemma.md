# Incumbent Dilemma

## Definition
Incumbents face a structural dilemma: defending current business model vs adapting to disruption. Defending is rational short-term but fatal long-term. Most incumbents choose defence because disruption cannibalises existing revenue.

## Why It Matters
Identifies businesses at risk of disruption and those positioned to disrupt. AI insight: 'For most companies, AI will be negative disruption. Leopards don't change their spots.'

## Application
When assessing a stock: is this company an incumbent facing disruption or a disruptor attacking incumbents? If incumbent, is management genuinely adapting or just defending?

## Examples from Track Record
- AI disruption: for most companies, AI will be negative. Accelerates digital transition, buries incumbents, creates new winners.
- Metacognition checklist item.
