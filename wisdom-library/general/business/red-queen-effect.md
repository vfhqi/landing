# Red Queen Effect (Running to Stand Still)

## Definition
From evolutionary biology: species must constantly evolve just to maintain fitness relative to competitors. In business: some companies must constantly invest, innovate, and compete just to maintain their current position. A red flag for investment quality.

## Why It Matters
Identifies businesses that are treadmills disguised as compounders. If revenue is growing but the company is just running to stand still competitively, the growth is not value-creating.

## Application
Ask: is the company getting relatively stronger or just maintaining pace? Long periods of slow earnings growth despite strong revenue = Red Queen effect.

## Examples from Track Record
- Metacognition checklist: 'Long periods of slow growth = red flag' and 'Running to stand still (strong revenue, weak earnings growth)'.
- Darwin's Finches variant: hyper-competitive vs defensible positioning.
