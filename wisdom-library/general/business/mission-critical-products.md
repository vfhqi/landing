# Mission-Critical Products

## Definition
Products or services that customers cannot operate without. Creates switching costs, pricing power, and revenue defensibility. The deeper the integration with customer operations, the higher the switching cost.

## Why It Matters
Mission-critical positioning is the foundation of recurring, defensive revenue. It's the strongest form of customer lock-in.

## Application
For every business: is the product mission-critical to its customers? What happens if the customer stops using it? The more catastrophic the answer, the stronger the positioning.

## Examples from Track Record
- Quality assessment framework: 'Recurring, defensive, resilient revenue streams (mission-critical to customers)' is the first criterion.
