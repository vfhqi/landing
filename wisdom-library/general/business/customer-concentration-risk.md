# Customer Concentration Risk

## Definition
Top 5 customers >30% of revenue = high fragility. Top 3 >40% = very high risk. Single customer dependency is a structural weakness that no moat can fully protect.

## Why It Matters
Customer concentration amplifies every other risk. Loss of a single relationship can be thesis-invalidating.

## Application
Check customer concentration at Triaging stage. If top 5 >30%, treat as a structural risk factor in the fragility assessment.

## Examples from Track Record
- Fragility checklist: 'Customer concentration (top 5 customers >30% of revenue = high fragility)'
- Diverse customer base is a quality signal.
