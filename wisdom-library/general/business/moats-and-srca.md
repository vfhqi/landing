# Moats & SRCA (Hard + Soft)

## Definition
Hard SRCA: pricing power, switching costs, network effects, vertical integration, negative NWC, information asymmetries, lock-in, brand, regulatory protection. Soft SRCA: culture, leadership, hiring standards, execution track record, governance. A company with only hard advantages is fragile (moats erode). Both hard + soft = structurally resilient.

## Why It Matters
The combination of hard and soft SRCA is the most reliable predictor of durable competitive advantage. Hard alone can be competed away; soft alone can be disrupted.

## Application
Assess both hard and soft SRCA independently. A company with A-rated hard and C-rated soft is less investable than B/B. Look for congruence.

## Examples from Track Record
- 1% Companies concept: top 1% on combined hard + soft SRCA. Rare but deserve premium valuations.
- S4 Capital: soft SRCA red flag (egotism in communication) despite apparent hard advantages.
