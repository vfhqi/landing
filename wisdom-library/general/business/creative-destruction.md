# Creative Destruction

## Definition
Innovation destroys existing products, business models, and industries while creating new ones. The process is continuous and accelerating. Companies that survive are those that disrupt themselves before competitors do.

## Why It Matters
Identifies the structural forces that will reshape industries over 3-10 year horizons. Critical for assessing the durability of any competitive advantage.

## Application
For long-term positioning: what would destroy this company's business model? Is that force accelerating? Is management preparing for it or ignoring it?

## Examples from Track Record
- ARK/Cathy Wood framework: 'co-evolution of disruption technologies' — adjacent technologies simultaneously enable each other.
