# Economies of Scale

## Definition
Larger firms can spread fixed costs over more units, reducing per-unit cost. Creates sustainable competitive advantage when combined with other moat sources. Not all scale is beneficial — 'bigger is not always better, but better is always better'.

## Why It Matters
Identifies businesses where growth itself creates defensive positioning. The opposite of treadmill businesses where growth requires proportional effort.

## Application
When assessing a business: does scale create a cost advantage that competitors cannot replicate? Or does scale just mean bigger without better? Check: are margins expanding with revenue growth?

## Examples from Track Record
- Metacognition checklist item: 'Scale = better not just bigger' — platforms, aggregators, physical networks.
- Diploma: supply chain positioning where scale creates procurement advantage.
