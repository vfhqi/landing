# Flywheel vs Treadmill vs Fragile

## Definition
Flywheel = gets stronger as it scales (network effects, pricing power, margin expansion). Treadmill = constant effort to maintain position (commodity, cyclical, thin margins). Fragile = deteriorating positioning. Portfolio should be 70%+ flywheel, 20-25% treadmill (sized small), <5% fragile.

## Why It Matters
Classification directly determines sizing and holding period. Flywheels compound; treadmills exhaust; fragile positions destroy capital.

## Application
For every position: is this a flywheel, treadmill, or fragile? If treadmill, size small and set clear exit criteria. If fragile, exit.

## Examples from Track Record
- Portfolio constraint: 'No treadmills, seek flywheels.'
- Kainos: treadmill — requires constant effort, not a compounder.
- Evolution: fragile — no clear positive gravitational pull, supplier vulnerability.
