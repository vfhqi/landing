# Win-Win-Win Strategy

## Definition
The best strategies create value for customer, company, AND ecosystem simultaneously. Customer-centric (solving real pain), ecosystem symbiosis (partners benefit), massive relative surplus customer utility (company captures minority of value created).

## Why It Matters
Win-win-win strategies are self-reinforcing. When all stakeholders benefit, the strategy is durable because nobody has incentive to disrupt it.

## Application
For every business: does the strategy create value for customers AND partners AND shareholders simultaneously? If it extracts value from one to benefit another, it's fragile.

## Examples from Track Record
- Kaufman six stakeholder test: assess impact on all stakeholders.
- Contrast with 'squeezed in the middle' and 'customer pick-and-choose' anti-patterns.
