# Incentives Drive All Behaviour

## Definition
'Show me the incentive and I'll show you the outcome' (Munger). Management compensation structures predict behaviour more reliably than strategy presentations. Follow the money.

## Why It Matters
The single most reliable predictor of management behaviour. Misaligned incentives explain most governance failures.

## Application
For every management assessment: how is the CEO compensated? For growth, margins, ROIC, or share price? Is there meaningful personal ownership? Are incentives aligned with long-term shareholder value?

## Examples from Track Record
- MasMovil/SMCP: founder-led businesses prioritise their interests over minority shareholders. 'You're riding a tiger.'
- Insight #5: 'Large insider ownership ≠ alignment of interests. Always a mismatch.'
