# Goldilocks Growth (20-30%)

## Definition
20-30% earnings growth is the sweet spot. 10% is too slow (inflection unlikely). 50-70% is cognitively hard to forecast, prone to stumbles. 20% growth is '10x easier to forecast'. Sexy isn't always smart.

## Why It Matters
Targets the intersection of meaningful growth and forecastable outcomes. Higher growth = more exposure to stumbles, speed bumps, rate concerns.

## Application
When assessing growth profiles, check: is this in the 20-30% sweet spot? If higher, stress-test forecastability. If lower, challenge whether inflection is realistic.

## Examples from Track Record
- Avanza: lazy extrapolation of cyclical high trading activity. Growth looked 50%+ but was cyclical, not structural.
- Confirmed across multiple holdings: 20-30% growth stocks had the best risk-adjusted outcomes.
