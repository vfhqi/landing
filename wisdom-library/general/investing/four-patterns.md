# Four Patterns (Confluences)

## Definition
The best ideas are confluences of at least two, ideally three, of: (1) Animal CEO, (2) Massive corporate change plan, (3) Huge demand explosion at sector level, (4) Very large share price decline onto bottom quartile multiples.

## Why It Matters
Single-pattern stocks can work but are fragile. Confluences create reinforcing dynamics where multiple drivers compound. The more patterns that overlap, the higher the probability of outlier returns.

## Application
At IG and Triaging, explicitly map how many of the four patterns are present. Two = interesting. Three = exciting. One = needs exceptional strength in that one dimension.

## Examples from Track Record
- Best historical returns: stocks with 2-3 patterns present simultaneously.
- Ryanair: Animal CEO + demand dynamics + share price decline = confluence.
- Pattern 4 alone (cheap) without 1-3 = value trap risk.
