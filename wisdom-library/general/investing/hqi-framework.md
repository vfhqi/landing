# High Quality Inflection (HQI)

## Definition
The intersection of business quality and inflection/transformation. Four dimensions: Resilience (business quality, moat), Inflection/Optionality (growth magnitude), Potential Returns (3Y IRR), Conviction (confidence in assessment). Categories: Robust HQI (10-15%), Resilient & Intense (10-15%), UHPYHQI (0-5%), Resilient Low Optionality (8%).

## Why It Matters
Provides the sizing framework anchored in quality assessment. Prevents oversizing fragile positions and undersizing proven compounders.

## Application
For every position, classify into an HQI category. Let the category determine the sizing range. Override only with explicit justification.

## Examples from Track Record
- Robust HQI examples: positions that delivered consistent returns at 10-15% sizing.
- UHPYHQI lesson: high potential but execution risk. Size at 0-5% until framework crystallises.
