# Risk Matrix (F x I x R)

## Definition
Three dimensions of risk severity: Forecastability (can we see it coming? Epistemic vs aleatory?), Impact (how much damage? Thesis-invalidating?), Resource Intensity (how much attention needed? Outside circle of excellence?). Aggregate by range, complexity, and mapping vs past lessons.

## Why It Matters
Structured risk assessment prevents both under-reaction (ignoring risks) and over-reaction (treating all risks equally).

## Application
For every identified risk: rate F, I, R. High I + Low F = most dangerous (can't see it coming, high damage). High I + High R = needs dedicated monitoring. Low I + Low F = noise.

## Examples from Track Record
- Risk categories: External-Fundamental (macro, regulatory, competition), Internal-Fundamental (execution, leverage), External-Sentiment (multiple, liquidity, shorts), Multiplicative (combination = non-linear downside).
