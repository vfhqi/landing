# Power of Compounding

## Definition
Small, consistent gains compound into extraordinary outcomes over time. The 8th wonder of the world. Applies to returns, knowledge, relationships, and process improvement.

## Why It Matters
Compounding rewards patience and penalises interruption. Every unnecessary exit or portfolio churn resets the compounding clock. The gap between 8% and 12% CAGR over 20 years is the difference between 4.7x and 9.6x.

## Application
When evaluating holding period decisions, ask: am I interrupting a compounding machine? When sizing, ask: does this position compound or does it require constant effort (treadmill)?

## Examples from Track Record
- Track record (2015-2025): 8.3% CAGR = top quartile but below 25% target. The gap is execution/timing, not analytical edge — compounding interrupted by exits.
- Avanza (2019-2021): compounded strongly when held through volatility. Trim on inflation concerns interrupted the compound.
