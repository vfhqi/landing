# Sizing Is the Superpower

## Definition
What separates average from superinvestors is the ability to make big bets when right. Van Tharp: position sizing accounts for 91.5% of variability in returns, not stock selection.

## Why It Matters
Getting the stock right but the size wrong destroys most of the edge. Conversely, being average at stock selection but exceptional at sizing produces outlier returns.

## Application
Before any sizing decision, ask: Is the sizing driving the expected return, or is the stock idea? If the answer is 'stock idea', the sizing is probably wrong.

## Examples from Track Record
- Van Tharp data (Apr-26): Position sizing = 91.5% of return variability.
- Track record: Concentration at 73.4% single-name max was indefensible — the upside of sizing was overwhelmed by the downside of concentration risk.
- 1Q26 redesign: 15% max position cap directly addresses this lesson.
