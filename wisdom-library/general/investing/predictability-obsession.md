# Predictability Obsession

## Definition
The singular obsession is predictable 18M-3Y EPS. Every dimension must rate A/B — anything less is 'fail'. D or C rated predictability is structural risk that rarely changes. Only viable strategy: avoidance.

## Why It Matters
More information does not equal better judgements — often the opposite. What matters is having the right information channels (trackable, quantitative, cross-referenceable) and the right mental framework to process them rapidly.

## Application
Run the 8-step EPS predictability checklist on every stock at ESA stage. If any step scores D or below, the stock is structurally risky regardless of other qualities.

## Examples from Track Record
- 8-step checklist: bridge strategy→outputs, conservative guidance, transmission mechanism, IR quality, quantitative tracking, Bayesian updating, high-frequency indicators, quarterly consensus trajectory.
- XVIVO lesson: complexity made predictability impossible — invalidation signal that came too late.
