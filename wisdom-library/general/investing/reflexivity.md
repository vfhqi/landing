# Reflexivity (Soros)

## Definition
Markets are reflexive: prices influence fundamentals, not just the reverse. Rising share prices improve credit ratings, attract talent, enable acquisitions. Falling prices do the opposite. The feedback loop can be self-reinforcing in both directions.

## Why It Matters
Explains why momentum works and why deterioration cascades. The market is not just a voting machine — it's a force that changes the thing it's measuring.

## Application
When analysing deterioration: is the falling share price itself causing fundamental damage (tighter credit, talent flight, customer hesitation)? If yes, the deterioration may accelerate regardless of management action.

## Examples from Track Record
- Goodwin: poor IR + lack of investor support caused 40% SP decline despite serviceable fundamentals. Reflexivity: the SP decline itself became the problem.
- BFF: SP decline → investor concern → further selling → reflexive loop.
