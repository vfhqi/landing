# Margin of Safety

## Definition
Buy at a price sufficiently below intrinsic value that errors in analysis, bad luck, or unforeseen events don't result in permanent capital loss. The margin of safety is the bridge between what you know and what you don't.

## Why It Matters
The foundational risk management principle. Every other risk control (diversification, stop-losses, etc.) is secondary to buying at the right price.

## Application
For every entry: what is the margin of safety? If the company delivers 20% below your base case, do you still make money? If not, the entry price is too high.

- Richard's setup profile: 'Very large share price decline onto bottom quartile multiples' = margin of safety pattern.
