# Right-to-Left Thinking

## Definition
Start with the financial output (predictable 18M-3Y EPS), then work backwards to understand what investment case delivers it, then what inputs and transmission mechanism deliver the case.

## Why It Matters
Prevents the common analyst trap of getting excited about a story without connecting it to financial outputs. Forces clarity on the transmission mechanism from strategy to EPS.

## Application
For every stock: can you clearly state the 18M EPS number, the drivers that produce it, and the tracking data that will confirm it quarter by quarter? If not, the case is incomplete.

## Examples from Track Record
- False friend pattern: HBX, Adevinta, S4, Nexi — all had stories but lacked clear transmission mechanism from strategy to EPS.
- Positive example: Fevertree — clear USA + UK drivers with trackable financial outputs.
