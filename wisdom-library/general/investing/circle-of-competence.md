# Circle of Competence

## Definition
Know what you know and what you don't. The edge comes from operating within your circle — where your knowledge and pattern recognition are genuinely superior. Operating outside it = no edge, just hope.

## Why It Matters
Prevents overconfidence in unfamiliar domains. The most dangerous situation is thinking you understand something when you don't — unknown unknowns.

## Application
For every stock: is this within our circle of competence? If not, what would it take to develop competence? Is that investment of time justified by the opportunity? Eight elements of conviction #4: 'Circle of deep expertise.'

## Examples from Track Record
- XVIVO: complexity beyond competence (Archetype #4). Too many moving parts, outside circle.
- Risk matrix: 'Resource Intensity (R)' dimension includes 'Outside our circle of excellence?'
