# Conservative Analysis, Aggressive Execution

## Definition
Thorough research with high standards. But once the case is made, act decisively. Don't wait for more information. Don't dither. Simple + completed beats complicated + incomplete.

## Why It Matters
The gap between knowing and doing is the primary constraint. Analysis quality is rarely the bottleneck — execution speed and decisiveness are.

## Application
Once IAJA produces a Judgement, the Action must follow immediately. If you find yourself wanting 'more research' after A&J, ask: is this genuine information need or avoidance?

## Examples from Track Record
- Track record: 8.3% CAGR vs 25% target. The gap is execution/timing, not analytical edge.
- Richard's self-insight: 'I genuinely know what I need to do but am not executing it.'
