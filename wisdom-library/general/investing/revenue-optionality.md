# Revenue Optionality Framework

## Definition
Seek companies with multiple sources of growth optionality: M&A expansion, geographic expansion, product expansion, pricing optionality. Key: multiple sources (not dependent on one), record of continual GTA (Greater Than Average) emergence, 'Goldilocks' growth pattern (not too lumpy).

## Why It Matters
Revenue optionality de-risks the growth thesis. A company with 4 sources of optionality can afford 1-2 to underperform. A company dependent on one source is binary.

## Application
Rate revenue optionality across all four dimensions. Companies layering multiple sources are substantially de-risked and command premium valuations.
