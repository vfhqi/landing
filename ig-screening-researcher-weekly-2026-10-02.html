4. Outside Europe, not carried

Non-European pitches and references. One line each; no entry however good the pitch.

  • The Trade Desk (United States), Yet Another Value Blog, 29 September: the week's only full-length single-stock write-up. The author reports a new ten-year chief executive officer (CEO) options package that vests in full only at $105 a share, about eight times the current price, and a roughly $150 million open-market purchase by the CEO earlier this year. He is sceptical of the signal because the company has filed an options repricing proxy that includes the C-suite, because the board gave a similar package in October 2021, and because the CEO has sold more than $1 billion of stock at far higher prices. His closing guess is that the stock performs well from here, but it is not the type of signal he looks for
  • Turning Point Brands (United States), Yet Another Value Blog, 1 October: podcast with George Baxter of Saberpoint Capital, preview only. The guest's case is that the company is the third player in nicotine pouches behind ZYN and Velo; the host lists a valuation range of $35 to $45 a share for the legacy Zig-Zag and Stoker's business and the guest's path to a $1,000 stock by 2030. Transcript behind the paywall
  • Anthropic (United States, private), ARK Invest, 1 October: fund marketing. ARK says the company has filed a confidential S-1 (the United States registration filing before an initial public offering, IPO), cites a reported $65 billion annual run-rate (ARR) revenue and reasons that a $2 trillion valuation could be a mid-20s multiple of sales. Held in the ARK Venture Fund
  • Kalshi (United States, private), ARK Invest, 1 October: fund marketing. ARK says notional volume rose 15 times year on year through August and market share is above 80%, and that it has added exposure to its ARKK, ARKW and ARKF exchange-traded funds (ETFs)
  • Birkenstock, Canada Goose, VF Corp, Pool Corp and Samsonite Group (none in universe), DeepValue Capital, 1 October: the author screened 17 apparel brands and retailers and kept three. Birkenstock (22 September) and Canada Goose (29 September) have separate write-ups linked but not carried in this email; VF Corp is planned for October; Pool Corp and Samsonite are on his list. The email was truncated before the portfolio commentary
  • Tobila Systems (Japan), with QLTU.TA (Israel) and Santec Holdings (Japan), Joe Kaye's Musings, 27 September: Tobila is his largest holding, up around 60% over the past year, with a 23 September update linked; the other two are cited as past winners (more than 500% in 18 months and more than 700% in 10 months) to illustrate that his best investments carried growth
  • Nvidia (United States), Cassandra Unchained, 1 October: preview only. The author disputes Nvidia's 27 September investor slide on retained value of its A100, H100 and B200 graphics processing units (GPUs) against a five-year accelerated depreciation curve, and draws a parallel with the 1968 computer-leasing stocks in Adam Smith's "The Money Game"
  • Microsoft, Amazon, Oracle, Meta and Alphabet (United States), Cassandra Unchained, 26 September: preview only of an abridged capital-cycle piece on the five hyperscalers; the readable part says market peaks have historically preceded peaks in net investment
  • HF Best Ideas Stock Analysis Compilation #148, 28 September, non-European names, name only: United States: Abacus Global Management, Adobe, AppLovin, Bloom Energy, Booking Holdings, Cogent Communications, Coinbase, Credo Technology, EquipmentShare.com, Fidelity National Information Services, Intuit, Jack in the Box, Microsoft, Ouster, Pacific Health Care Organization, Robinhood Markets, TELUS, Uber Technologies.
