## Section 4 — Dassault Q4 2025 Disappointment Post-Mortem (Feb 11, 2026 -19%)

1. **Top-line miss:** Total revenue €1,681M fell short of €1,697M–€1,817M guidance range.

3. **Life Sciences (Medidata) flat-guidance shock:** Subsequent FY2026 guidance for flat Life Sciences growth sparked sell-side disagreement. 4. **FX distortion:** Q4 2025 total revenue grew 1% in constant currencies but **decreased 4% on reported basis**.
5. **JPY assumption challenge:** Management built FY2026 outlook on JPY 17X assumption (truncated in extract); sell-side aggressively challenged.
6. **AI substitution overlay:** Cyclical CAD weakness now framed by sell-side through an AI substitution lens; "bear market" stock behavior reflecting terminal value fears.

**Net:** Move was triggered by miss + Medidata structural concern + FX + AI-narrative overlay. Not a single-large-customer-loss event.
