## REPORT TITLE
**RELX and Wolters Kluwer: AI Augmentation Beneficiaries Facing UI Disruption Headwinds**

## Key Takeaways (from AS report)

- **RELX's Lexis+ AI enterprise subscription base more than doubled** in the past year, reaching "multiple hundreds of thousands" of users globally, directly contradicting immediate substitution fears and demonstrating strong customer adoption of AI-augmented legal workflows.

## Section 1 — RELX Management Commentary on AI Strategy

CEO and CFO commentary (Engstrom et al., Q4 2025 earnings, 12 Feb 26): Lexis+ AI "more than doubled" enterprise subscribers in the past year, with users in "multiple hundreds of thousands now across the globe on Lexis+ AI." Demand built on trusted, curated content sets.

**Product roadmap cadence — extractive AI → generative → agentic:**
- Legal: Lexis+ AI followed by **Protégé** (integrated agentic legal assistant capable of multi-step workflows).
- STM division: Scopus AI, Sherpath AI, **LeapSpace** end-to-end researcher solution (newly announced).

**Investment intensity:** RELX maintains technology-agnostic multi-model approach supported by **$2 billion annual technology spend**.

**Pricing:** Per institutional investor reports, RELX has **introduced AI-enabled legal products at approximately a 20% price premium** relative to legacy offerings. RELX is also explicitly **shifting away from per-seat pricing in Legal**, testing new agentic tools like Protégé.

---

## Section 2 — Sell-side bear case (Rothschild & Co Redburn framing)

Four primary pillars:
1. **Growth constraints** — RELX/WKL lose historical pricing power as AI-native competitors replicate analytical capabilities.
2. **Margin pressures** — incumbents must aggressively ramp capex/opex simply to differentiate baseline offerings.
3. **UI disintermediation** — AI startups own the workflow layer; incumbents reduced to "underlying data" supplier.
4. **Terminal value risk** — share of addressable market structurally compressed.

**Water Tower Research:** markets actively pricing in immediate "workflow displacement risk" for these high-margin, text-heavy professional information businesses.

---

## Section 3 — Counter-bear: Sell-side defending the augmentation thesis

**ING Wholesale Banking (12 Mar 26):** Fear of AI disruption is "significantly overdone" following FY2025 earnings published late February. Wolters Kluwer reported **6% organic revenue growth and 7% increase in adjusted operating profit**, contradicting bear thesis of immediate fundamental decay. Argues "substantially easier for Wolters Kluwer to rapidly replicate generative AI tools than for startups to recreate decades of proprietary, expert-validated databases."

**Rothschild & Co Redburn:** "RELX's recent derating implicitly undervalues the extreme defensibility of its data moats." Models a strongly positive trajectory for RELX Legal — upgrading revenue growth forecasts to track 2–3% ahead of consensus through 2028. Estimates Legal revenue: £2,083m (2025) → £2,267m (2026) → £2,460m+ (2027/28).

## Section 6 — Q1 2026 selloff post-mortem

The February 2026 selloff was specifically triggered by **Anthropic's Claude Cowork legal plugins** announcement, causing multiple contraction not fundamentals-driven. Subsequent earnings (FY2025 results, late Feb 26) demonstrated continued fundamental resilience:
- WKL: 6% organic revenue, 7% adjusted operating profit growth.
- RELX Risk segment: 10% adjusted operating profit growth, with **>90% of divisional revenue now generated through machine-to-machine interactions and embedded AI algorithms**.
- Management 2026 guidance: underlying adjusted operating profit growth will continue to exceed underlying revenue growth across all business areas.

ING Wholesale Banking and Rothschild both call the move overdone. Sell-side defended both names post-print.
