## Prompt

Provide a deep research report on the European HVDC cable cluster — Prysmian (PRY.MI), Nexans (NEX.PA), NKT (NKT.CO) — focused on order book quality, execution risk, and competitive durability. Period of focus: 2024 to present.

Synthesise across earnings transcripts, sell-side, expert calls, and trade-press to address:

1. **Order book quality and economics.** For each of the three companies:
   - Total HVDC order book value and tenor (when does it deliver?)
   - Margin profile of recent awards vs legacy book (have margins improved?)
   - Customer concentration — top 5 customers as % of HVDC backlog
   - Project-level disclosure on the largest 3-5 awards each
   - Currency exposure and pass-through mechanisms

2. **Demand mix decomposition.** What proportion of HVDC backlog is driven by: (a) offshore wind interconnection, (b) onshore transmission build (BalWin etc.), (c) cross-border interconnectors, (d) datacentre / AI-related, (e) other? How is the AI-related share evolving? Is it growing as a share of new awards?

3. **Capacity expansion economics.** Detail on each company's announced capacity expansion programmes: timing of new capacity coming online, capex run-rate, expected ROIC, financing plan. Specifically:
   - Prysmian $2bn expansion through 2027
   - NKT $1bn HV capacity 2023-2026
   - Nexans offshore + cable-laying vessel investment
   What are sell-side return expectations? Are they de-risked or aspirational?

4. **Execution risk evidence.** Any earnings disclosures, sell-side notes, or trade-press reports on: (a) project delays, (b) cost overruns, (c) quality incidents, (d) installation difficulties, (e) liquidated damages exposure. By company. NKT execution history specifically.

5. **Vessel availability and installation bottleneck.** What is the global capacity for deep-water cable installation? Who owns the vessels? How is vessel availability evolving relative to demand? Is vessel scarcity becoming the binding constraint rather than cable manufacturing?

6. **Competitive landscape evolution.** Any new entrants — Asian, Middle Eastern, US — into the HVDC cable market? Any consolidation moves? Any IP or technology shifts (e.g., 525kV vs 320kV becoming standard, GIS technology evolution)? Any pricing pressure visible from new-entrant competition?

7. **Sell-side rating and target trajectory.** What is the sell-side rating distribution across the three names, target price progression over the past 12 months, and what specifically is driving any divergence between the three companies in sell-side framing?

8. **Capital cycle indicators.** Are any of the three companies signalling overbuild, capacity discipline concerns, or pricing concerns that would suggest the cycle is maturing? Compare to historical late-cycle commentary patterns.

Bias toward 2025-2026 sources. Quantitative evidence preferred. Flag where sell-side framing diverges between PRY, NEX and NKT.
