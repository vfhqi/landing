# AS Query 2 — Mid-tier European industrial software disruption pace

**Mode:** Deep Research
**Date submitted:** 2 May 2026
**Target tickers:** DSY.PA (Dassault), HEXAB.ST (Hexagon), NEM.DE (Nemetschek), SGE.L (Sage)
**Memo conclusion to stress-test:** Mid-tier European industrial software (one rung below SAP/Siemens) faces faster Christensen disruption than sell-side models — break-trend candidates within 18m. Sage SMB most-decomposable, Dassault CAD-only customer segment exposed, Nemetschek moderate switching cost only.

## Prompt

Provide a deep research report on the AI disruption pace facing the mid-tier European industrial and design software vendors, specifically: Dassault Systèmes (DSY.PA), Hexagon (HEXAB.ST), Nemetschek (NEM.DE), and Sage Group (SGE.L). Period of focus: H2 2025 to present.

Synthesise across earnings transcripts, sell-side, expert calls and management presentations to address:

1. **Customer cohort behaviour.** For each of the four companies, what evidence exists on customer behaviour by cohort? Specifically: (a) **Dassault** — are pure-CAD customers (no PLM/manufacturing integration) renewing at lower rates or smaller seat counts than integrated-suite customers? (b) **Hexagon** — are factory-workflow-integrated customers retaining better than standalone metrology customers? (c) **Nemetschek** — are AEC customers showing any switching evidence to AI-native architectural design tools? (d) **Sage** — what is happening to gross retention and net-revenue-retention by customer-size cohort, especially the smallest SMB tier?

2. **Management acknowledgement of AI threat.** Direct quotes from each company's management on (a) acknowledged AI-substitution risk, (b) competitive response (in-house AI agents vs partnership vs acquisition), (c) pricing strategy implications. Compare to comparable management commentary from late 2024 and early 2025 — has acknowledgement deepened?

3. **AI-native competitor mapping.** Who are the credible AI-native competitors targeting each mid-tier incumbent's customer base? Specifically:
   - Dassault CAD/PLM: Onshape (Siemens-owned now), AI-augmented CAD startups, generative-design tools
   - Hexagon metrology / factory: AI-vision quality control startups, CV-as-a-service vendors
   - Nemetschek AEC: AI-native architectural design tools, generative BIM startups
   - Sage SMB: Pylon, Rillet, AI-native bookkeeping startups, Stripe billing automation, etc.
   What is the funding trajectory, customer count, and traction evidence for each cluster?

4. **Dassault Q4 2025 disappointment post-mortem.** What was the actual mechanism behind Dassault's worst-day-ever -19% move on 11 Feb 2026? Sell-side framing: was it AI substitution evidence, was it cyclical CAD weakness, was it specific large-customer loss, was it FX/macro? What has subsequent commentary clarified?

5. **Margin and pricing trajectory.** Quantified evidence on: (a) ASP trends per seat, (b) discount rates being offered to retain customers, (c) gross margin pressure, (d) opex investment in AI capability. By company and by segment where available.

6. **Acquisition and consolidation activity.** What strategic M&A has each company done in 2025-2026 to address AI threat? What private-market deals have hyperscalers or US software firms done that could change the European competitive landscape?

Bias toward H2 2025 and 2026 sources. Distinguish company-disclosed evidence from sell-side estimates. Flag disagreements within sell-side.
