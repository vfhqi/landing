# RES - AI Thematic Research — Brief

**Created:** 2 May 2026
**Role:** RESEARCHER
**Mode:** DEVELOPMENT (thematic build) + Opus
**Owner:** Richard (Viewforth) / Watson

## Mission Command

**Context.** AI thematic build — sister to Iran and Bear Market thematics. Risk-off backdrop. Most obvious AI exposure is US-listed; European edge sits in second-order picks-and-shovels, infrastructure enablers, displaced incumbents, and regulatory/structural beneficiaries.

**Higher intent.** Equip Viewforth's thematic engine with the analytical base to (a) build a watchlist of European-listed names with above-average probability of large 18-month moves driven by AI dynamics, both long and short-of-conviction-park; (b) hold a sharper-than-consensus view on the sub-themes.

**Objective.** Deliver ~15,000-word memo that maps European AI opportunity/risk surface, identifies and pressure-tests sub-themes, and runs three analytical passes (value-chain, alternative mental models, pre-mortem). Tilt challenging over bland.

**Constraints.** European-listed (wide scope: primary EU + adjacent dual-listed/ADR with EU operating substance). Technical reader — no 101 explainers. ~18-month horizon for SP catalysts. Risk-off — heavy weight on invalidation paths.

## Confirmed gaps (post back-brief)

1. **Scope:** wide. Primary-European-listed plus US-listed-with-European-operating-substance (ASML, ARM, Spotify, etc.) and dual-listed. Mark each PRIMARY-EU vs ADJACENT-EU.
2. **Weighting:** ~60% beneficiary / ~40% trapped-capital + disruption. Disruption side gets the more contrarian analytical pass.
3. **Output emphasis:** **THEMES AND INSIGHTS, NOT COMPANIES.** Triage candidate lists are a separate follow-on project. Where companies appear, they are illustrations of sub-theme mechanics, not triage candidates.

## Three passes

- **Pass 1.** Value-chain map — sub-themes, mechanics, bottlenecks, where value accrues.
- **Pass 2.** Six mental-model lenses — capital cycle, Christensen disruption, reflexivity, base-rates of tech transitions, bottleneck/Amdahl, picks-and-shovels (with commoditisation caveat).
- **Pass 3.** Pre-mortem / inversion — "It's May 2027. Thematic destroyed European GARP investors. What happened?"

## Output

- `.docx` memo, ~15,000 words, Polished Notes register, technical reader.
- Companion Notion summary page (separate task).
- Saved to `drafts/` then promoted to root of project folder when final.

## Deliberately NOT in scope

- Named triage candidates (separate follow-on project).
- A-F scoring of named companies (separate follow-on project).
- Notion Stock Notes pages per company (separate follow-on project).
