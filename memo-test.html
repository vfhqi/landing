B.1 Business Description
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.

Bravida Holding AB (BRAV-SE) is the leading Nordic multi-technical installation and service contractor, listed on Nasdaq Stockholm with a market capitalisation of approximately SEK 15bn. The company operates in four markets — Sweden (47% of revenue), Denmark (26%), Norway (19%), and Finland (8%) — delivering electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire safety, and building-automation installation alongside multi-year maintenance and service contracts.

Revenue was SEK 29,620m in 2024. EBITA margin was 5.90% on a group basis, against a management medium-term target of 7–8%. The business is a serial acquisition compounding engine: 166 acquisitions since 2014 at 5–6x EBITA, integrating acquired businesses into the 350-branch network and cross-selling service contracts. Cash conversion has averaged 99% since the 2015 IPO, impaired to 77% in 2024–25 by disputed Norwegian installation receivables. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.10x (investment grade).

The customer base is 91,000 clients across 206 geographic locations; service-segment retention is 94%. Service mix is 49% of revenue (up from 40% in 2020), on track toward a 55%+ target under the current corporate change programme led by CEO Ola Romson.

Bravida's investment case rests on two interlocking change drivers: (i) operational margin expansion from 5.90% to 7–8% EBITA via service-mix shift and productivity programme savings; and (ii) M&A compounding — the continued rerating of acquired EBITA from 5–6x acquisition entry to ~15x market multiple on integration. These two drivers are expected to compound EPS at 15–18% per annum over the 2025–2028 horizon.
