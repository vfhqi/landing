Current architecture (16 positions, 15%/10%/8%/0-5% ladder) directly addresses this.

5. **Recovery speed:** The 2+ year recovery from -54% drawdown is slow. What would acceleration require? Better exits? Better entries? Reduced position sizing?

## APM (Assistant Portfolio Manager) Implications

Given this volatility profile, the 1Q26 structural changes are CRITICAL, not cosmetic:

**Why 16 stocks matter:**
- Reduces idiosyncratic risk: spread -54% drawdown impact across more positions
- Prevents 73% position sizing: with 16 stocks, max realistic position is ~20%, not 73%
- Built-in diversification prevents 2022-type wipeouts

**Why the 8/8 risk-on/risk-off split is essential:**
- With 5.51% monthly volatility, systematic directional exposure (8 risk-on) can amplify or dampen market moves
- The 8 risk-off anchor is risk insurance — prevents all-in-on-beta during bear markets

**Why sizing framework (15%/10%/8%/0-5%) is calibrated:**
- Top 3 positions: 15% + 10% + 8% = 33% (vs 43.4% historical average)
- This prevents worst-case concentration while maintaining conviction
- Bottom positions at 0-5% = dry powder for new ideas

**Why 30-day disprove-or-exit shot clock is non-negotiable:**
- With -54.75% drawdown potential, thesis decay in early holding must be caught fast
- 30 days is long enough to separate market noise from real thesis break, short enough to avoid compounding
- At 5.51% monthly volatility, delay = exponential risk

**Why position monitoring cadence needs tightening:**
- Historical trailing stop: none observed (hold through 54% drawdowns)
- New cadence: weekly (not monthly) for Top 3 positions, monthly for others
- Triggers: 20% loss from entry forces review, 30% triggers exit consideration, 40% triggers automatic exit
