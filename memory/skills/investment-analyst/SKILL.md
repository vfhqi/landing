## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Investment Case Construction
Build investment cases using Richard's four-pillar framework:

**Pillar 1: What Makes a Great Investment**
- Start right-to-left: predictable 18M-3Y EPS → investment case → inputs + transmission mechanism
- Test against four patterns: Animal CEO, massive corporate change, huge demand explosion, large SP decline
- ACH approach: build profiles of "no," "false friend," and "yes"

**Pillar 2: Setup Profile Classification**
- Classify into: Predictable CfC, EPT (Earnings Power Transformation), or EPSU (Earnings Power Step-Up)
- Identify which of 6 sub-setups applies
- Screen against unacceptable setups and false friend indicators

**Pillar 3: Predictability Assessment**
Run the 8-step EPS predictability checklist:
1. Can I clearly bridge strategy to financial outputs?
2. Is guidance conservative vs modal case?
3. Do I understand the transmission mechanism?
4. Will IR give useful intra-earnings operating momentum?
5. Can I track quantitative data on key drivers?
6. Can I Bayesian update forecasts as data comes in?
7. Do I have fortnightly tracking data on leading indicators?
8. Can I predict quarterly consensus trajectory?

**Pillar 4: Operator Quality Assessment**
Three dimensions:
- Right direction (ambition, strategy, growth priority)
- Right resources (CEO quality, hiring, investment, culture, guidance, communication)
- Right record/action/behaviour (RONIC, execution track record, shareholder alignment, governance)

### 2. Quality Assessment — Three-Layer Framework
**A. Normalised Growth (Revenue Optionality):**
- Recurring, defensive, resilient revenue streams
- Customer concentration (<25-30% top 5)
- Secular tailwinds (5%+ minimum)
- Multiple growth optionality sources (M&A, geographic, product, pricing)
- Record of continual GTA emergence

**B. Hard SRCA / BtE / Sector Structure:**
- Pricing power, customer loyalty, network effects
- Vertical integration, negative working capital
- Information asymmetries, lock-in, brand
- Regulatory protection, process integration

**C. People / Culture / Governance / Execution (Soft SRCA):**
- Founder-involved or owner-operator
- Top quartile management team
- Alignment of interests (follow the compensation)
- Governance and disclosure quality

### 3. Conviction Assessment — Eight Elements
1. Gut feel (after analysis, not before)
2. Conviction in framework inputs
3. Gathering information widely and recently (≥5 primary checks in 3 months)
4. Circle of deep expertise
5. Eyes-on work (own model, own memo, full refamiliarisation)
6. Recent bear case exploration ("Two Hat" brainstorm)
7. Pre-mortems and mid-mortems
8. Position monitoring quality

### 4. Fragility Analysis
Assess across four dimensions:
- **Revenue fragility:** Stability, concentration, contract risk, pricing power, discretionary vs contractual
- **Margin fragility:** Operating leverage, cost stability, working capital efficiency
- **Financial fragility:** P&L leverage, NWC, FCF, share price leverage
- **Market structure fragility:** Short interest, liquidity, shareholder base, industry sentiment

### 5. Valuation and Financial Analysis
- Build/review financial models (Excel-based)
- Consensus analysis: track sell-side estimates, identify where Richard's view differs
- Margin of safety calculation: trading multiple vs intrinsic, EPS trajectory vs consensus
- TSR analysis: target >20% for qualification
- Scenario analysis: bull, base, bear cases with probability weighting

### 6. ACH (Analysis of Competing Hypotheses)
Structure every investment assessment as ACH:
- **Rows:** Evidence dimensions (quality, growth, management, financials, macro, sentiment)
- **Columns:** "No" profile (structural problems), "False Friend" profile (looks good but isn't), "Yes" profile (genuine opportunity)
- Weight evidence against each hypothesis
- The case only progresses if "Yes" survives systematic disproval

## Analytical Standards (from Richard's Journals)

### What Richard Demands
- **IAJA on every judgement:** Information → Analysis → Judgement → Action (+ 2 downstream actions minimum)
- **Right-to-left thinking:** Start from EPS, not from narrative
- **Transmission mechanism clarity:** If you can't explain how strategy translates to EPS quarter by quarter, it's a false friend
- **No open loops:** Every analytical thread resolved or explicitly parked
- **Conservative analysis, aggressive conclusions:** Be thorough in the work, then be bold in the judgement

### Analytical Pitfalls (from Stock Lessons)
| Pitfall | Example | Lesson |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Trees not wood | XVIVO | Too much detail without strategic view |
| Execution not potential | XVIVO | Judge on what they DO, not what they COULD do |
| Event angle as main course | Flatex | Must be icing, not cake |
| Lazy cyclical extrapolation | Avanza, Covid stocks | Assume demand pulse temporary unless proved structural |
| Pret sandwich risk | Sdiptech | Nothing individually bad but collection is fatal |
| Insider ownership ≠ alignment | MasMovil/Orange | Follow the money — how is management compensated? |
| Vague financial analysis | Avanza | Must have model ready, walk the P&L |
| Legacy moat vs execution moat | XVIVO | Critical distinction — legacy decays without reinvestment |
| Growth too fast to forecast | Medartis, Wise, Cazoo | 50-70% growth = stumbles likely. 20-30% is goldilocks |

### False Friend Detection
A false friend is the most dangerous analytical outcome. Defining characteristic: zero clarity of the transmission mechanism from company actions to EPS.

**Red flags:**
- Can't clearly explain how strategy → financial outputs
- No trackable quantitative data on key drivers
- Management communication unclear or evasive
- "Pret sandwich" — no single problem but accumulated mediocrity
- "Sorta quality maybe" — if quality requires caveats, it's not quality
- CFO assessed as "poor" (XVIVO) — alone should invalidate

## Stock Classification Framework

### HQI Categories
- **VHQ ("Gravitational pull"):** Network effects, economies of scale, consolidating sectors. Highest conviction, highest sizing (15%).
- **UHPYHQI:** Ultra High Potential but Young/Fragile. Exceptional thesis but unproven. Size 0-5%.
- **Quality compounders with spice:** Good business hitting temporary air pocket. Repricing play. Core sizing (10%).
- **MQBI:** Medium Quality Big Inflection at value. Limited long-term upside. Size 6-8%.

### Tennis Balls vs Eggs
- **Tennis balls:** Solid operating momentum, will rebound to trend. Safe to hold through noise.
- **Eggs:** Negative momentum, structural deterioration. Exit first, re-attack only after troughing evidence.

### Investment Lifecycle
- 3Y+ case required
- Normal expected ownership: 18 months
- Enter within 0-6 months of trough/turn
- Alpha decay apex: ~18 months
- First 6 months: grace period. Must prove winner by month 4-6 or case is wrong.

## Output Standards

All analytical outputs must include:
1. **Setup classification** (which of the 6 sub-setups)
2. **Quality rating** (A/B/C/D across all dimensions)
3. **Predictability score** (8-step checklist)
4. **Key drivers** (trackable, quantitative, fortnightly-updatable)
5. **Transmission mechanism** (how strategy → EPS, quarter by quarter)
6. **ACH matrix** (no/false friend/yes profiles with evidence)
7. **Conclusion with conviction level** (high/medium/low + key assumptions)
8. **2 downstream actions** (always)
