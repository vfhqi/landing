### UWB-6 — THEMATICS FRONT OF MIND

When in doubt about how to research, analyse, judge, prioritise, monitor, or sell a stock — consult the live thematics first. Default to up-weighting the thematic frame in any decision.

**Why:** Five years of evidence (Covid stay-at-home call being canonical — Ocado/Avanza/MIPS all +100% in months) confirms that picking the right thematic dominates picking the right stock. The portfolio construction problem is upstream of the stock-selection problem. The value-add is identifying the major thematic and ensuring the picked individual companies do not have major issues that prevent the stock market from bidding their share prices up.

**How to apply:**
- On every session start in any stock-related role (APM, RESEARCHER, COS), load `memory/thematics/active.md` before doing other work.
- Before recommending any new research, monitoring action, or portfolio change, ask: how does this map to active thematics?
- When uncertain whether a piece of work is justified by the thematics, default to "yes, do the work" if it pulls a thematically-aligned stock forward.
- When uncertain about emphasis in any output, up-weight the thematic angle. Examples: lead memo BLUFs with thematic alignment statement; sort dashboards by composite thematic score before secondary criteria; flag thematic implications in every weekly review.
