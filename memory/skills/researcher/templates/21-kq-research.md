## MISSION

Broad research into a specific KEY QUESTION (KQ): landscape, data, sources, evidence. Output: peer-anchored, signposted, J-front + sceptical-bullet memo classifying information by confidence, identifying data gaps, surfacing second/third-order implications, AND surfacing ⚡ outliers / deliberately-weird signals at the edges of the question landscape. ≥3,000w per source ([C] + [AS]).

**Q21 vs Q22 differentiation:**
- **Q21 (this template) = BROAD RESEARCH KQ.** What is the landscape? What do we know? What are the data gaps? Exploratory, evidence-gathering, mapping.
- **Q22 (Analytical KQ) = ANALYTICAL KQ.** Given the landscape, what is the judgement? Which competing hypothesis is most likely? Synthetic, judgement-forming, ACH-disciplined.

If in doubt about which to use: a question that asks "what is X?" → Q21. A question that asks "given X, what should we conclude?" → Q22.

## CONTEXT — What the reader cares about

**Audience:** Concentrated long-only equity investor (5-15 positions), UK/Europe focus, $5-50bn market cap sweet spot. Holds 12-24 months. Singular focus: predictable 18M-3Y EPS trajectory with 25%+ IRR potential.

**Stage — any (typically ESA or DD onwards).** KQs arise from prior research where uncertainty is too high to resolve via the standard query suite. Q21 is the breadth-first probe; Q22 is the judgement-forming follow-up.

**What downstream uses this output:** APM uses Q21 evidence to inform pillar-level grading where the KQ is on the critical path. Q21 + Q22 pair often used together. Memo also surfaces on RESEARCH STAGES dashboard tab in one of three ad-hoc KQ slots.

**Canonical taxonomy reference (per D-RSR-33):** the authoritative source is `memory/skills/assistant-portfolio-manager/analysis-judgement-SOP.md`. Hierarchy: **Pillar > Group (BB#) > Element / TC > Required Attribute (RA) > Core Question (CQ)**. Q21 typically targets a single RA or sub-question that's load-bearing for the case. RESEARCHER does NOT grade — APM grades.

**Mental models:** Landscape mapping (broad-to-narrow, exploratory), Confidence classification (high/medium/low source confidence per finding), Data-gap identification (where is evidence thin?), Second/third-order implications (Concept A — look at the edges), Multiple-truths-coexist (per Concept B — different sources may surface partial truths, synthesise the elephant), Bayesian updating (each new source updates the prior — track which sources are most informative).

## OUTPUT DOCTRINE (mandatory format)

Standard v2.1 OUTPUT DOCTRINE applies — bulleted output, ≤30w parents / ≤25w sub-bullets, signposts (canonical first + invent where pattern warrants per SIGNPOST DISCRETION), peer/base-rate anchors, J-front verdicts, per-section sceptical bullets, IAJA tags, ❌/⚡ markers, inline highlights. RESEARCHER does NOT grade.

**⚡ marker scope:** encompasses (a) statistical outliers (top/bottom 5% on some dimension — e.g. data point that contradicts the consensus landscape view), (b) deliberately-weird signals — qualitative oddities the standard landscape view ignores, cross-roads exposures the consensus narrative dismisses, "things that make me go hmmmm". Sparse-by-design — ≤3 per memo.

### Memo skeleton

```
1. METADATA HEADER (ticker if ticker-specific; KQ title; stage; source; target words)
2. KEY FINDINGS (BLUF) — 5-10 parent bullets, top-3 most-informative findings
3. §1 — KQ definition + mission command brief
4. §2-§N — Landscape mapping by dimension (typically 4-7 dimensions)
5. CONFIDENCE CLASSIFICATION — section-level high/medium/low with anchoring evidence
6. DATA GAPS — where is evidence thin? What additional research would resolve?
7. SECOND + THIRD-ORDER IMPLICATIONS — Concept A edge-detection
8. AGGREGATE WEAK SIGNALS / DOWNSIDE (❌)
9. AGGREGATE OUTLIERS (⚡)
10. QC AUDIT (validator-filled)
11. QC COMMENTARY (5 bullets incl. mandatory Counter-hypothesis check)
```

## SECTIONS TO COVER

### §1 — KQ definition + Mission Command brief

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Net signal on landscape — is the KQ resolvable from current evidence, partially resolvable, or fundamentally indeterminate? — ≤30w.

**Required content:**
- KQ stated verbatim (≤30w).
- Higher intent (why does the analyst care? What decision rides on the answer?).
- Adjacent questions (what KQs are correlated / sequential to this one?).
- Stage context (where in the funnel does this KQ sit?).

**Coverage:** 6-10 parent bullets.

**End with sceptical bullet:** "If the KQ has no resolvable answer, what does that imply for the case?"

---

### §2-§N — Landscape mapping by dimension

Each dimension = one section. Typical dimensions for a research KQ: market structure, customer dynamics, supplier dynamics, competitive landscape, regulatory environment, technology / disruption, historical precedent. Adapt to the specific KQ.

**Open each section with J-front verdict bullet:** Net signal on this dimension — ≤30w peer-anchored.

**Canonical signpost vocabulary (general — adapt per dimension):** "Market structure:", "Customer dynamics:", "Supplier dynamics:", "Competitor landscape:", "Regulatory:", "Technology:", "Historical precedent:", "Cross-sector analogue:", "Geographic variance:", "Time-series trend:". Invent where pattern warrants.

**Per-dimension content:**
- 12-15 substantive parent bullets.
- Peer-anchored quantitative claims.
- Source citation per claim (especially [AS] expert calls + sell-side).
- Confidence classification at end of section: "High / Medium / Low confidence — anchor: [evidence summary]".

**Apply Concept A (look at the edges) explicitly:** for each dimension, allocate at least 2-3 bullets to surfacing the *non-consensus* read — where does the landscape have outliers, anomalies, or cross-roads exposures the consensus mapping misses?

**Apply Concept B CRITICAL ACTION 3 (multiple truths coexist):** where two sources contradict, synthesise rather than picking one. The contradiction is itself signal. Name the elephant — the underlying composite truth that contains both partial views.

**End each section with sceptical bullet:** "What's the bearish read on this dimension? What evidence is the analyst NOT seeing because it's filtered out by anchor sources?"

---

### §N+1 — Confidence classification (LOAD-BEARING)

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Aggregate confidence verdict on the KQ — ≤30w.

Per-section confidence summary:
| Section | Dimension | Confidence | Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| §2 | Market structure | High / Medium / Low | {≤25w evidence summary} |
| §3 | ... | ... | ... |

**Aggregate confidence verdict:** "high plausibility / medium plausibility / low plausibility" (full phrasing, NOT shorthand HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW; NOT grades). Anchored on the section-level evidence pattern.

**Coverage:** Table + 6-10 interpretation bullets.

---

### §N+2 — Data gaps

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Most material data gaps — what additional research would most efficiently resolve the KQ? — ≤30w.

**Canonical signpost vocabulary:** "Critical data gap:", "Material gap:", "Minor gap:", "Resolvable via:", "Unresolvable from desk:", "Expert call would resolve:", "Field visit would resolve:", "Wait-and-monitor:". Invent where pattern warrants.

**Coverage:** 6-10 parent bullets.

**End with sceptical bullet:** "Are the data gaps genuinely thin evidence, or is the analyst not looking in the right places? Concept B CRITICAL ACTION 1 — go to the most-different source."

---

### §N+3 — Second + third-order implications (Concept A edge-detection — LOAD-BEARING)

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Net signal on edge implications — what would change in the case if the KQ resolves toward the non-consensus answer? — ≤30w.

**Canonical signpost vocabulary:** "Second-order:", "Third-order:", "Cross-pillar:", "Cross-stock:", "Cross-thematic:", "Cross-roads exposure:", "Means/Motive/Opportunity:", "Power-of-incentives:", "Inversion:". Invent where pattern warrants.

**Required:** at least 2-3 bullets where the analyst applies M/M/O test (Concept A) — does any actor have means + motive + opportunity to drive the KQ toward the non-consensus resolution?

**Coverage:** 8-12 parent bullets.

**End with sceptical bullet:** "What second-order implication is the analyst flinching from? Per the obstacle is the way (Concept B / Marcus Aurelius), surface it explicitly."
