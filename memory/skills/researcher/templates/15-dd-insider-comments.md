## CONTEXT — What the Reader Cares About

**Audience:** Richard Black, concentrated long-only equity investor (5-15 positions), UK/European focus, $5-50bn market cap. Holds 12-24 months. Singular focus: predictable 18M-3Y EPS trajectory with 25%+ IRR potential.

**Stage — DD (Deep Dive):** Purpose: resolve key questions from ESA. Stress-test the fulcrum driver thesis. This query surfaces what insiders and experts actually believe vs what management says in formal settings.

**Why this query matters:** Management says one thing in earnings calls. Insiders bet (or don't) with their own money. Expert networks (former employees, customers, suppliers, competitors) speak more candidly than CEOs. The disconnect between narrative and behaviour is signal — false-friend stocks often look fine in headline numbers but show insider selling or expert-network concern.

**Mental models:**
- Insider transactions = real-money vote. Buying = conviction. Selling = potential concern. Pattern matters more than single transaction.
- Expert networks = "ground truth" layer.
- Management tone = proxy for confidence. Hesitations, qualifying language, topic avoidance = red flags.
- False friends = management saying right things but insiders selling / experts concerned.

**What downstream uses this output:** APM Pass 3 + DD-stage decision-making. Q15 feeds Pillar P4 BB#1 (Operator + leadership) + BB#7 (Insider behaviour) + cross-cutting case-invalidation watch. Memo also surfaces on RESEARCH STAGES dashboard tab.

**Analytical sub-questions:**
- Pull last 3-4 earnings call transcripts (L12-18M).
- Analyse tone shifts: more/less confident? More/less transparent? Avoiding certain topics?
- Note hesitations + qualifying language ("we think" / "we believe" vs "we know") + deflections + topic avoidance.
