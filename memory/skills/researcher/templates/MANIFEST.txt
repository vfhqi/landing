SEQUENCING & WORKFLOW
================================================================================

Stage 3: ESA (Early-Stage Assessment) has THREE passes:

PASS 1 (APM work):
  - APM reads Triaging output (Queries 1-7, Stage 2)
  - APM runs FCS Analysis across 5 Pillars + 13 Categories
  - APM identifies preliminary key drivers and risks
  - APM flags evidence gaps
  - APM proposes setup classification (HQI, BFF, Turnaround, etc.)
  - APM posts Pass 1 findings to Notion

PASS 2 (RESEARCHER work — THIS TEMPLATE SET):
  - RESEARCHER executes Queries 8-14 (7 templates)
  - Queries posted to Notion as completed (typical 2-5 day window)
  - Query 14 runs LAST, requiring {APM_KD_SUMMARY} from APM Pass 1
  - Query 14 includes "Watson Back-Brief" for APM refinement

PASS 3 (APM work):
  - APM reads Query 14 back-brief
  - APM refines FCS ratings and setup classification
  - APM finalizes ESA summary with fulcrum drivers, key risks, DD priorities
  - Hands off to DD Team with clear scope and success criteria

Execution order (recommended):
  1. Query 08 (BM/Sector Primer) — foundational
  2. Query 09 (Earnings History) — foundational
  3. Query 10 (Short-Sellers) — foundational
  4. Query 11 (Value-Chain Map) + Query 12 (Value-Chain Analysis) — parallel
  5. Query 13 (Guidance Tracking) — after fundamentals clear
  6. Query 14 (KD Assessment) — LAST, after APM provides {APM_KD_SUMMARY}

================================================================================
QUALITY GATES (All Templates)
================================================================================

Word Count:
  - Queries 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13: 3,000+ words each
  - Query 14: 4,000+ words total (or 400-600 per driver × number of drivers)
  - NOTE: These are research output word counts, not template file word counts

Quantification:
  - Every assertion on financials, metrics, trends, probabilities includes
    specific numbers/percentages
  - No vague statements ("The company is growing" ❌)
  - Specific numbers required ("Revenue CAGR 12% over L3Y" ✓)

Highlighting:
  - All Notion postings: 30%+ coverage using process_report.py
  - Highlight key evidence, inflection points, risk statements, quantified
    milestones, Red/Orange/Yellow/Green ratings

Bullets:
  - 12-18 bullets per major section (Query 14: 8-12 per driver)
  - Each bullet: 100-150 words max
  - Self-contained; avoid "as mentioned above" references

Tables:
  - Minimum 1-2 summary tables per query (see EXECUTION section of each)
  - Clean, readable, quantified, easily referenced from text

Scepticism:
  - Every query includes explicit bear-case or sceptical view section
  - Fair presentation of counter-arguments; don't dismiss
  - Ratings: Red/Orange/Yellow/Green with explanation
    * Green: High confidence; bull case well-supported
    * Yellow: Medium confidence; uncertainty remains
    * Orange: Low confidence; bear case stronger
    * Red: Very low confidence; unlikely as assumed

No Repetition:
  - Each section adds new information, doesn't restate priors
  - Avoid copy-pasting same insight across multiple sections
  - Reference repeated data points rather than repeat them

ESA Purpose (embedded in all 7 templates):
  (1) Interrogate the change thesis — is the change significant enough to
      drive required financial outputs? Is management dynamic enough to
      execute? Are the required outputs (EPS trajectory, returns) compelling?
  (2) Breadth coverage — ensure ALL aspects of the investment case are
      covered at light or medium depth. The setup title crystallises during
      ESA. ESA also establishes the key risks, key questions, and key
      confusions that DD will focus on.
