### §1 — Career History + Biography

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Net signal on CEO's career arc — operator track / specialist track / consultant track / mixed — ≤30w.

**Canonical signpost vocabulary:** "Education:", "First role:", "Tenure pattern:", "Function progression:", "Industry continuity:", "Geographic footprint:", "Promotion trajectory:", "Career inflection:", "Why this CEO got the job:". Invent where pattern warrants.

**Analytical sub-questions:**
- Education + early career.
- Career history with tenure at each role.
- Function progression (sales / ops / finance / tech / etc.).
- Industry continuity vs cross-industry switches.
- Why was this CEO appointed to {COMPANY}? Internal promote / external hire / activist-driven?

**Coverage:** 8-12 parent bullets + sub-bullets.

**End with sceptical bullet:** "What's the bearish read on the career arc? Through what mechanism might apparent operator-track CEO actually be a generalist or career-administrator?"

### §3 — Track Record at Prior Companies (pattern recognition)

**Open with J-front verdict bullet:** Net signal on cross-company pattern — consistent operator / mixed / inconsistent — ≤30w.

**Canonical signpost vocabulary:** "Prior company:", "Role at prior:", "Tenure at prior:", "Outcome at prior:", "Pattern consistent:", "Pattern inconsistent:", "Animal-CEO marker:", "Career-administrator marker:", "Crisis at prior:". Invent where pattern warrants.

**Analytical sub-questions:**
- For each prior role: company, role, tenure, outcome (revenue / margin / TSR / strategic).
- Pattern recognition: does CEO repeat consistent outcomes (operator pattern) or vary (situational pattern)?
- Animal-CEO markers across career: force, agency, dynamism, outlier standards, capital-allocation discipline, communication.
- Career-administrator markers (anti-pattern): risk-aversion, status-quo bias, missed inflections.

**Coverage:** 10-15 parent bullets + sub-bullets. Mandatory: prior-company-outcomes table (company × tenure × revenue at start × revenue at exit × margin Δ × TSR Δ × outcome verdict).

**End with sceptical bullet:** "What's the bearish read on the pattern? Through what mechanism might apparent consistency be selective storytelling? What career disappointment might be missed?"
