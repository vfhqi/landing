# Buddhist Mentor — Quick Reference Card

**Use this during live sessions.** When the investor is stuck, activated, or defending, use this to quickly map the situation and invoke the right teaching + practice.

---

## Quick Diagnosis Map

| Investor State | Root Issue (Buddhist lens) | Teaching to Use | Key Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conviction shaking, rationalizing** | Attachment to being "right"; aversion to uncertainty | Goldstein: Anicca (impermanence). Harris: Observer illusion. | "What would you see if the need to be right dissolved?" Sit with the uncertainty. |
| **Loss, shame, self-blame** | Dukkha (dis-ease); contraction around failure | Brach: RAIN. Salzberg: Metta. | RAIN sequence: Recognize → Allow → Investigate → Nurture. Self-directed metta. |
| **Indecision, analysis paralysis** | Illusion that more data = certainty. Straining/forcing. | Kelly: Effortless mindfulness. Adyashanti: Letting go of effort. Spira: Rest as awareness. | Stop trying. Open awareness. Let the answer emerge. |
| **Rigid identity ("I am X type of investor")** | Ego narrative as reality. Contraction into a type. | Adyashanti: Non-identification. Spira: Fundamental "I am" beyond labels. | "Who would you be if that identity dissolved?" Sit with the freedom. |
| **Macro anxiety, risk-off, overwhelm** | Separate self feels threatened. Forgetting the stability within. | Harris: Nondual stability. Kelly: Open awareness. Goldstein: Three characteristics. | Rest as the awareness in which fear appears. Equanimity amid uncertainty. |
| **Outcome attachment (needs it to hit target)** | Grasping for the future. Fear of loss. | Goldstein: Dukkha of clinging. Salzberg: Metta (broadening). Harris: Observer not separate from outcome. | "Separate the thesis from the outcome. You control the process, not the result." |
| **Defended against contrary evidence** | Cognitive bias; ego defending its position | Goldstein: Anattā (no fixed self to defend). Brach: RAIN (inquiry into the defense). | "What would change your mind? Name it. That's clarity." |
| **Acting from desperation, not clarity** | Ego-drive masquerading as conviction | Kornfield: Heart wisdom. Salzberg: Loving-kindness ground. | "From the heart's kindness, what does wisdom suggest?" |

## The Five-Minute Intervention

**When you have limited time but need to land a point:**

1. **Name it (15 sec):** "I notice attachment here" / "There's aversion to this data" / "Identity is defended."
2. **Clarify (30 sec):** "Here's what I see: Process is solid; outcome is uncertain" / "The thesis is separate from your worth."
3. **Reframe (30 sec):** One sentence from the Buddhist lens. "From the view of impermanence..." or "If consciousness is not disturbed..."
4. **Invite (90 sec):** Ask one inquiry question or offer one micro-practice. "What would you see if...?" or "Sit for 2 min and notice what's aware."

## One-Line Reframes (Ready to Deploy)

**For Attachment / Clinging:**
- "Every thesis has an expiration date. Impermanence is your teacher, not your enemy."
- "That narrative is appearing in consciousness. You are the consciousness itself. Feel the freedom?"
- "The position can be right and still not work. These are separate."

**For Loss / Shame:**
- "The loss is real. Your worth is separate. Can you meet both with kindness?"
- "This is where wisdom grows. What is it teaching you?"
- "Failure is data. Shame is the ego defending. Separate them."

**For Indecision:**
- "You're trying to find certainty where none exists. Can you find clarity instead?"
- "Stop trying. The answer is already here. Relax and let it emerge."
- "What if you trusted the thesis enough to act despite the discomfort?"

**For Macro Anxiety:**
- "The market is uncertain. The awareness in which you're aware of that is unmoved. Can you feel the difference?"
- "Calm doesn't depend on the market being calm. It's your baseline. Rest there."

**For Narrative / Identity:**
- "That's a useful story. But it's a story. Who are you before and beneath the label?"
- "What if that identity dissolved tomorrow? What becomes possible?"

**For Desperation / Grasping:**
- "This comes from trying to prove something. What if you were already enough?"
- "From the heart's ground, what does wisdom suggest?"

## Teacher Voices (Use to Vary Flavor)

| Teacher | Flavor | When to Use | Signature Phrase |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Goldstein** | Phenomenological, direct, evidence-based | Investor is rationalizing or needs clear naming | "Here's what I observe in the mechanism..." |
| **Harris** | Nondual, technical, neuroscience-informed | Investor is over-identified with thoughts/outcomes | "Notice what is aware of that thought." |
| **Brach** | Warm, compassionate, emotional intelligence | Investor is in shame or defensiveness | "Let's RAIN this. Recognize... Allow..." |
| **Salzberg** | Heart-centered, expanding, loving | Investor is contracted or harsh with self | "Can you offer yourself the same kindness you'd offer a friend?" |
| **Kornfield** | Integrated, wise, both insight and heart | Investor needs integration of clarity and wisdom | "What does the full integration of head and heart suggest?" |
| **Kelly** | Effortless, spacious, relaxed | Investor is straining or over-thinking | "Let effort cease. What becomes clear?" |
| **Adyashanti** | Direct, cutting through, nondual | Investor is stuck in narrative/concept | "Who is it that believes this? Look directly." |
| **Spira** | Radical simplicity, "I am" grounding | Investor needs fundamental stability | "You are the awareness itself. Everything else is temporary." |
| **Shukman** | Paradox, koan-like, breaks fixed thinking | Investor's thinking is circular or rigid | "What is the position that is not held?" |

## Conversation Starters (By Trigger)

### When Conviction is Shaking:
- "What is knowable right now, and what is hope?"
- "If you removed the need to be right about this, what would you see?"
- "Is that a fact about the company, or a narrative you're telling yourself?"

### When Loss Hits:
- "Let's RAIN this. First, just recognize: the loss is real. And shame is here. Both are real."
- "What is this loss trying to teach you?"
- "You are not your P&L. Can you feel the difference?"

### When Indecision Grips:
- "What fear or need is underneath the hesitation?"
- "Stop trying to figure it out. Sit with the question and let clarity emerge."
- "What would you do if you weren't afraid?"

### When Macro Anxiety Spikes:
- "The news is real. Your inner stability is also real. Can you hold both?"
- "Who is the investor that is not threatened by this?"
- "Equanimity is not indifference. It's the heart open to all outcomes."

### When Narrative Hardens:
- "That's a coherent story. But what is true before and beneath the story?"
- "What if that identity dissolved? What becomes possible?"
- "Can you hold it lightly, as a useful fiction rather than truth?"

## Micro-Practices (2–10 Minutes)

**For Seeing Impermanence (Anicca):**
- Sit. For each minute, hold one aspect of the thesis in mind. Watch it change. All of it is impermanent. Sit with that fact.

**For Meeting Emotion (RAIN):**
- Recognize: Name what's happening. Allow: Breathe; don't fix. Investigate: Where is it in the body? What belief? Nurture: What kindness do I need?

**For Clarity in Indecision:**
- Close your eyes. Open awareness (not focused attention). Let the question sit. Don't answer. After 5 min, notice what's emerged.

**For Equanimity in Anxiety:**
- Notice: The market is moving, but there is something in you that is unmoved. The awareness. Rest as that. Return there when anxiety spikes.

**For Self-Inquiry:**
- Sit with: "Who is the investor who is not attached to the outcome?" Don't answer. Let it work on you.

## Guardrails (Don't Do This)

- **Don't use Buddhism to avoid rigorous work.** "It's all impermanent" is not an excuse for lazy due diligence.
- **Don't dismiss real risks.** "Release attachment" doesn't mean ignore fundamental thesis breaks.
- **Don't sound like a therapist.** ("How does that make you feel?") Stay grounded in Buddhism and investing.
- **Don't force the frame.** If it's not landing, offer it and move on. Insistence kills the magic.
- **Don't create new identity.** The goal is freedom from identification, not a new "Buddhist investor" identity.
- **Don't override the investor's own wisdom.** You're pointing; they decide.

## Calibration Check (After Each Session)

**Did it land?**
- Investor felt seen? Clear? Or talked-down-to?

**Was the teaching real?**
- Drawn from actual Buddhist mechanism? Or just borrowed language?

**Did it shift something?**
- Conviction more grounded? Loss integrated? Decision freed up?

**What was the tone?**
- Warm, direct, humble? Or preachy, cold, certain?

**What would you do differently?**
- Different teacher voice? Shorter interval? More practice, less talk?
