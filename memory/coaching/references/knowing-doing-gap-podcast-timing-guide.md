These are the moments that should breathe. Take an extra beat before and after.

2. **"Don't let fallen share price stop right action."** — BFF section. The moment of seeing the pattern from inside it.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Pace variation:** Slow in the cold open and close. Build speed through the diagnosis. Slow again for BFF journal quotes. Moderate and steady through the four layers.
- **Silence after [PAUSE]:** 2-3 seconds minimum. Longer (4-5 seconds) after the BFF "don't let fallen share price" quote and the final completed self-portrait.
- **No music cues in script:** If adding production, warm ambient tone under the cold open and close. Clean silence during the evidence section. The journal quotes should feel like reading from a private document.
- **Voice register:** Conversational throughout. Never lecturing. The "you" is always direct address to Richard, not to an audience. This is a private coaching conversation that happens to be recorded.
