Glossary

Every acronym and technical term used in this note, defined in plain English.

  • APM (Assistant Portfolio Manager) — the analytical role generating this note; a human analyst's aide, not an autonomous decision-maker
  • SLQ (Short-List Qualified) — the pool of stocks passing all three eligibility gates: Sector Weight ≠ F; Timeliness A or B; Stage Signal active. The re-run produced 310 stocks passing this filter from a universe of 985 European equities
  • Universe — 985 European listed equities tracked by Viewforth Partners' APM system. Each has technical price data, sell-side estimate data, and valuation history
  • Stage 2 (Stan Weinstein framework) — a confirmed price uptrend: the stock is above its rising 30-week moving average, making higher highs and higher lows
  • Stage 1 — a basing phase: price is no longer in a downtrend but has not yet broken to new highs. A Stage 1 pick is a bet on the entry into Stage 2
  • Persistence Window — the 12 trailing calendar month-ends, each scored as to whether the stock qualified for Stage 2 (Probable, Plausible, Possible, or None)
  • Proven Stage 2 (Bucket A) — ≥4 qualifying months. The trend has been confirmed on multiple occasions; not a one-month fluke
  • Early Stage 2 (Bucket B) — 1–3 qualifying months. A young signal: real, but short track record. Higher false-positive rate than Proven
  • Stage 1 Transition (Bucket C) — Stage 1 signal only, with Valuation grade A or B. Entry bet relies on cheapness as the catalyst trigger
  • Record Length — months since the first qualifying month. Fresh ≤7 months (preferred); Mid 8–11 months; Extended 12 months (maximum — signal first appeared a full year ago)
  • SSEM (Sell-Side Estimate Momentum) — composite score −15 to +15 measuring analyst estimate revisions across six metrics over three windows. Graded A–F on a bell curve relative to all 985 stocks
  • Component deterioration — the ssem_comp.deteriorating flag; True means profit-line estimates were cut in the most recent period even if the composite SSEM score is positive. Three re-run removals (ARIS, ALC, CARM) had positive composites but deteriorating profit lines
  • Valuation Grade — how cheap or expensive the stock is vs all 985 stocks, ranked on pe_percentile. A = cheapest 10% of universe; C = middle 25%; F = most expensive 25%
  • SW (Sector Weight) — Richard's discretionary sector stance. SOW = Strong Overweight; OW = Overweight; NW = Neutral Weight; UW/SUW = Underweight; F sector = excluded entirely
  • Timeliness — qualitative entry-setup grade. A = ideal setup (VCP on verge of breakout, or clean first retest); B = acceptable setup; C = poor setup (disqualifies from SLQ)
  • VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) — a Timeliness A setup: series of smaller consolidations with lower volatility and declining volume, just before a breakout
  • cur=True/False — whether the stock still meets Stage 2 criteria as of the most recent data point. cur=False is normal signal variability; two consecutive cur=False warrant exit
  • VSL (Very Short-List) — the reserve bench: best non-portfolio names per industry, held ready for rotation
  • J→A→I — Judgement first, Analysis second, Information last. This note follows this ordering throughout

Appendix — The Three Corrections

  • Correction 1 — Valuation Grade is cross-sectional, not absolute-level:
