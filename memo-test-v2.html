B.1 Business Description 4 findings
Business Description -- A plain-English description of what the company does, how it makes money, and its primary market.
1.
Nordic multi-technical leader; four-country perimeter. Bravida Holding AB (BRAV-SE) is the undisputed market leader in Nordic building services installation and maintenance, operating across Sweden (47% of revenue), Denmark (26%), Norway (19%), and Finland (8%). Services span electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire safety, and building automation — installed work (51%) and multi-year service contracts (49%).
2.
Serial acquisition compounding engine at scale. 166 acquisitions since 2014 at 5—6x EBITA entry multiple; each acquisition integrates into the 350-branch network and cross-sells service contracts. Revenue SEK 29,620m in 2024. Cash conversion averaged 99% over 2015—2024 but fell to 77% in 2024—25 due to disputed Norwegian installation receivables. Net debt/EBITDA is 1.10x (investment-grade).
3.
Service-mix shift is the structural margin lever. Service segment has expanded from 40% of revenue in 2020 to 49% in 2025, with a management target of >55% by 2027. Service revenues carry structurally higher margins than installation (recurring, lower cyclicality, higher retention). Current EBITA margin 5.90% against a 7—8% medium-term target — trajectory is correct but pace is below aspiration.
4.
Two interlocking change drivers expected to compound EPS at 15—18% pa. (i) Operational margin expansion from 5.90% to 7—8% via service-mix shift and productivity savings (E1/E2 — Required Input Change Forces / Required Case Outputs); and (ii) M&A compounding — acquiring EBITA at 5—6x entry then rerating to ~15x market multiple on integration. Q2 Norwegian resolution on 13 July is the gating event for thesis progression.
