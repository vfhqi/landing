The seven decisions this list raises

Each has a worked example and my pick. The first three are the ones I need before building.

Decision 1. One high, used by every test

Three different highs are in use today. The S2 Pullback and S2 Base use the six-month high. The count of pullbacks or bases uses the highest close since Stage 2 (advancing) began. The VCP tests use the most recent local peak. So the Base Pivot Point and the VCP tests can refer to different highs for the same stock.

My pick: the six-month high for all three. You agreed this on 17-Nov-2026 for the first two. I propose the VCP tests follow.

Decision 2. When a pullback or base is "completed"

Today: when the price closes 5% above the high it fell from. This decides whether a stock is on the Broadening page or the Reinforcing page.

My pick: completed on the first close above the high it fell from, which is the Base Pivot Point. It matches your ruling of 17-Nov-2026 that an S2 Base ends on a close above its Base Pivot Point, and the 5% goes.

Decision 3. The fortnight: two points to confirm

(a) I read your fortnight as applying to "200/150D Crossed?" as well as to "200D Turned Up?". Week = 5 sessions, fortnight = 10, month = 21. (b) The volume test is measured over the last 5 sessions as they stand today. With a fortnight, a turn 9 sessions ago would be judged on the volume of the last 5 sessions, not on the volume when it turned.

My pick: (a) yes, both. (b) measure the volume from the day of the cross or the turn to today.

Decision 4. One formula for "turned up"

"200D Turned Up?" means higher than 21 sessions earlier. "5D Turned up?" and "10D Turned up?" mean a rise on each of the last two sessions after a fall. For the 200-day the first formula dates the turn late: Amadeus Mon 16-Nov-2026 against Mon 2-Nov-2026 on the chart.

No decision asked yet. I bring you a twelve-month comparison of the two ways of dating the 200-day turn first, as agreed.

Decision 5. The 20-day moving average in the Retest

The band of 6% either side is wide for a 20-day moving average. GVS: the 20-day is 5.81, so the band runs from 5.46 to 6.16.

My pick: keep one band of 6% for every moving average. Before it goes live I tell you how many stocks the 20-day adds to each page.

Decision 6. "Sessions within 6% of that MA" (Intercos: 47)

The column counts closes within 6% since the six-month high. No test uses that number. The Retest test uses the count over the last 10 sessions and needs 5.

My pick: replace the column with the number the test uses, shown as "9 of the last 10". This replaces my earlier suggestion of two counts side by side.

Decision 7. Stage 4 (declining): a test that nearly repeats the gate

The gate needs the 200-day moving average lower than 21 sessions ago. Test (2) needs it lower than 20 sessions ago. The two are almost always true together, so Plausible in practice needs only test (1).

My pick: bring you the count of stocks affected before proposing a change. It is one of your Stage definitions.
