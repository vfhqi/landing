How each step is measured

  • Trigger day: the close up 1.5% or more on the day; volume 1.20 times the average of the 50 sessions before it or more; the close in the top quarter of the day's range. It counts only on or after the first day of the late indicator, while the late indicator still holds (your "Q2 = A" of 13-Nov-2026).
  • "Turned up?" (my reading): the line rose on each of the last two sessions, having fallen earlier in the week; it holds while the line has not fallen since. On the 12-Nov close it gives your four re-entry verdicts of 12-Nov (Volex and Xvivo Perfusion valid; Arteche and Theon not).
  • "Flattening? (from falling)" (my reading): the line fell over the week before yesterday, and its change on the last session is no worse than a third of that week's average daily fall.
  • "Crossing Soon?" and "Turning Up Soon?" (my reading): sessions to the cross or the turn at the last 10 sessions' rate; Probable within 7, Plausible within 14, Possible within 21 (the next month). The 200-day line "turns up" on the day it becomes higher than 21 sessions earlier.
  • Retest of a rising moving average: the Master Dashboard's Retest (the close within 6% of the 50, 100, 150 or 200-day line on 5 or more of the last 10 sessions, while Pulling Back in Uptrend). "Rising" = higher than 21 sessions ago. "On low volume" (my reading) = the average volume on those days below the 50-day average. "1st" or "2nd or later" follows the count of pullbacks since Stage 2 (advancing) began.
  • "VCP? With Higher Lows?": the Master Dashboard's four contraction tests, on a stock that is Basing in uptrend (signed form: a fall of 10% or more from the six-month high, 20 or more sessions since it, price above a rising 200-day line). Prior Pivot High (my reading, from your Hexatronic note, "didn't recover the Prior High of 47 SEK"): the six-month high the base fell from.
  • Classification today, when more than one fits (my reading): a cross or turn in the last week first; then a Core classification; then Inflecting on an expected cross or turn; then Stage 3 (topping), Stage 1 (basing), Stage 4 (declining).
  • Healthy confirmation (information only; the stop-losses sell): days 2 and 3 each up 1.5% or more against the market, on 1.20 times the 50-day volume, closing in the top quarter; day 6 5% or more ahead of the market since the day before the trigger day. The market is the equal-weighted index of European companies of 500 million euros or more that the Sell Criteria use.
