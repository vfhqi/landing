How each step is measured

  • One set of definitions. Every test in this table is measured exactly as on the Master Dashboard's Investment Classification pages, by the same calculation. Each page states its tests in full, with their look-back periods, under "How this page works": the Reinforcing / Proven S2 Core Investment? page and the Broadening / Early S2 Core Investment? page (Pull Back, Retest of a rising moving average, Breakout; Basing, Recovery of the Base to the Pivot Point, Breakout from the Pivot Point), the Inflecting / S1→S2 Probing Bet? page (the 150-day moving average crossing above the 200-day; the 200-day moving average turning up), and the Stage 1, Stage 3 and Stage 4 pages (the 5-day and 10-day moving averages turning up).
  • Trigger day: the share price up 1.5% or more on the day, on volume 1.20 times or more the average of the 50 sessions before it, closing in the top quarter of the day's range. A trigger day counts only on a session on which the Late Stage Indicator is met.
  • Healthy Confirmation (information only; only the stop-losses sell): the five sessions after a trigger day. Day T+1 and Day T+2 are each healthy when the stock rises at least 1.5% more than the market on the day, on volume 1.20 times or more the 50-session average, closing in the top quarter of the day's range. Week T+1 is the fifth session after: healthy when the stock is at least 5% ahead of the market since the close before the trigger day, on volume 1.20 times average or more, with every close in the top quarter. The market is the equal-weighted index of European companies of EUR 500m or more.
  • Classification today, when more than one fits: a cross or turn in the last fortnight (10 sessions) first; then a Core Investment classification; then Inflecting on an expected cross or turn; then Stage 3, Stage 1, Stage 4.
