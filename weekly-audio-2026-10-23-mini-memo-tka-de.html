APM Mini-Memo. thyssenkrupp. The twenty-third of October 2026.

thyssenkrupp sits at rank sixteen on the Ideas Bench in the lists drawn from the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection.

The view is neutral, with medium conviction: a conglomerate being unbolted into listed parts, at a price that already pays for the two parts done and the labour agreement signed, so the remaining return rests on a Steel Europe separation with no date and no structure.

First, what the company does.

thyssenkrupp is a German industrial group of five divisions: Automotive Technology, Decarbon Technologies, Steel Europe, Marine Systems, and tk accelis, its materials distribution business. In the year to September 2025 adjusted operating profit was eighty-three million euros on revenue of thirty-two point eight billion euros, a margin of three tenths of one percent. Steel Europe is the European Union's number two flat-steel producer but bottom of its group on unit margin, Marine Systems leads the world in conventional submarines with a backlog above twenty billion euros, and tk accelis is Europe's number one materials distributor.

The chief executive, whose mandate runs to May 2031, is converting the group into a holding of listed segments. Marine Systems listed in October 2025 with fifty-one percent retained, forty-nine percent of tk accelis is approved for distribution with a listing targeted for late October, and the HKM joint venture stake went to Salzgitter in July 2026. The Steel Europe sale stalled with EP Group in late 2025 and Jindal in May 2026. The Krupp Foundation holds about twenty-one percent of the votes, Cevian Capital eight percent.

Second, the thesis.

This is a structure and liability case, not a margin-recovery case. Each listing converts a conglomerate discount into separately priced parts, the December 2025 agreement with IG Metall cuts eleven thousand positions by September 2030, the HKM exit saves more than three hundred million euros a year from 2028, and European Union safeguards have pushed hot-rolled coil toward eight hundred euros per tonne.

The Investment Analyst's memo of the tenth of October has adjusted earnings per share at about minus eighty cents in fiscal 2026, a charge year, then one euro five in fiscal 2027, one euro forty in fiscal 2028, and one euro eighty-five by fiscal 2031, after the tk accelis minority leakage.

The drivers to track are dated. The tk accelis listing. Results on the eighth of December, where the memo's test is Steel Europe earnings before depreciation above fifty euros per tonne. The January 2027 contract resets, which reverse the upgrades below seven hundred and fifty euros per tonne. And net cash, down from four point nine to two point six billion euros by June 2026. The memo rated Thesis's Change Forces' Strength B on the tenth of October: broad forces, unmoved cash.

Third, the Target Setup.

The Target Setup is Big Corporate Change-driven EPSU. The Investment Analyst's memo calls the pattern Major Corporate Change and rated Setups' Fit/Crispness B on the tenth of October: seven of twelve internal forces at B or better against the screen's three, and consensus earnings per share for fiscal 2027 and 2028 up thirty-three and forty-eight percent over twelve months. The mechanism is management and the regulator, not the business, so the setup fits crisply and the robustness does not. Demand-driven EPSU fails: this is price, not demand.

Fourth, Case Riskiness and simplicity.

Complex and risky. The memo rated Case Riskiness D on the tenth of October, where A is simple and low-risk: eight company-specific causes for concern against a threshold of three, and a chain to earnings per share that is vague below segment level. The fragility sits in liabilities no third party has taken: a brokers' aggregate of eleven point four billion euros of pensions, net debt and decarbonisation drag exceeds the nine billion euro market capitalisation, and the memo's downside to the five-year median multiple is thirty percent.

Fifth, business quality.

Weak, with the two good parts being given away. The memo rated Foundations' Robustness D on the tenth of October. Clean post-tax return on capital employed averaged four point three percent over fifteen years, and the group burned cash in fourteen of fifteen years. Marine Systems and tk accelis are real franchises, but both are being floated, while the parent may be left with the steel asset and its negative standalone equity. The operator has delivered four transactions in twelve months, with two chief financial officers in eighteen months against.

Sixth, returns and valuation.

At about thirteen euros sixty on the twenty-third of October the shares trade on nine point nine times current earnings. The ratings store's TSR/Valuation of the twenty-third of October is C, graded on the memo's modal return rebased to today's price: about sixteen percent a year over eighteen months, carried by the tk accelis distribution, and eleven percent a year over three years, below the twenty percent bar. The three-year bar clears at ten euros seventy-six, near the two-hundred-day average of eleven euros twenty-seven. A completed break-up at eleven and a half times earnings gives nineteen percent a year at three years, at odds the memo puts at thirty percent. A thirty-two percent drop-through to net income, against the forty percent consensus implies, gives a three-year loss. Sell-side Momentum in the store is A as at the twenty-second of October, and the script's Technical Momentum reading of the twenty-third of October is B; the memo had pinned B and A.

Seventh, the value chain and thematics.

The pros are policy and defence: safeguards cut tariff-free flat-steel imports by about half, and Marine Systems has sovereign customers of forty years' standing. The cons are structural: automotive is about half of Steel Europe's volumes and not turning, and German energy prices are among the highest in the world. The memo's judgement: the supportive forces can be reversed by policy, the opposing ones cannot. The script's Thematic Fit/Momentum reading of the twenty-third of October is C, where the memo pinned B.

Eighth, the strongest counter-case.

The park is circular. The two D grades describe the conglomerate as it was, and the break-up is the cure. The tk accelis listing is weeks away, Marine Systems traded up fourteen percent on its first day, Deutsche Bank sees the listing crystallising one to three euros a share above street expectations, and an activist presses for the steel sale. Waiting for a price twenty-one percent lower forfeits an eighteen-month return the memo puts at fifteen percent a year, or thirty-three percent if the break-up completes. The memo's answer: the bar is twenty percent at three years and the plausible adverse case is a loss.

The one-line view.

Neutral, with medium conviction. thyssenkrupp is a conglomerate being unbolted into listed parts, at a price that already pays for the two parts done and the labour agreement signed, so the remaining return rests on a Steel Europe separation with no date and no structure.
