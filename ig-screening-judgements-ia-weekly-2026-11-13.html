How the grades are set

Comparison set: the candidate pool of 178 stocks. Base rate: the pool's own spread of snapshot letters, counted from 10-candidate-pool.md. A provisional grade is the percentile, inside the pool, of the middle of the group of stocks sharing the snapshot letter, moved a notch only where the entry says why. A provisional grade below the snapshot letter is not a downgrade: the pool is selected on relative strength, so 60 of its 178 stocks hold a snapshot A on technical momentum and that A sits at about the 83rd percentile, a B.
