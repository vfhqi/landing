Week of Friday 2026-10-09. This week produced no lists. What follows says why.

Suggestions for Richard’s consideration, not instructions to trade

What happened

This week the run stopped before any lists were made. The weekly pack assembler refused to build, because one of the layers it reads, the long-list designations table, was last updated on 2 October 2026 and is therefore 7.2 days old against its limit of 4 days. The staleness is not a broken job. The old nightly long-list refresh was deliberately retired on 3 October 2026, once the Lists began to be made from the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection instead, and nothing has refreshed that table since. So the assembler will refuse every week from now on until its staleness check stops reading a layer this line no longer uses. No lists, no Fitness-for-Fighting judgement and no evening notes were produced this week, and the stale data was not analysed as though it were current. What happens next: removing the long-list designations layer from the assembler staleness check is a change for a supervised session, not for an unattended run, so it waits for Richard.

Why you are seeing this rather than nothing

The weekly line publishes a page every week whether or not it had a good week. A week that produced nothing is the week most worth seeing, because a pipeline that fails silently is a pipeline nobody notices has stopped. That is how the previous weekly product died in June 2026: it simply stopped running and left no trace.

Nothing on this page is a judgement. No lists were formed, no stock was ranked, and no memo was commissioned. Last week’s lists stand unchanged until the next successful run, and they are still on last week’s page.

What happens next

If the run was cut partway, the resume run picks it up from the last completed step and publishes over this page. If a layer of data was stale, the run waits for that layer to refresh and tries again at its next scheduled time. Either way nothing needs to be done by hand, and if the same thing stops two weeks running, that is worth looking at.
