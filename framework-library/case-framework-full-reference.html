2. The 6-stage funnel — purposes & target conditions

The investment process moves an idea through six discrete stages. Each has its own primary purpose and target condition. Cases that fail park; cases that clear graduate.

Key shift from old approach: stages are sequential filters, not deepening passes. The old model ("onion peeling") covered the entire case at every stage. The new model focuses on different specific elements at each stage, with cross-stage execution providing cumulative coverage.
#StagePrimary purposeTarget condition (verbatim)
IGeneration / screeningQualify a caseRequire sufficient technical momentum
IIFiltering outFilter out cases with obvious headwindsPlus … Avoid avoidable losses by side-stepping large and/or worsening 'exogenous' headwinds/risks
IIITriaging (first A&J pass)Lightly triage attractivenessPlus … Require large amounts of positive "change forces" (because stock markets react to change (2nd derivative) even more than the [vector])
IVESA (second A&J pass)Broadly test attractivenessPlus … (A) Change forces must manifest in financials improving — not just qualitative narrative. As measured by guidance beats and positive sell-side consensus earnings upgrade momentum (which drive algorithmic buying/selling and trend-following). (B) Sufficiently "bankable" foundation ("business quality") needed for the market to extrapolate momentum into mid/long-term and drive multiple expansion.
VDeep dive (third A&J pass)Robustly probe riskinessPlus … Avoid avoidable case failure and horrible return-on-time by (A) shunning mediocrity in management quality, business quality, case predictability, KD trackability, and (B) avoiding 'weirder, worser, further (down), for longer' and 'cockroaches' patterns; only backing simple, crisp, top decile/quartile A/B cases.
VILiveMitigate / avoid / reduce risk of capital lossUse the defined "case components" and the monitoring of leading tracking indicators to assess monthly/more regularly if the fundamental investment case is tracking to plan or invalidated and to be exited

Stage TCs are NOT rated. They are qualitative alignment language. Go/no-go decisions come from the combination of element ratings at the stage. There are no per-element acceptance thresholds. The gate is APM judgement.

5. The 6 master ratings + hierarchical rating layer

The 6 master ratings are the highest-level ratings for a case — same subjects as the old P1–P6 pillars, renamed. Master ratings are loosely-derived qualitative summaries: APM uses element ratings as primary inputs but retains discretion to weight and override.

#Master ratingWhat it summarises

MR2Sell-side momentumNeeded for Stage 2 stocks to "keep going"
MR3Thematic fitWhen long the right thematics, picking is easy

Hierarchical rating structure

ALL Pillars + Groups + TCs + RAs + CQs are explicitly rated by APM — every layer is a separate APM judgement, informed by but not derived from lower layers.

Master ratings (6)
  ↓ informed by, not derived from
Pillar ratings (I, II, III)
  ↓
Group ratings (Pillar II's 5 groups, Pillar III's 3 groups)
  ↓
Element ratings (E1–E32)
  ↓
Sub-question ratings (where applicable)

The judgement layer is at every level. A Master Rating can be A even if one element is C, if the APM judges that element non-load-bearing — and vice versa. This is where APM expertise adds value above mechanical roll-up.

6. Pillars I, II, III — the case structure

The case is structured into three pillars. Pillars I + II = case ATTRIBUTES (the "teaching manual"). Pillar III = case COMPONENTS (the "in-flight checklist"). See § 8 for the plane analogy.

Pillar I — Case change elements (3 elements)

Whether the case has enough exciting change to drive the share price.

  • E1 Required "input change forces" (internal/external)?
  • E2 Required case "outputs"? (financials must improve, not just narrative)
  • E3 Sufficient TSR / valuation upside?

Linked: E1 drives E2 → market rewards via E3.

The bankability foundations — what makes change forces extrapolable mid/long-term.

8. The plane analogy — operating cadence

The CASE is about CHANGE (Pillar I) on a foundation base (building blocks, Pillar II). These are the case ATTRIBUTES.
Case COMPONENTS (Pillar III) is a synthesis distilled into a position management playbook.

Plane analogy: ATTRIBUTES (Pillars I + II) = a plane educational teaching manual. COMPONENTS (Pillar III) = simple, short in-flight checklists. "You need to research, analyse, judge Pillar I and II to be able to define Pillar III, then you can sort of discard all that learning — like a pilot focusing on in-flight checklists, not re-reading the teaching manual."
PillarPlane analogyWhen APM engagesCadence
I Case changeAerodynamic theoryDD-stage A&J; major case re-evaluationsOnce per stage; rarely re-touched after DD
II Case building blocksEngineering specsDD-stage A&J; major eventsOnce per stage; rarely re-touched after DD
III Components / playbookPilot's flight manualEvery flight (every monitoring run)Weekly (RR23) + Monthly (review meeting)

Cognitive efficiency: once a plane is built and the pilot is trained, the pilot never re-reads the engineering specs. Same with held positions: Pillar III is the operating layer; Pillars I + II are reference-only. This is the load-bearing concept for Stage VI throughput.

  2. Walk rows top to bottom; for each row with a non-blank cell, produce per cell instruction:

    • Row 52 E1: first pass

The "broadly" caveat — top of the matrix, not separate

Rows 48 + 49 (exec summary + master ratings) are the top of the matrix, sitting alongside the deeper element work below. They give a light, full-case summary at every stage from Triage onwards, even when most pillars are still under-informed. One document, one matrix: light at the top + deep for in-scope elements below.

12. Strategic context — doctrines, INVALIDATION ACHs, glossary

The two-engine doctrine (Stages III + IV)

From Stage IV target condition. Investment cases need two engines working together:

EngineDrivesMechanism
Engine 1 — Change momentumNear-termEarnings beats → SS upgrade momentum → algorithmic + trend-following buying
Engine 2 — BankabilityMid/long-termMarket extrapolates momentum → multi-year compounding → multiple expansion

Both required. Strong change without bankability fizzles. Strong bankability without change has no fuel. The exec summary judgement explicitly evaluates both engines at every stage from Triage.

Mediocrity-shunning discipline (Stage V)

From Stage V target condition. Three load-bearing principles:

  1. Mediocrity-shunning: cases scoring C/D in operator quality, BQ, predictability, KD trackability are parked, not progressed. Don't grind on improving mediocre judgements.
  2. "Weirder, worser, further (down), for longer" / cockroach pattern: a case keeps surfacing new issues, each worse than the last. Park immediately.
  3. Top-decile / top-quartile A/B only: only A or B grade cases clear DD. C/D/F park.

Stage V is the finest mesh in the funnel.

The 10 INVALIDATION ACHs (Stage VI exit-trigger framework)

Locked 21-Apr-26. These 10 thresholds apply only while owning a position. Each is a one-strike rule — when triggered, APM escalates to Richard with sell/trim recommendation. Live in D.II.1 of every memo.

#NameTrigger condition
1Top-lineOne probable/actual near-term revenue cut to SS/G caused by exogenous problem
2CockroachOne actual + one probable, OR three probable/likely current/near-term problems
3DithererDeterioration in operator assessment AND one or more current/near-term probable/actual problems
4CyclicalSP underperformance ≥15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY plausible+ near-term revenue cut from threshold+ exogenous problem
5NT/MT one-twoSP underperformance ≥15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY any probable/actual near-term cut
6Wisdom of crowdSP underperformance ≥15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY any plausible+ VF or actual SM threshold+ concerns re. mid/long-term growth, margins, SRCAs
7Market catch-upVF (not SM) plausible+ threshold+ concerns FOLLOWED BY SP underperformance ≥15% or 3M
8Narrow framePeerset underperformance ≥15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY any actual cut/problem
9SS EEG≥2% SS EPS cuts AND SP underperformance ≥15% or 3M (either order)
10Case outputs/attributes≥8 case-output thresholds at D or F
