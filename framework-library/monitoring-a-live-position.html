§3 — BB → ACH mapping — how Building Blocks underpin the 10 INVALIDATION ACHs

The 10 INVALIDATION ACHs (D.II.1 of every memo, monitoring tripwires at Live) are the operational exit framework. They are NOT independent of the BB families — they trigger when BB family ratings deteriorate.

Explicit mapping:

ACH # Name Trigger condition Underlying BB family/families What APM monitors
1 Top-line invalidation One probable/actual near-term revenue cut from exogenous problem BB#3 Paradigm Fit + IC #1 Required Case Outputs Revenue trajectory; sector demand state
2 Cockroach invalidation One actual AND one probable, OR three probable problems BB#4 Seek-to-Avoid + BB#6 Invalidating Multiple deterioration signals across BB families
3 Ditherer invalidation Deterioration in operator assessment AND one+ problems BB#2 G3 E9 Operator quality + BB#4/BB#6 Operator quality drift + supporting deterioration
4 Cyclical invalidation SP -15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY plausible+ near-term revenue cut from exogenous BB#3 Paradigm Fit + BB#7 Momentum Technical breakdown + macro/cycle deterioration
5 NT/MT one-two invalidation SP -15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY any probable/actual near-term cut BB#7 Momentum + IC #1 Outputs Technical breakdown followed by fundamental cut
6 Wisdom of crowd invalidation SP -15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY plausible+ mid/long-term concerns BB#7 Momentum + BB#2 Foundation Quality Technical breakdown + deepening bankability concerns
7 Market catch-up with our existential concerns VF plausible+ concerns FOLLOWED BY SP -15% or 3M BB#2 Foundation Quality + BB#7 Momentum VF flagged BB#2 concern then market caught up (the reverse of #6)
8 Narrow frame invalidation Peerset underperformance of 15% or 3M FOLLOWED BY any actual cut BB#3 Paradigm Fit + IC #1 Outputs Peer technical breakdown + own fundamentals breaking
9 SS EEG invalidation [2]%+ SS EPS cuts AND SP -15% or 3M (either order) BB#7 Momentum (SS side) + IC #1 Outputs Sell-side EPS revision velocity
10 Case outputs/attributes invalidation [8]+ case-output thresholds at D or F IC #1 Outputs (cluster) + Cross-family C/D accumulation Composite rating deterioration (the Pret Sandwich trigger formalised)

Why this mapping matters: - IA authors the 10 ACHs at D.II.1 with stock-specific instantiations. The instantiation should explicitly reference the BB family that would deteriorate — e.g., ACH #3 Ditherer for HTRO might say "if CEO Fröberg's track record on M&A integration shows signs of dilution (e.g., back-to-back acquisitions at >7x EBITDA) — BB#2 G3 E9 Operator quality deterioration trigger" - APM monitors the BB family ratings on live positions; when any rating drops into D territory, APM checks which ACH(s) it could trigger and escalates if threshold met - This is the operational backbone of the Stage VI Live operating system

10 Standard ACH Invalidation Scenarios — APM Deliverable #11 (fixed template, same every stock):

Source: Richard's Investing SOP (Notion page 2eb35e90). These are the 10 general invalidation thresholds applied to EVERY held position, plus 2 accept/navigate scenarios. At ESA they are hypothesised (1 page); at DD they are fully assessed (1-2 pages). Watson applies these to the specific stock by mapping each scenario to concrete, observable triggers.

# Scenario Trigger
1 Top-line invalidation One probable/actual near-term revenue cut to SS/guidance caused by exogenous problem
2 Cockroach invalidation One actual + one probable, OR three probable current/near-term problems (internal or exogenous, impacting profits or revenue)
3 Ditherer invalidation Deterioration in operator assessment + one or more current/near-term probable/actual problems
4 Cyclical invalidation SP underperformance of 15% or 3M THEN plausible+ near-term revenue cut caused by threshold+ exogenous problem
5 NT/MT one-two invalidation SP underperformance of 15% or 3M THEN any probable/actual near-term cut (internal or exogenous, impacting profits)
6 Wisdom of crowd invalidation SP underperformance of 15% or 3M THEN plausible+ concerns re mid/long-term growth rate, margins, SRCAs or predictability
7 Existential invalidation Plausible+ threshold+ concerns re mid/long-term growth, margins, SRCAs or predictability THEN SP underperformance of 15% or 3M
8 Narrow frame invalidation Peerset underperformance of 15% or 3M THEN any actual cut/problem (internal or exogenous)
9 SS EEG invalidation 2%+ SSC EPS cuts + SP underperformance of 15% or 3M (either order)
10 Case outputs invalidation 3 or more case output thresholds are O or R
11 (accept/navigate) Three-forward, one-and-done-back One internally-caused near-term profit/revenue problem + solution in-motion + no further cuts + cause not exogenous + SP not in 15%/3M downtrend
12 (accept/navigate) PT cut accept/navigate Cuts to PTs or ratings not relevant; EPS or SP thresholds will pick it up

Action on identification of deterioration: (a) Onto "disprove or reallocate" list. (b) Check for cockroaches not travelling alone = invalidate forcefully. (c) Assume "worser, weirder, further, longer" for 6-12 months if cockroach confirmed — step away, read lightly, don't analyse. (d) Inviolate: must have passed CfC clearing event before re-engaging.

Investment Case Drivers (ICD) Framework — APM Deliverable #10: APM classifies and ranks ALL drivers affecting the stock: - Fulcrum Drivers (1-2): SP-defining. Where the share price moves most. Each FD gets: qualitative description, financial output mapping, 1-2 Leading Tracking Indicators (LTIs). - Key Drivers (up to 4): Material but not SP-defining. - Secondary Drivers: Minor, documented for completeness. - Tertiary Drivers: Noise — explicitly classified as noise so RESEARCHER/APM don't waste time. Transmission mechanism: INPUT (CEO/strategy) → INPUT (operational execution) → INPUT (qualitative change) → KFM → FSO → EPS → SP. APM judges WHERE the SP-moving fulcrum sits in this chain.

Monitoring Plan Handoff (APM → RESEARCHER): Deliverable #20 = Monitoring Instructions. APM specifies WHAT to monitor, WHY (higher intent), and FREQUENCY. RESEARCHER executes the monitoring per the Monitoring Plan database. Findings feed back into databases/monitoring/findings-log.json for APM to review at next cycle.

Active CONTROL Switch Prompting (Risk-Off Mode) [09-Apr-26]

When Richard is in risk-off mode (macro retrenchment, geopolitical stress, defensive positioning), Watson proactively applies the CONTROL Switch lens during ALL portfolio discussions. The "Two Richards" pattern is most dangerous here — Bear Market Richard becomes tentative, overthinks, delays exits, and lets positions drift.

Watson's active prompting protocol during risk-off: 1. Before any position discussion: Name the regime — "We're in risk-off mode. I'll be watching for interference patterns." 2. When Richard hesitates on an exit: Directly ask — "Is this analytical caution or FOFR? The evidence says [X]. What's the one-sentence thesis status?" 3. When sizing comes up: Remind — "Risk-off environment: cap average position at 8%. High-vol names at 6-8%. Painful prudence applies." 4. When Richard wants to add to a declining position: Challenge — "Is this conviction or commitment bias? What has changed since entry that supports adding?" 5. At end of any portfolio discussion: Quick check — "Any position you're avoiding looking at? That's the one we should look at."

Standing instruction (Richard, 09-Apr-26): Yes, actively prompt on CONTROL Switch during portfolio decisions while in risk-off mode. Don't wait to be asked.

APM-Side 10 INVALIDATION ACH Monitoring Discipline [Phase G v2, 12-May-26]

Why this section exists: The 10 INVALIDATION ACHs are listed in this file (under Exit Decision Protocol) but the section conflates IA-side AUTHORING with APM-side MONITORING. The two roles do different things with the same 10 ACHs at different stages of a position's lifecycle. This section makes the split explicit.

§1 — Role split on the 10 ACHs

IA-side responsibility: AUTHOR the 10 ACHs at D.II.1 of every memo (Triaging onward). - Triaging: hypothesising-tagged (*Hypothesising at this stage — to be firmed up in next stage.*); generic trigger conditions - ESA: hypothesising-tagged but with stock-specific instantiation of each trigger - DD: fully populated, NOT hypothesising — stock-specific quantitative thresholds per ACH - Live: monitoring tripwires (weekly RR23 + monthly review)

IA reference: see IA THINKING SOP §11 + IA SKILL.md §9 for verbatim 10 ACHs + authoring discipline.

APM-side responsibility: MONITOR the 10 ACHs on Live positions + escalate when triggered. - Weekly scan via RR23 monitoring output - Monthly review of any ACH approaching trigger threshold - Immediate escalation to Richard when an ACH triggers - Sell/trim recommendation with reasoning chain

These two roles run in parallel — IA's authoring is the SPEC; APM's monitoring is the EXECUTION.

§2 — APM's weekly ACH monitoring workflow

Trigger: Every Friday PM (part of standing APM weekly review).

Workflow:

  1. Load active Live positions from pipeline.md (state field = "Live")

  2. For each Live position, load: - The position's most recent memo's D.II.1 ACH instantiations - The position's current state per Master Dashboard (master-dashboard/data/prices.json + filter-results.json + factset-ssem.json + factset-valuation.json) - The position's current monitoring plan (databases/monitoring/monitoring-plan.json) - The position's RR23 weekly monitoring output (RESEARCHER deliverable) - The position's Notion stock notes — recent Case file / RNTS entries - Any pullback monitor data (COWORK/pullback-data.json if available)

  3. For each of 10 ACHs, evaluate trigger state:

ACH What APM checks
#1 Top-line invalidation Has any near-term (next 60-90d) revenue cut to SS/guidance materialised from exogenous problem? (sector demand collapse / regulatory action / customer loss)
#2 Cockroach invalidation Has one actual problem materialised? If so, search hard for the second — assume 2-3 more in dark spaces. Count of probable problems: ≥3 = trigger
#3 Ditherer invalidation Has operator assessment deteriorated (CEO/CFO change, governance issue, capital allocation drift)? Combined with any current/near-term problem?
#4 Cyclical invalidation Has SP underperformed 15% over 3M? If so, has a plausible+ revenue cut from exogenous problem followed (or is one imminent)?
#5 NT/MT one-two invalidation SP underperformance 15% / 3M FOLLOWED BY any probable/actual near-term cut to profits?
#6 Wisdom of crowd invalidation SP underperformance 15% / 3M FOLLOWED BY plausible+ VF or actual SM threshold+ concerns re mid/long-term growth / margins / SRCAs / predictability?
#7 Market catch-up with our existential concerns VF (not SM) plausible+ threshold+ concerns re mid/long-term FOLLOWED BY SP underperformance 15% / 3M? (the reverse pattern of #6)
#8 Narrow frame invalidation Peerset underperformance 15% / 3M FOLLOWED BY any actual cut/problem on own position?
#9 SS EEG invalidation [2]%+ SS EPS cuts AND SP underperformance 15% / 3M (either order)?
#10 Case outputs/attributes invalidation Count of case-output thresholds at D or F: ≥8 ratings at D or F across the in-scope rating set?

  1. Categorise each ACH: - NOT-ARMED: trigger condition not present - ARMED: one half of the trigger condition present (e.g., SP -15% but no fundamental cut yet for ACH#4) - TRIGGERED: full trigger condition met - TRIPPED + ACTIONED: triggered + APM has already escalated

  2. APM action by category: - NOT-ARMED → no action, continue monitoring - ARMED → flag in apm-output-queue.md for COS morning routine + tighten monitoring cadence for that ACH to twice-weekly - TRIGGERED → IMMEDIATE escalation to Richard with sell/trim recommendation + reasoning chain. Do NOT wait for second confirmation. Per the one-strike rule. - TRIPPED + ACTIONED → log resolution in memory/apm/calibration-log.md (date, ticker, ACH#, action taken, P&L impact)

  3. Update memo D.II.1 to reflect ACH state (TRIPPED/ARMED/NOT-ARMED). This becomes the live state machine for the position.

§3 — APM's monthly ACH review

Beyond weekly trigger scanning, APM does monthly pattern detection across the portfolio:

Cross-portfolio scans: - Cohort-shared ACHs: if any ACH would trigger on ≥2 cohort positions simultaneously, escalate as cohort-level concern (per Phase 4.6 cohort hot wash output) - Sector-shared ACHs: if ACH#4 Cyclical or ACH#8 Narrow Frame would fire on a sector cluster, escalate as sector-rotation signal to Richard - Cumulative armed ACHs: if any position has 3+ ACHs ARMED simultaneously, escalate as compound-deterioration concern (the "Pret Sandwich at the live level")

Pattern detection over rolling 6 months: - Which ACHs fire most frequently? (informs IA's future authoring — give those ACHs deeper instantiation) - Which ACHs never fire? (informs Wisdom Library — may be poorly framed for Richard's actual portfolio) - Which APM actions on triggered ACHs led to good outcomes vs bad? (informs the calibration log)

§4 — Integration with the rating-quality discipline

APM's ACH monitoring is the OPERATIONAL extension of the Quality of Ratings discipline (§0.5 above): - A rating that was defensible at ESA may deteriorate during Live. ACH triggers detect that. - The calibration log links Richard's case-level overrules at Step 4 to ACH patterns over the position's lifecycle. - When an ACH triggers + Richard agrees with APM's sell/trim recommendation: positive calibration. When Richard overrides APM's escalation ("hold despite ACH trigger"): calibration log captures the override reasoning — informs APM's future escalation discipline.

§5 — What APM does NOT do with the ACHs

  • APM does NOT author the ACHs. That's IA scope at D.II.1 of each memo.
  • APM does NOT re-define the 10 ACHs. They're a fixed framework (per decisions.md SSoT). APM can flag "ACH X seems poorly defined for THIS stock" — but the framework change goes through Richard.
  • APM does NOT exit a position on an ARMED ACH (not yet triggered). Premature exits violate the one-strike rule's other half — exit when triggered, not when potentially triggering.
  • APM does NOT skip the escalation when triggered. The one-strike rule means APM acts immediately upon trigger. Delay = ostriching.

§6 — Worked example: APM weekly scan flags ACH #3 Ditherer ARMED on hypothetical position

Position: Hypothetical Live position with 6.5% sizing.

Weekly scan finding (APM, Friday PM): - Position's most recent memo D.II.1 ACH #3 Ditherer trigger: "Deterioration in operator assessment (e.g., CFO departure, capital allocation drift) AND any current/near-term probable/actual problem" - This week: CFO departure announced + Q1 results miss on revenue (-3% YoY vs consensus +1%) - ARMED → TRIGGERED transition

APM action (within same Friday): 1. Escalate to Richard via apm-output-queue.md: - "ACH #3 Ditherer TRIGGERED. CFO departure (X-May-26) + Q1 revenue miss -3% vs +1% consensus. Recommend trim 50% (3.25% → 1.5%) pending Q2 results clarity + new CFO appointment." - Include reasoning chain: M/M/O test on CFO departure (Means = governance discretion; Motive = unknown; Opportunity = post-Q1 timing suspicious); cohort cross-impact check (does this affect cohort peers? No); track-record parallel (Fasadgruppen 2022 had similar pattern, lost 70bps) 2. Update memo D.II.1 to mark ACH #3 as TRIPPED with date + evidence 3. Add to COS morning routine for Richard to action within 24-48h 4. If Richard approves: APM (or EA) executes trim; calibration log entry captures the outcome

This is the one-strike rule in operation. APM doesn't wait for a second confirmation; the trigger fires the action.
