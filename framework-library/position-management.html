Position Entry and Exit Rules

The one statement of the position entry and exit rules in force. Every other file points here and does not restate these rules in full. Named by Richard, 2-Nov-2026 ("Q7 = A"). Last changed 2-Nov-2026 (decisions D-PMS-V4-129 to V4-132 in projects/SA - Position Management System/decisions.md; Richard's words in RICHARD-VERBATIM-2026-11-02-POSITION-ENTRY-AND-EXIT-RULES-SUMMARY-CHANGES-AND-ANSWERS.md in that folder). Change it only on Richard's word; superseded text goes to POSITION-ENTRY-AND-EXIT-RULES-ARCHIVE.md, word for word.

Terms: RS = relative strength; SS = sell-side analysts; PT = target price; SP = share price; MA = moving average; MA SL = a Probing Bet tranche's MA and its stop-loss; order sheet = the list of sales and adds the rules give for the next Tuesday session.

1. Principles

  • Returns decide; drawdown is a floor. Rules are judged on rolling 12-to-24-month relative returns within acceptable drawdown. Trading costs are irrelevant.
  • Rule-led, with recorded overrules. Every signal goes on the order sheet. Richard may overrule; each overrule is recorded with its reason and tracked against what the rule would have done.
  • Selling is unlinked from buying. The one exception is the one-off in/out link (section 9).
  • The Assistant Portfolio Manager informs; Richard decides capital. Investment and trading decisions are his alone.
  • No trims. The only partial sale is a single tranche: a Core Investment tranche under Sell Criterion 6, or a Probing Bet tranche under its stop-loss rule.

2. Holding types and book shape

  • About 20 stocks: two-thirds to three-quarters Middle Innings Core Investments; a quarter to a third Early Innings Probing Bets; Late Innings Speculative Bets are named but never bought.
  • The type is set at entry: a stock bought from the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection is a Core Investment; any other purchase is a Probing Bet. A Probing Bet that enters the Pool may become a Core Investment. When Richard reclassifies a holding, its new type's rules govern it from that day, and its tranches are read under them from their entry dates.
  • Classification in force (pms-data/holding-status.json): Probing Bets are D'Ieteren, Alfen, IMCD, Hilton Food and Fevertree (Fevertree from 2-Nov-2026); every other holding is a Core Investment.

3. Entry

A Middle Innings Core Investment is entered when: - the stock is in the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection; - a Stock-specific Investment Case A&J Memo exists before tranche 1; - the Market Cycle Phase is Uptrend (if the phase changes: hold under the Sell Criteria, buy no new Core Investments); - Richard decides the capital.

The Pool's tests, read each Friday (a buying gate only; a holding is never sold because it leaves the Pool): - market capitalisation of €500 million or more; - Stock RS L12M Composite of 80 or more; - Sector RS L12M of 50 or more; - SS Sales Net Upgrade Breadth over 100 days of zero or more. A stock with no analyst estimates passes this test (from 2-Nov-2026). - A stock enters after passing on two Fridays in a row (from the weekly run of Friday 6-Nov-2026; 30-Oct can be the first of the two) and leaves after failing on two Fridays in a row. The Pool's size never changes these tests without a discussion with Richard.

An Early Innings Probing Bet: Turning or Inflecting cases; its own Pool and Target Setup Profiles are not yet defined, so entry is Richard's judgement.

4. Sizing and limits

  • Middle Innings Core Investment: each tranche is 1.67% of capital at entry (0.5% of capital lost at a 30% fall); up to about 6.68% of capital per holding (4 tranches).
  • Early Innings Probing Bet: each tranche is sized so that 0.5% of capital is lost at its stop-loss, capped at 3% per tranche; up to 2 tranches, so up to 6% per holding. A tighter stop-loss allows a larger tranche.
  • Promotion: when a Probing Bet becomes a Core Investment and a tranche is above 1.67% of capital at cost, Richard is told to split it into 1.67% tranches plus a smaller remainder (each keeping its original entry date and price), and the Position Management System offers to make the split.
  • Industry cap: no purchase or add may take one industry above 30% of the fund's capital, measured on the capital actually deployed today (not on full tranche allowances), counting every holding.
  • Limits are capital, not a count of tranches, and govern purchases only; they never force a sale.

5. Adds

  • Allowed when no Sell Criterion gives a sell signal (in the Pool or not), the holding is below its limit and inside the industry cap; for a Probing Bet, also while its MA SL is intact (no test running and no confirmed exit).
  • Richard's add triggers (17-Oct-2026): "Add new tranche on 1. Positive retest of rising moving average, or 2. Broadening of case (Momentum increase in either of Guidance, SS, Technicals, Peers)".
  • The Assistant Portfolio Manager is clear and vocal about where each holding stands (Richard, 2-Nov-2026), naming the stage in full: 1. pulling back or basing; 2. may be retesting a rising MA, or forming a volatility contraction pattern (VCP); 3. healthily retesting a rising MA, or breaking out of a VCP (normally after a pullback, but not always); or, said plainly, none of the three (for example, a broken trend).
  • Supporting evidence for an add: a VCP breakout on volume above the 50-day average; an add trigger in Richard's own position notes; a Probing Bet qualifying for the Pool.

6. Exit: Middle Innings Core Investments — the six Sell Criteria (Framework 14)

The first five sell the whole holding: 1. Industry RS below 30 (read each Friday). 2. The relative price's 200D MA flat or falling month on month (read each Friday). 3. Sector RS below 50 and Stock RS below 70 (read at month-end). 4. SS Sales or SS PT Net Upgrade Breadth below zero AND Stock RS below 50 (read at month-end; no analyst data does not trigger it). 5. Relative price more than 25% below its 52W high (read at month-end). 6. Per tranche, read daily: the close is 30% or more below the highest close since that tranche's purchase, and Stock RS is below 70. That tranche alone is released at the first of a 15% rebound from its lowest close since the trigger, or 63 trading days.

Relative price = the SP divided by an equal-weighted index of all European companies of €500 million or more, dividends included. RS is the composite measure everywhere. MA SLs do not apply to a Core Investment.

7. Exit: Early Innings Probing Bets — the 10-day stop-loss rule

Two levels per tranche: the MA Richard chooses (any length from 5 to 250 days) and the stop-loss, the MA less 6% (the default; editable per tranche). Both move with the MA: each day is judged against that day's MA and stop-loss, on the close only. The six Sell Criteria are information only for a Probing Bet. - The test starts on the first close at or below the MA: day 1 of up to 10 trading days. - Exit confirmed on the first close on which any one of these holds: 1. the close is at or below the stop-loss (any day, day 1 included); 2. from day 2, the average volume of the days after day 1, up to and including that day (each day's volume as a multiple of its own 50-day average), reaches 0.75; 3. three consecutive lower closes, all at or below the MA; 4. three consecutive closes at or below the MA, each in the bottom half of its day's range; 5. the 10th consecutive close at or below the MA. - The test ends without an exit on any close back above the MA; the count restarts at the next close at or below it. - A confirmed exit stays a sale until sold or Richard overrules it (recorded); a later close above the MA does not cancel it. Index-rebalance and expiry days are left out of the volume test only. Changing a tranche's MA, or promoting it, is Richard's own ruling on the tranche: it clears a confirmed exit not yet sold and restarts the count that day.

8. Fundamental exits (every holding)

  • The 10 Invalidation ACHs (one-strike rules, escalated at once): listed in memory/skills/assistant-portfolio-manager/SKILL.md, "Live-Case Invalidation Thresholds".
  • The 30-day shot clock: when deterioration appears without a tripped invalidation, 30 days to evidence the case is intact, or exit.

9. Execution

  • Every sale executes at the first Tuesday session after its signal (a signal on a Tuesday sells the following Tuesday): a Friday reading sells the following Tuesday; a month-end reading at the first Tuesday after it; a 30%-fall tranche at the first Tuesday after its release; a confirmed Probing Bet exit at the first Tuesday after it is confirmed, or, if that Tuesday has passed unsold, at the next Tuesday session. The cost of waiting for Tuesday is measured against the next day's close; Richard is told if it exceeds 0.3 points a year.
  • The one-off in/out link ("NOT normal SOP from here onwards"): seven old Core Investments (Avanza, Bally's Intralot, Carlsberg, CVS Group, Helios Towers, Theon, Xvivo) are sold under a whole-holding Sell Criterion only when a replacement is bought, each purchase allowing one such sale, in the order of sale the Assistant Portfolio Manager sets and explains in the APM PMS Q&A SOP note. A 30%-fall tranche on these holdings still sells at the Tuesday session after its release.
