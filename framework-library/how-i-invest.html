How Richard Invests

Philosophy

Solo public equities investor. Long-only, concentrated (target 16 positions), UK/Europe focus. Style: GARP + event-driven/special situations. Buy-side background.

The core belief: system execution matters more than any individual stock idea. The system serves the strategy. The process serves the system. Everything is means to an end.

Target: 25%+ IRR with high predictability on a rolling 18M-3Y basis.

Actual track record: See track-record.md for full V2 data (2015-2025, 138 months, 96 stocks, 8.3% CAGR).

2022-23 Crisis & Recovery: The -40.61% decline in 2022 (followed by -54.75% cumulative through Oct 2023) forced systematic review of process, psychology, and execution discipline. Recovery followed five-phase arc: Foundation (sleep) → Self-image → Framework → Standards → Execution. This rebuild cycle informs all current coaching and process.

Key self-insight: "I genuinely know what I need to do but am not executing it." The constraint is rarely analysis/knowledge — it's execution + interpersonal dynamics. Biggest breakthroughs come from forcing execution despite friction.

The Four Patterns

The best ideas are confluences of at least two, ideally three, of:

  1. Animal CEO — Slootman archetype. Relentless, commercial, ambitious. Holistic/congruent culture. Not defensive — attacking and widening the moat.
  2. Massive corporate change plan — Big restructuring, transformation, or strategic pivot. The most attractive scenario: company coming out of a large investment phase (multi-year margin/EPS depression from doing the right thing, then revenue growth accelerates, margins expand as one-off costs fall away).
  3. Huge demand explosion — Structural theme or demand pulse at sector level. S-curve and co-evolution thinking (ARK/Cathy Wood framework adapted). Caveat: most demand pulses totally reverse to trend — assume 100% cyclical/temporary.
  4. Very large share price decline — Bottom quartile multiples. Market has over-discounted.

The Playbook

Right to Left

Start with the financial output (predictable 18M-3Y EPS). Work backwards: what investment case delivers it? What inputs and transmission mechanism deliver the case?

Disprove, Don't Prove

ACH approach. Don't solve for "yes." Build profiles of "no," "false friend," and "yes." Systematically test evidence against each. If it survives disproval, it might be an investment.

Conservative Analysis, Aggressive Execution

Thorough research with high standards. But once the case is made, act decisively. Don't wait for more information. Don't dither.

Simple + Completed > Complicated + Incomplete

Good plan executed beats great plan in the drawer. Action, dynamism, drive.

Thematic Investing Discipline

There have been a few big, simple, obvious mega trends to ride: AI + datacentres, gold, oil. These are the simple, obvious themes to OWN rather than trying to navigate around them.

This requires many industries' knowledge and watchlists to draw from. Watch all industries with thematic and style agnosticism — no extreme preferences for industries, business models, or value vs growth. Preferences are fine; dogmatisms ("never look at oil") are not.

Creating Knowledge Optionality (Tao Jones concept of Structured Serendipity): build broad knowledge first, then react. Portfolio tilted the right way allows fluid movement into the best setups when they appear.

"Hell Yeah or No"

From historical data: 80% of a durable PM's returns over 50 years came from 20 stocks (diversified fund). Easy to be average at investing. The edge comes from looking through lots of ideas to find outlier setups, then backing outlier CEOs (e.g., IG Group, Flutter, Ryanair).

Decide + move on beats analysis paralysis. Don't over-research mediocre ideas.

Portfolio Management

  • Concentrated: 5-15 positions (target 16 stocks)
  • Universe: ~1,300 European stocks, sweet spot $5-50bn market cap
  • Minimum liquidity: $5m+ daily
  • Minimum company size: 100+ employees
  • Expected ownership: 18 months
  • Enter within 0-6 months of trough/turn
  • Alpha decay apex: ~18 months
  • First 6 months: grace period, must prove winner by month 4-6

Position sizing & exit framework: See investment-strategy.md → "Portfolio Architecture" and "On Deterioration and Exits" for full detail. Summary: 15%/10%/8%/0-5% sizing ladder by HQI category. 80% of max rule. 30-day shot clock on deterioration. Default to 0%, re-argue from zero. Minervini emotional cycle awareness (exit by stage 2-3, not stage 4-5).

The "Love" Portfolio Test

Binary target condition: every week, Richard should be able to say every stock is "loved here and now today" such that he'd be happy if it was 25% of AUM. If not, why not? This eliminates holdings that are "just sitting there."

When a stock is up a lot but fundamentals haven't improved and valuation is stretched, trim the winner. Capital reallocation is simpler than holding stretched positions.

See investment-strategy.md → "Goldilocks Growth 20-30%" for full framework. Sweet spot: 20-30% growth ("10x easier to forecast").

Bear Market Playbook

Two phases: 1. First phase: Own what is fundamentally quality AND perceived to be quality 2. Second phase: Own what is fundamentally high quality BUT perceived to be low quality (oversold)

Howard Marks Lesson (2006-2008)

Marks raised $10bn in commitments pre-crash, deployed $500m/week in 2008. Strategy: buy everything in bear market (all end up cheap, few bankruptcies, strong recoveries). In a big bear market, the dirtiest/scariest list items recover the most.

Critical discipline: deploy massive capital BEFORE fair market entry. Must pre-discuss capital deployment plans with investors.

COVID Lesson

Do NOT exit stocks in market-level sell-offs just because worried about "mediocre quality." This is the baby-and-bathwater fallacy. Hold nerve, be calm, show discipline, ride it out. Can make a lot of money BUYING oversold companies. Be opportunistic, engaged, in COPE (optimist) mode.

Drawdown Expectations

Studies of 20%+ CAGR stocks show: 15% drawdown multiple times per year, 20%+ most years, 50% every decade. Target: controlled "symphonic" burning off. One stock at a time. 1-2 trading slugs live at a time (4-5% each). Recycling through them.

IR Calls SOP

Keep IR calls very financially centric. This helps avoid the "miss risk" — the biggest risk to investment cases (IT/NT/MT).

Requirements: - Available model (minimum: sell-side consensus model) - Preparation to walk through the P&L

This enables the investor to "walk the P&L" with management — understanding the revenue drivers, gross margin mechanics, opex structure, and how investments/restructuring flow through to EPS.

Negative example: Avanza CEO meeting was vague because Richard was vague on the numbers. Positive example: Fevertree IR calls are financial-centric and clear.

Supply Chain Physics

Companies supplying into physically tight supply chains (e.g., Diploma) are attractive. Demand EPSU (embedded supply chain utility) rather than demand transformation. Tight supply chains create sustainable positioning and pricing power.
