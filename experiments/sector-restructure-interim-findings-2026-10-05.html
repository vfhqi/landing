What this page is, and how to read it

  • What it is: the mid-way stop you asked for on 3 October (your words: "before final decisions made by Watson, it stops and presents 'interim findings' re how sectors might change; details on Cohorts and stocks not needed at this stage"). It shows how the Sector layer would change if three things are applied to every Sector: your definition of a Sector (both parts: similar business and revenue models, and similar market-perceived drivers of the two-to-four-year case, within one Industry); your size guide (normally ten stocks or more, very rarely fewer than six); and the criteria in the deliverable plan of 3 October
  • What it is not: nothing is applied anywhere. No Notion page has changed, the Cohort store has not changed, no stock has moved. Company names appear only where they explain a Sector decision
  • What every placement rests on: the business descriptions on your disk (the master-digest file, the tearsheet, the business-foundations summary) for 616 stocks; for the 745 stocks with nothing on disk, the Yahoo Finance company profile fetched this morning, used only to detect, never to decide, the same rule you set for FactSet
  • One technical figure appears throughout, explained here once. The co-movement figure is the average correlation of weekly share-price returns over the last two years between every pair of stocks in a group, or between two groups. It runs from 0 (no relation) to 1 (moving in lockstep). A high figure within a group and a low figure across two groups means the market trades them as different things; similar figures within and across mean the market trades them as one thing. It is shown beside every merge and split so that part (ii) of your definition rests on data as well as on my reading. It never decides on its own: a threshold I invented never decides, it is shown
  • The convention for every Sector name on this page: its code followed by its full name as Notion spells it, for example "A.8. Beverages - spirits". A new Sector's name is my proposal until you change it
  • Still to come after your answers: stage 4, my final decisions and the stock-by-stock map with the Cohorts of every changed Sector and the default target weight letters; stage 5, the one-sitting approval where every new Sector, every move and every letter gets your explicit yes; stage 6, the apply in Notion and the downstream readers, verified before the weekly run on Friday 9 October
