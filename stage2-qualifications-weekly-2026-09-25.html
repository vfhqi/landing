2. How to read the entries

Each stock below sits under a numbered heading in the table's order. The first bullet gives what triggered, with the rungs and the date the data supports, and says the name is new to these reports. Where the screen flags the rating as oscillating, the entry says how many times it fell back and recovered inside the 20-trading-day window. The Stage 2 picture is read from the JSON's stage2_now block only: four gates (price above the 200-day moving average, price above the 150-day, the 150-day above the 200-day, price within 25% of the 52-week high) and five tests (the 50-day above the 150-day, the 50-day above the 200-day, industry relative strength at or above 70, sector relative strength at or above 70, the stock against its own industry at or above 70). The relative read names the cohort first, then sector and industry. Richard's existing view is given as the table and JSON hold it: interest level, long-list tier, timeliness letter; a blank or "Uncategorised" is reported as no rating on file. The last bullets give the conditions under which the transition would be worth the Ideas Analyst's time and the conditions under which it would be noise, and the counter-case; these are conditions, not instructions. Every bullet carries a signpost from [+++] (strongly supportive technical evidence) through [+], [=], [-] to [---] (strongly negative); the signposts grade the technical information only.
