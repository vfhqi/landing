APM Mini-Memo. Demant. The ninth of October 2026.

Demant comes to you as rank eleven on the Ideas Bench in the lists drawn from the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection.

The view is this. I am bullish on the business with medium conviction and neutral on the shares with high conviction. The self-help and the product cycle are landing on every line, but at three hundred and forty-one point two Danish kroner on the ninth of October the price already pays for that, and what is left to earn over eighteen months is about eleven and a half percent a year.

First, what the company does. Demant is a vertically integrated Danish hearing healthcare group with three legs. It sells wholesale hearing aids under the Oticon and Philips brands. It owns more than four and a half thousand retail clinics. And it leads audiological diagnostics. Full-year 2025 revenue was roughly twenty-three billion Danish kroner at a margin of seventeen point two percent before special items, and the United States is just under a third of sales.

Second, the thesis. The mechanism is internal and mechanical. A category-leading custom product takes premium share, a cost programme and the German retail conversion lift the margin, and a falling interest bill turns all of that into earnings per share faster than revenue grows. The Investment Analyst's memo of the eighth of October carries adjusted earnings per share of fourteen point two kroner this year, sixteen point six next year and nineteen point one in 2028. That is about seventeen and a half percent a year, two to five percent above consensus, and he rated Thesis's Change Forces' Strength B on the eighth of October. Four things to track, all of them dated. The share Oticon Zeal takes of United States Veterans Affairs custom units, twenty-three point one percent by July from nineteen point two a year earlier. Wholesale organic growth of eight percent or better with the gross margin held, at the third-quarter statement in early November. The cost programme, three hundred million kroner of savings this year out of five hundred million by 2028. And the KIND German clinics, guided to three hundred and twenty-five million kroner of profit this year.

Third, the Target Setup. The Target Setup is Big Corporate Change-driven earnings per share step-up, and the mapping to your six Case Setups is mine. The memo of the eighth of October names its own pattern Major Corporate Change as primary with no secondary, and rates Setups' Fit slash Crispness B, on five of twelve internal forces strong. The cost of that crispness is maturity: the change has already travelled to full output. The Clearing setup in a high-quality low-cyclicality business on a bottom-quartile multiple fitted a year ago, and it has gone with the oversold condition.

Fourth, Case Riskiness and simplicity. The Investment Analyst's memo of the eighth of October rated Case Riskiness C, on the scale where A is simple and low-risk, built from Riskiness at C and Simplicity at B. The case states in one line and is tracked monthly, but the two factors that decide the next two quarters, the competitor launch window and the multiple, both sit outside the company's control. The research digest puts aggregated failure probability at twenty to thirty percent over the next eighteen to twenty-four months.

Fifth, business quality. A good industry carrying a solid but not great operator, and Foundations' Robustness is B in the same memo. Five manufacturers hold more than eighty-five percent of a wholesale market worth seven point seven billion dollars, with Demant about twenty-one and a half percent of it. The gross margin is seventy-seven point one percent against GN's fifty-four point six, and the route to market is the most diversified of the majors. Against that, return on invested capital was twelve point seven percent in 2025, below the fifteen percent floor and well below Sonova's eighteen, and the operator's record holds four guidance revisions in two years.

Sixth, returns and valuation. This is where the case fails today. In the ratings store on the ninth of October, TSR slash Valuation reads D, set by script, on a rebased eighteen-month return of eleven and a half percent a year at today's price, even though the valuation pin itself is a C. The memo had that rating pinned at B on the seventh of October, when the modal return from three hundred and twenty-one point four was about sixteen percent a year, so the whole of the difference is price and not business. FactSet on the ninth of October shows nineteen point two times earnings on forward earnings per share of seventeen point seven seven kroner, the midpoint of its own history. The upside branch, the ten-year median multiple within eighteen months, pays twenty point nine percent a year at odds of thirty percent. His clearing price was about three hundred and five kroner, some eleven percent below today's price.

Seventh, the value chain and thematics. On the good side, this is consumer-paid, ageing-driven demand in a market growing three percent in units and four percent in value, with the over-the-counter disruption story disproved. Against that sits buyer power, chiefly Costco, which pays two to four hundred dollars a device against three to five hundred for independents. And you should know the standing of the thematic rating. Thematic Fit slash Momentum carries a C in the store on the ninth of October only because I overrode the script's D on the weekly, arguing that the consumables sector's F relative strength describes Xvivo-type consumables and not hearing aids. That override rests on a brainstorm, and the sector's relative strength of twenty-three against the stock's ninety-five is the fact underneath it.

Eighth, the strongest counter-case. At today's price you would be paying about six percent more than the price at which the Investment Analyst said, two days ago, that he would not buy, and paying it two weeks before the event that decides the year. Four of the five major manufacturers launch premium receiver-in-canal platforms into one condensed second-half window, and Bernstein reads Oticon Reveal as a software update on the same chip as Intent, at Market-Perform with a target of two hundred and seventy-six. The forward integration that creates the value provokes the independent audiologists who are fifty-two percent of the wholesale book. And the two reassuring momentum readings, Sell-side Momentum A and Technical Momentum B, are script readings of the ninth of October, not judgements of the case.

The one-line view. Bullish on the business with medium conviction and neutral on the shares with high conviction: the product cycle and the self-help are real, but the return left to earn from here is about eleven and a half percent a year over eighteen months, and the two dated tests are the October Veterans Affairs data and the statement in early November.
