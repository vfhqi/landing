How to read this page

The six components of the Portfolio Fitness for Fighting judgement run in strict dependency order: each one is built from the one above it. Components 1 to 3 are on the note “FfF - Environment and general FfF - APM Weekly AL A&J Note on NTM Likely Environmental Conditions + Optimal Conceptual Portfolio(s)”; components 4 to 6 are on the note “FfF - Our Portfolio's FfF - APM Weekly AL A&J Note on Live Portfolio's Fitness-for-Fighting Assessment + Optimal Portfolio Changes”. If component 1 was thin this week, everything below inherits that thinness, and each section is required to say so rather than sound more confident than its evidence. The arithmetic never has the last word. Every score is a starting point that the judgement may overrule, and every overrule is stated and reasoned.
