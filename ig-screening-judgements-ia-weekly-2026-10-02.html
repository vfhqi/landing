Two caveats that bind every grade. The scale. A, B, C, D, F, no E. A is the 90th percentile and above, B 75th to 89th, C 50th to 74th, D 25th to 49th, F below the 25th. Comparison set: the candidate pool of 177 stocks (10-candidate-pool.md) unless an entry says otherwise. So a snapshot reading maps to a band as follows, and I move it one step only where the week's information justifies it and say so: technical momentum A to grade B, B to C, C to D, D to F; estimate momentum A to B, B to C, C to D, D or F to F; valuation keeps its letter; the three fundamentals keep their letter except D to F. "No change" means the field is blank or I have no view.
