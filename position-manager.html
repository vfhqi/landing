How to use: 16 investment slots × 6 tranche rows (PB1, PB2, Core MM 1–4). Type %AUM as 3 for 3%. Pick a date + stop MA. MA→stop buffer defaults to 2% (= stop is MA × 0.98) and is editable per tranche. Derived columns auto-fill. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to move tranches between slots — drop a PB onto an MM to promote. Cross-investment drag blocked.
Slot caps: PB1/2 = 2.5%, MM1 = 4%, MM2 = 3%, MM3 = 2%, MM4 = 1%. PB stops restricted to 20D/50D; MM stops any of 5 MAs. Hybrid grace clock: 150D/200D = immediate; 20D/50D/100D = 3 daily closes OR 1 weekly close.
Row colours by MA for stop-loss: orange=20D, blue=50D, teal=100D, magenta=150D, slate=200D. Breach states override. Empty slots can have a ticker typed in (must be one of the 9 in chart data). New MM tranches warn if no PB exists for that investment (override permitted for backfill). Connect to disk for auto-save (auto-reconnects on reload).
