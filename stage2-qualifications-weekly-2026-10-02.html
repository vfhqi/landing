3. How to read the stock notes

  • Rungs: the Stage 2 qualification runs None (no rating that day), Possible, Plausible, Probable. The table reports moves up to Plausible or Probable that still stand, and 150-day over 200-day moving average crosses
  • Gates (four): price above the 200-day moving average; price above the 150-day moving average; the 150-day above the 200-day; price within 25% of the 52-week high
  • Tests (five): the 50-day moving average above the 150-day; the 50-day above the 200-day; industry relative strength at or above 70; sector relative strength at or above 70; the stock against its own industry at or above 70. The last three are the relative-strength tests. All gate and test readings are taken from the JSON's stage2_now block as at 1 October and nowhere else
  • Stability uses the JSON's oscillating flag and round_trips_in_window count over the last twenty trading days
  • Richard's file: interest level, long-list tier, timeliness and priority band exactly as the table and JSON give them. The six fundamental ratings are Master, Valuation, Fundamental change, Fundamental robustness, Market paradigm fit and SSEM; blanks are reported as blanks and never filled
  • Signpost markers run from [+++] (strongly positive) through [++], [+], [=], [-] and [--] to [---] (strongly negative) and describe the technical reading only. "Worth the Ideas Analyst's time if" and "noise if" state conditions; the call belongs to the Ideas Analyst and the Assistant Portfolio Manager downstream
