APM Mini-Memo. Saipem. The ninth of October 2026.

Saipem comes to you at rank fifteen on the Ideas Bench in the lists drawn from the Pool of Stocks Eligible for Middle Innings Portfolio Selection, promoted eleven places since last Friday.

The view is neutral on the shares with medium conviction, and bullish on the business. Saipem owns scarce deepwater installation assets inside a tightening oligopoly, in the strongest offshore sanctioning cycle since twenty thirteen, and at four euros fifty-seven the shares already pay for the cycle, for the margin repair and for the merger. What was promoted this week is a technical and thematic signal rather than a case.

First, what the company does. Saipem is the Italian offshore energy services contractor, with about fifteen and a half billion euros of revenue in twenty twenty-five and a backlog of twenty-nine point nine billion euros. It installs subsea pipelines and production systems, drills offshore with its own rigs, and builds onshore liquefied natural gas plants. The three reporting lines are Asset Based Services, at about fifty-eight percent of revenue, Offshore Drilling, and Energy Carriers. Eni owns twenty-one percent, described as non-core, and with CDP Equity a quarter of the company under a pact running to January twenty twenty-eight.

Second, the thesis. The mechanism to earnings is margin and drop-through, not volume. Global offshore sanctioning capital spending is forecast to rise thirty percent, to one hundred and forty-nine billion US dollars in twenty twenty-seven. Twenty high-end rigid pipelay vessels exist in the world, none is being built, and Saipem and Subsea Seven hold eight of them. Because Saipem's fleet is already booked into twenty twenty-eight and twenty twenty-nine, the cycle reaches it as price and mix, with contract rates up three to five percent and day rates up five to seven percent. The Investment Analyst's memo of the fourth of October has earnings per share on the brokers' basis rising from about fifteen cents in twenty twenty-five to about twenty-six cents in twenty twenty-seven and thirty-one cents in twenty twenty-eight, roughly twenty-six percent a year. Almost all of that comes from margin and from below the cash profit line, as depreciation, interest and tax fall with the lease roll-off. Track the Asset Based Services margin against its high-teens target, the recovery of the one hundred and forty million euros of Middle East transit costs, and the guidance in February twenty twenty-seven.

Third, the Target Setup. The Target Setup is Demand-driven EPSU, with Big Corporate Change-driven EPSU alongside it, and those two combine freely. The Investment Analyst's memo of the fourth of October wrote them under its own names, Demand Inflection and Major Corporate Change, and rated Setups' Fit and Crispness C. The mapping into your six Case Setup names is mine. The input half of the pattern is crisp: a sector-wide cycle, a top-three position, and a genuine oligopoly. The output half is missing. Consensus earnings per share for twenty twenty-seven is three percent lower than a year ago, and the current year has been cut by thirty-nine percent. This is also not a trough-on-trough cyclical: the shares are up seventy-nine percent in twelve months and sit under six percent below their high.

Fourth, Case Riskiness and simplicity. This is where the case binds. The Investment Analyst's memo of the fourth of October rated Case Riskiness D, where A is simple and low-risk. A fall from about thirteen times twenty-four-month-forward earnings back to the two-year median of eight times is a thirty-eight to forty-eight percent fall on unchanged earnings. The Thai Oil arbitration claims three point eight billion US dollars, it is unprovisioned, it is larger than the company's equity, and there is no verdict before twenty twenty-eight. Thirty-eight percent of the backlog sits behind the Strait of Hormuz. The thesis is four lines long and trackable monthly, but thirteen brokers cannot agree on next year's earnings within a factor of two.

Fifth, business quality. Advantaged assets, below-average conversion. The same memo rated Foundations' Robustness C. The moats are physical and verifiable, and three to four years are needed to qualify a new supplier. The financial outputs are not there yet. The margin on earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation was eleven point one percent in twenty twenty-five, against Subsea Seven guiding to twenty-four percent and TechnipFMC at twenty-three. Cash is the better story, with free cash flow after leases of nearly eight hundred million euros last year and net cash of about one billion euros.

Sixth, returns and valuation. At four euros fifty-seven the company is worth eight point three billion euros, on a price-to-earnings ratio of twelve point six times. Our two readings disagree, and that disagreement is the decision. The script's TSR and Valuation reading of the ninth of October is B, and it is a valuation reading only; it carries a plausible return of thirty-five percent a year, thirty-eight percent over eighteen months and thirty-two percent over three years, on the multiple moving from twelve point six times towards fifteen point two times. The Investment Analyst's memo of the fourth of October, at four euros thirty-two, put the modal return at minus six percent a year over eighteen months and about nil over three years, on exit multiples of eleven and ten times. The gap between the two is almost entirely the exit multiple you are willing to assume.

Seventh, the value chain and thematics. The thematic read is the strongest thing on the card. Thematic Fit and Momentum in the ratings store on the ninth of October is A, with a strengthening energy-prices tailwind, and the memo of the fourth of October had it at B. The deepwater upcycle and European energy security cover about eighty-five percent of the backlog. Against that, Petrobras is piloting direct charters and has appealed the merger clearance, TechnipFMC takes share with an integrated model Saipem does not have, and the research names the cycle as plateauing after twenty twenty-eight, which is when an owned fleet turns into fixed cost.

Eighth, the strongest counter-case. The lens is wrong. On enterprise value to cash profit Saipem trades at under four times twenty twenty-seven, before leases, against about six times for Subsea Seven and thirteen times for TechnipFMC, because lease depreciation overstates economic depreciation. Consensus is too low, with the drilling recovery and three hundred million euros of merger synergies sitting outside it. And a cleared Saipem Seven would hold sixty to sixty-five percent of global subsea construction with no new supply before twenty twenty-eight, which on Subsea Seven's multiple is worth five euros fifty to six euros.

The one-line view. Neutral on the shares with medium conviction, and bullish on the business: Saipem owns scarce deepwater installation assets in a tightening oligopoly in the strongest sanctioning cycle since twenty thirteen, and at four euros fifty-seven the shares already pay for the cycle, the margin repair and the merger.
